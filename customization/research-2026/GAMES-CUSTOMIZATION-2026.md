## 1. 2025–2026 Releases with Notable Character Creators

### Code Vein II — Bandai Namco (Jan 30, 2026) — BEST-IN-CLASS ANIME CREATOR
The current gold standard for stylized/anime character creation, per official Bandai Namco announcements and community guides.
- **"Advanced Settings" toggle** unlocks decimal-point slider precision (vs. grid-snapped sliders in CV1) — eye spacing, jawline curvature, nose bridge height.
- **~50 accessory slots** governed by a new "Cost" system (trade-off budgeting instead of hard caps).
- **Standalone Character Creator Demo** (Jan 23, 2026) with **64 save slots that transfer into the full game** — pre-launch engagement tool we should copy.
- **Per-part clothing on/off toggles** (mix and match across outfits); **material texture AND pattern** customization per garment.
- Kintsugi-style detailing (crack inlays) as a customization category; nail color; ear angle/sharpness.
- **Makeup layer separated from skin texture** — scars/face paint can be layered *under* foundation, or over it.
- In-game **Mirror** at Home Base for post-creation edits (Gender + Voice permanently locked — deliberate commitment design).
- Built-in **lighting test environments** ("hot spring at MagMell Institute" with different lighting) + Photo Mode to verify looks before committing.
- **Pull/study:** the cost-system accessory budgeting; separated makeup/skin layers; per-part clothing toggles; the 64-slot transferable demo as a pre-launch funnel; lighting-test room.
- Sources: [Bandai Namco EU overview](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/code-vein/news/code-vein-ii-new-combat-gameplay-and-all-new-partner-system-revealed-the-overview), [Bandai Namco news](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/code-vein-ii-overview-trailer-showcases-new-combat-gameplay-and-all-new-partner-system), [Fextralife wiki](https://codevein2.wiki.fextralife.com/Character%20Creation)

### WWE 2K26 — Visual Concepts/2K (Mar 13, 2026; early access Mar 6) — THE REFERENCE FOR OUR SUITE
The single most important research target: the direct evolution of the creation suite our user wants to beat. Major 2K26 upgrades per the Feb 2026 Ringside Report:
- **Body morphing returns** — mix and match body parts for unusual/amusing appearances (separate body parts independently).
- **Decoupled layer pools:** Body = 40 layers; Ring Attire = 60; Entrance Attire 1 = 60; Ring Attire 2 = 60; Entrance Attire 2 = 60. No more sacrificing physique realism for gear detail.
- **200 Create-A-Superstar slots, 2,000 Create-An-Image slots** (doubled from 2K25's 100/1,000).
- **Two-tone hair blending** — two colors with adjustable **blend point and sharpness**.
- **Advanced entrance-motion timeline** for champion/double-champion/MITB/default entrances.
- **Switch 2 mouse controls** for face and body painting; Image Uploader now on all platforms.
- "The Island" mode comes to PC; intergender matches; Ringside Pass replaces DLC.
- **Pull/study:** EVERYTHING — the decoupled layer pools are the model for our attire system; body-part mix-and-match morphing; two-tone hair with blend-point control; the entrance timeline editor (maps directly to our entrance/cutscene tooling).
- Sources: [2K Ringside Report](https://newsroom-uk.2k.com/news/players-creativity-takes-centre-stage-as-wwer-2k26-universe-and-community-creations-details-revealed-in-ringside-report-narrated-by-bayley), [Operation Sports deep-dive](https://www.operationsports.com/wwe-2k26-creation-suite-upgrades-add-body-morphing-and-60-layer-attires/), [VGC reveal](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/wwe-2k26-release-date-editions-new-match-types-ringside-pass-system-showcase-mode-and-pre-order-bonus-revealed/)

### Dune: Awakening — Funcom (PC Jun 10, 2025; PS5/Xbox Sep 22, 2026) — STRONG REALISTIC CREATOR
- **16 culture-based body presets**; height, limb thickness, neck length, chest size, muscle tone sliders.
- **Vitiligo patterns, freckles, skin blemishes, redness, dark circles** as first-class skin-detail categories.
- 35+ hairstyles, 45 hair colors, 22 beard styles; tattoos, scars, makeup all present.
- **Background triad (Homeworld + Caste + Mentor)** affects dialogue options and starting abilities — identity creation feeds gameplay.
- **Standalone Creator & Benchmark tool** released before launch; characters saved as **portable `.json` files** importable into the full game — an open, shareable character-data format worth imitating.
- Appearance is **permanent after creation** (no salon) — deliberate identity-commitment design, same philosophy as Avowed.
- **Pull/study:** vitiligo/skin-condition categories; `.json` character portability (fits our share-code/transfer goals); culture-preset body foundations.
- Sources: [LagoFast creator guide](https://www.LagoFast.com/en/blog/dune-awakening-character-creation/), [MonsterVine](https://monstervine.com/2025/02/dune-awakening-release-date-character-creator/), [dtgre appearance guide](https://www.dtgre.com/2025/06/dune-awakening-change-character-appearance-after-creation-guide.html)

### Street Fighter 6 — Capcom (updates through 2026) — FIGHTING-GAME AVATAR MODEL
- **Custom avatar + moveset mixing:** learn masters' fighting styles in World Tour and **combine special moves from different fighters** into a personal kit — the closest any fighter comes to our "moveset library" concept.
- Gear items carry **stat effects** (fashion = build, like FFXIV glamour with stats).
- **Body Shop** allows full appearance overhauls anytime with in-game currency (anti-permanence philosophy, opposite of Dune/Avowed).
- **May 28, 2026 update:** Random Avatar Matches + Avatar Arcade (fight online as your custom avatar; earn DLC fighters' moves without buying them) — custom avatars promoted from side mode to core competitive content.
- Switch 2 launch (Jun 5, 2025) added Local Avatar Match.
- **Pull/study:** the avatar-moveset-mixing paradigm (validates our per-fighter custom moveset assembly); avatar-as-competitive-character promotion; Body Shop re-edit economy.
- Sources: [chainplay avatar update](https://chainplay.gg/blog/street-fighter-6-random-avatar-matches-avatar-arcade-mode/), [pixelarushx creator features](https://pixelarushx.com/news/street-fighter-6-character-creator-features-and-customization-options)

### TEKKEN 8 — Bandai Namco (2025 updates) — SHARED-SLOT ARCHITECTURE
- **Patch 1.11 (Feb 2025):** new **"Shared" customize tab** — **20 slots usable by ALL characters**; "Add to Favorites" (prioritized at select screen); **"Replace"** slot reordering.
- Customized characters can now be displayed **in the main menu** (showcase your designs on boot).
- Tekken Shop cadence: Gold Suit Pack, Flamboyant Pack, Sublime Pack, Maid Pack, Seaside/Beach Fun swimsuit packs, Karate Kid: Legends tie-in — cosmetic microtransaction pack pipeline worth studying for our shop design.
- **Pull/study:** shared-slot concept (one design applied across characters = our "attire templates"); favorites/replace UX for managing large slot libraries.
- Sources: [PSU patch 1.11](https://www.psu.com/news/tekken-8-update-1-11-now-available-with-major-balance-changes-to-clive-shared-customisation-slots-added/), [Bandai Namco 2.04 notes](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/tekken-8-patch-notes-v2-04), [comicbook 2025 teaser](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/tekken-8-updates-2025-menu-customization/)

### Avowed — Obsidian (Feb 18, 2025; Anniversary Update Feb 17, 2026)
- Backgrounds that **reshape world perception** (unique dialogue, NPC reactions) — the "Biography Forge" idea.
- Godlike features (glowing eyes, organic growths like mushroom caps/tree branches).
- **Anniversary Update:** 3 new playable races (Orlan, Dwarf, Aumaua), new Godlike options — creators expanded post-launch.
- Appearance **permanent**; compensated with a full **transmog system** for armor.
- **Pull/study:** background-as-identity mechanic; transmog as the answer to locked appearance.
- Sources: [Game8 options](https://game8.co/games/avowed/archives/495817), [altchar anniversary](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/avowed-is-getting-new-playable-races-and-its-finally-coming-to-ps5-aOtH21x2UUL4)

### Crimson Desert — Pearl Abyss (Mar 19, 2026) — THE NEGATIVE CASE
- **No character creator at all** — fixed protagonists (Kliff, Damiane, Oongka), despite Pearl Abyss making BDO's famous editor. Customization limited to barbershop (hair/tattoos) + dyehouse (armor/weapon/mount dyes).
- The modding community is **building its own creator mods** (NexusMods "Character Creator" with 98 beard presets / 159 hairstyles).
- Lesson for us: even in 2026, players demand creation tools; skipping them creates immediate mod demand.
- Sources: [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/crimson-desert-wont-let-you-build-a-character-heres-what-you-get-instead/), [thegameswiki](https://thegameswiki.com/crimson-desert/wiki/does-crimson-desert-have-character-creation)

## 2. Missed Deep Systems (not previously researched)

### Soulcalibur VI — still the fighting-game creation benchmark
- **Creation mode:** pick a fighting style, then full part-by-part outfit assembly; **8 sticker/decal slots** placeable on armor AND body (up from 6); per-part colors/textures; multiple races.
- Custom characters playable **online**; shareable with the community.
- Weakness noted by reviewers: **faces are preset-based, not morphable** (unlike body) — exactly the gap our morphing suite should exploit.
- **Pull/study:** sticker/decal system on body+armor; style-pick-then-dress structure; the face-morphing gap is our opening.
- Sources: [RocketChainsaw review](https://www.rocketchainsaw.com.au/review/soulcalibur-vi-review-the-soul-still-burns/), [Shacknews review](https://www.shacknews.com/article/107995/soulcalibur-6-review-the-soul-burns-bright)

### Nioh 2 — Team Ninja — YOKAI-FORM CUSTOMIZATION
- Deep creator (hair curl/length/shine, facial hair with tip color/shine, aging wrinkles, full makeup: eyeliner/eyeshadow/lipstick with type+color+shine).
- **Separate customization of the Yokai (demon) transformation form** — the "second self" gets its own editor. Directly relevant to our Oversoul/transformation characters (Zen, Chatur).
- **Shareable character codes** — community exchange system.
- Re-editable in-game via the Hut; save/load multiple characters.
- **Pull/study:** dual-form editing (human + spirit/demon form) — maps 1:1 to our transformation/Oversoul designs.
- Sources: [Fextralife Character Creation](https://nioh2.wiki.fextralife.com/Character+Creation), [PlayStation editorial](https://www.playstation.com/content/global_pdc/emea/locales/nz/en-nz/editorial/8-new-things-you-will-be-doing-in-nioh-2.html)

### Phantasy Star Online 2: New Genesis — SEGA — FASHION AS ENDGAME
- **Layered wear:** base wear + inner wear + outer wear + body paint + accessories, nearly all mix-and-match — "feels closer to genuine fashion design than gear selection" (ComicBook, 2026).
- **Accessories can be resized, rotated, repositioned, and stacked** — sculpting, not dressing.
- Granular body morphing (height, limb length, torso width, muscle, posture, facial structure).
- Free **Salon** re-editing (cosmetics free; physiology changes cost passes).
- Active through **Ver.2 updates in 2026** (monthly AC Scratch fashion drops: body paints, nail chips, eye designs, motion alterations).
- **Pull/study:** the accessory transform gizmo (resize/rotate/reposition) — the single most "creation suite" feature here; layered wear categories as our attire-slot taxonomy.
- Sources: [ComicBook feature](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/no-other-game-comes-close-to-this-level-of-character-customization/), [Third News Ver.2](https://third-news.com/article/3c79025a-122a-11f1-ba77-9ca3ba0a67df)

### Warframe — Digital Extremes — "FASHIONFRAME"
- Community-coined **"FashionFrame"**: fashion is the true endgame; dojo fashion shows, relay showcases.
- **Voidshell skins** — change the *material/texture* of skins (not just colors).
- Operator/Drifter: voice and body shape selectable **independently** of any gender setting.
- **TennoGen** — community-created cosmetics sold in-game with revenue share (the UGC monetization model for our Community Creations).
- Jan 2026 devstream added more distinct face options for Operator/Drifter.
- **Pull/study:** Voidshell material-swapping; TennoGen as the template for a community-creation marketplace; gender-independent voice/body.
- Sources: [Digital Extremes devstream](https://www.digitalextremes.com/news/january-warframe-devstream-highlights), [MassivelyOP FashionFrame](https://massivelyop.com/2023/07/21/warframe-of-mind-fashion-framing-with-digital-extremes-megan-everett/)

### Final Fantasy XIV: Dawntrail — Square Enix — GLAMOUR SCIENCE
- **Two dye channels per gear piece** (7.0) — dye different regions of one item independently.
- **Headgear + facewear simultaneously** (glasses no longer compete with hats).
- **Patch 7.4 (Dec 2025): glamour restrictions by class/job/level REMOVED** — wear anything on anything.
- Full graphical overhaul of character materials (7.0).
- **Pull/study:** two-dye channels per garment (our clothing dye system should have ≥2 channels); slot-consolidation (facewear + headwear stacking).
- Sources: [Dawntrail official systems](https://eu.finalfantasyxiv.com/dawntrail/system), [DualShockers 7.4](https://www.dualshockers.com/final-fantasy-xiv-removing-glamour-job-restrictions/), [Destructoid dye guide](https://www.destructoid.com/?p=537874)

## 3. Clothing / Fashion Systems — Biggest Libraries

| Game | Library model | Why it matters for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Roblox UGC marketplace** | Millions of user-made avatar items; **Dress to Impress** (weekly themed fashion drops, still updated 2026) | The largest clothing library in gaming is *community-built*. Our Community Creations needs this economy. |
| **WWE 2K26 Community Creations** | 2,000 image slots + Image Uploader (upload your own textures/logos) + cross-platform sharing (Switch 2 included) | Upload-your-own-image is the cheat code for infinite clothing/tattoo content. Copy this. |
| **PSO2 NGS AC Scratch** | Monthly fashion drops: layered wear sets, body paints, nail chips, eyes, motion alterations; player trading economy | Monthly fashion cadence as live-service engagement; layered-slot taxonomy. |
| **FFXIV Glamour** | Thousands of gear pieces, 2-dye channels, glamour dresser/plates (saved outfit presets) | **Glamour plates = saved outfit presets** — our attire ladders need named, swappable preset slots. |
| **ZEPETO** | Creator marketplace (ZEPETO Studio with official armature/rig docs); brand collabs (e.l.f. makeup, 2026) | Licensed-brand cosmetic drops; documented creator pipeline lowers UGC barrier. |
| **Code Vein II wardrobe** | Per-part clothing on/off + material/pattern editing per garment | Garment-level material editing beats item-swapping for uniqueness. |

**Key takeaway:** the winners all do some version of (a) player-uploaded content, (b) saved outfit presets/plates, (c) multi-channel dyeing. Our suite needs all three.

## 4. Tattoo / Face Paint / Makeup — Best in 2026

1. **WWE 2K26** — body paint with **mouse-precision painting** (Switch 2 Joy-Con mouse); Image Uploader tattoos; two-tone hair with blend control. Best *body-coverage* tattoo system in a sports/fighting title.
2. **Code Vein II** — dedicated **Paint tab** (face paint), kintsugi crack detailing, makeup layered independently of skin/scars; nail color.
3. **Nioh 2** — best *cosmetic-detail* makeup: eyeliner/eyeshadow/eyelash/lipstick each with type + color + shine + length sliders.
4. **Dune: Awakening** — tattoos + scars + makeup + **vitiligo patterns** (skin-condition representation no one else does).
5. **ZEPETO (mobile)** — **e.l.f. cosmetics partnership (2026)**: real-world beauty brands styling virtual makeup; makeup down to eyeliner level is core to the avatar economy.
6. **FiveM mod scene (GTA RP)** — **3D tattoo creator**: paint tattoos directly onto the 3D model in a live preview, export as overlay layers. This is the *interaction model* to copy — painting on the model, not picking from a list.
7. **Covet Fashion (mobile)** — real-brand makeup looks as unlockables; educational color-harmony angle.

**Pull/study:** 3D paint-on-model tattoo interaction (FiveM pattern); makeup categories split by type+color+finish (Nioh 2); real-brand cosmetic collaborations (ZEPETO/e.l.f.); vitiligo/skin-condition options (Dune).

## 5. Mobile / Gacha — Surprisingly Deep?

Honest finding: **no mobile game matches PC/console creator depth.** The closest:

- **ZEPETO** — selfie-to-avatar generation + full manual sculpt (eye/nose/lip/face shape, makeup, fashion); **ZEPETO Studio** lets creators rig and sell fashion on the official armature (documented pipeline); brand collabs (e.l.f. makeup, 2026); DRESSX's **Avatar Fashion Converter** auto-converts one master garment to Bitmoji/Roblox/ZEPETO formats — a **cross-platform garment pipeline** worth studying for our own multi-format intake.
- **Roblox (Dress to Impress + UGC)** — the fashion-game phenomenon; weekly theme drops; JSON-defined avatar specs; premium UGC marketplace.
- **Gacha dress-up apps** (Character Maker: 12M installs, AvatarDo, LoveToon) — large item counts (500–1000+ pieces) but **paper-doll depth only**: no morphing, no 3D. Not worth deep study; useful only as a reference for *item-catalog UX* at scale.

**Verdict for our suite:** study ZEPETO Studio's creator documentation model and DRESSX's cross-platform garment converter; skip the dress-up apps beyond catalog-UX inspiration.
