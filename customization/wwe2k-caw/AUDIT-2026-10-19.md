### Why the commercial files were NOT pulled

The PULL-PLAN targets the commercial game files (WWE 2K19 ~51GB via
Archive.org, WWE 2K18 ~45GB). Those are commercial software. Per standing
law they are **documented but not pulled**: no cracking, no paywall bypass,
no redistribution of commercial game data. This is a hard boundary, not a
deferral.

### Honest gaps (still missing)

- **Masks**: no dedicated mask parts yet. Rogue hood not separable (fused
  mesh). Lucha masks are the obvious next pull.
- **Tattoos**: decal-library/ has attire decals but no tattoo designs yet.
  Needs a CC0 tattoo-flash curation pass.
- **Belts / gloves / boots as separates**: WMXIX has some; modern separates
  needed.
- **Facial hair variety**: only Quaternius beard. More styles needed.
- **Realistic hair**: current set is stylized/low-poly. Realistic CC0 hair
  is scarce; David Grette's 2026 pack is commercial (documented, not pulled).
- **Morph sliders data**: body-morphs/ has MPFB tooling; MakeHuman target
  library not yet pulled.
- **Quaternius Modular Outfits** (12 outfits, 62 modular parts, CC0, FREE):
  blocked on itch.io download flow — needs a browser lane. Highest-value
  next pull.
- **KayKit paid character packs** ($19.99/ea, CC0 but paywalled):
  documented, not pulled — no paywall bypass.
