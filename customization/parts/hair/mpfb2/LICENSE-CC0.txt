3. Facial hair (wh-beard-*): original Western Heart work — shells cut from the

   Released CC0-1.0.

No MB-Lab (AGPL) data is copied into these files: the template bodies are read
only for fitting (head joint matrix, skin surface, slider deltas resampled onto
the part). The resampled morph deltas are derived from the AGPL template bake —
flagged here so the licence review of the template bodies (customization/templates/README.md)
covers them too.
