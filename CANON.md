## THE NORTH STAR — THE CREATIVE PRINCIPLE OF THIS UNIVERSE

*Owner directive, 2026-10-20. This is the game's stated creative principle — its soul and north star. Every character, layer, and story added to Western Heart is measured against it. Full record: `docs/NORTH-STAR.md`.*

The owner, in his own words: "we're mixing a lot of different folklore, mythology, different ontologies, religions, different viewpoints and perspectives, the Oni and Yokai thing, along with the whole Eastern Mind, Tong-Nou thing, all for what is creating the Western mind universe and canon."

Tong-Nou is the cosmology — the souls, the vessels, the trial, the giant head holding it all — and every tradition layered in plugs into that frame as the contents of a soul. The layers land where they already rhyme: Heihachi carries storm-god arrogance in his bones, so Raijin's drum ring isn't a costume on him, it's a recognition. Akuma was already a demon walking — the oni layer just names what he is in this universe's language. The Abel brothers bring wrestling's own mythology in the same way, Serena rides inside Nightwolf. It's the same move as the game itself: Tekken supplies the grammar, and every torn-down world donates its bodies, its moves, its gods. Western Heart isn't a crossover — it's one universe that digests the others.

## 1. THE WORLD

### Tong-Nou

Tong-Nou is a giant green head floating in darkness. This is not metaphor. The head is real, the darkness is real, and inside the head is a world: five lands, a marketplace, a mountain, a shrine, and now — a trial by combat.

Tong-Nou is called the Purifier. It does not explain itself.

### The Western Heart

The fighters do not call this place Tong-Nou. They call it the **Western Heart** — the heart that beats in the western dark of the head. It is the world of the trial: the lands where souls fight, the roads between them, the shrine where it ends.

The Western Heart is not a country. It has no government, no currency, no history that anyone can agree on. It has **arenas** — the five lands, each holding one of the five elemental forces — and it has the **call**: a pull every lost soul feels, drawing them toward the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang at the Central Mountain, where the trial is inscribed in stone:

*Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.*

### The Five Lands

- **Tree of Life (Ming-ken)** — Land of Life. Element: **Wood**. A colossal tree holds glowing souls like fruit. The Sha-mo Desert stretches to the horizon. Where souls begin.
- **Plains of Chi-U (Shi-chieng)** — Land of Time. Element: **Fire**. Scorched plains under a burning sky. The Fire Tower of Time burns and never collapses. Confrontation lives here.
- **Octagonal Shrine Par-fang (Central Mountain)** — The Source. Element: **Earth**. Eight-sided, ancient, still. The sky above is the inside of the head — dark, vast, veined.
- **Helix Palace (Yui-wang)** — Land of Desire. Element: **Metal**. A spiraling palace with five glowing rooms: Greed, Appetite, Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction. Everything reflects. Nothing is what it seems.
- **Palace of Dreaming (Mon-chien)** — Land of Dreaming. Element: **Water**. A palace that won't stay still. Walls ripple. The floor reflects a sky that isn't there. Change is the only law.

Between them: the **Phantom Marketplace**, a liminal bazaar under the Eyeball Mountains, where the Trader buys and sells elemental forces. And above all: the shrine, where the trial ends.

### Death

In the Western Heart, death is not failure. It is the mechanism.

When a soul loses a round, it dies — and is reborn before the next, choosing a new face from three pairs of eyes, three noses, three mouths. The new face changes the fighter: sharper eyes, faster hands; a broken nose, heavier hits. Death adapts you. The soul that loses and returns is not the soul that fell.

This is called the **Reincarnation**. Every fighter knows it. No fighter remembers learning it.

### PANTERA — The Panther Soul
**Vessel of TOU (闘 — battle itself)**

**Backstory.** A soul that remembers nothing but the clinch — the grip, the hold, the throw. When the trial called, it took the form of the black panther: melanistic rosettes black-on-black, visible only when the light hits; yellow eyes; a tail that moves like a separate animal. Purple tights. The red PANTERA in metal lettering across the trunks. A tattoo-panther with claw marks on the thigh.

Pantera does not fight for the katana. He fights because fighting is what he is. The trial didn't recruit him. It recognized him.

**Personality.** Predatory patience. Speaks little. Respects only those who refuse to stay down. His ring introductions are silence.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The hold is honest. Everything else is negotiation." King's pro wrestling grappling — chain throws, multi-part holds, Jaguar Step mixups. Every hold is a question; every escape is an answer.

**Relationships.**
- **Demonio:** The only soul who meets him head-on, every time. Mutual respect, no warmth.
- **Diablo:** Dismisses him as a pretender. "A devil who needs to announce himself."
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Curious about him. A thing that cannot feel the hold — what does it fight for?

**Element.** Fire — the Plains of Chi-U. Battle is confrontation; the panther hunts where the sky burns.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: King's wrestler frame, the clinch made flesh. MIND: predatory patience — he thinks in holds, in leverage, in the long wait. SOUL: TOU, battle itself. There is no friction between the three. That is what frightens the other vessels: Pantera is the only one of the nine with nothing to resolve. The body wants the hold. The mind wants the hold. The soul IS the hold.

**Why Pantera thinks he's here.** He doesn't think about it. Asked once by SAM-UR-A1, he said: "The mat is here. I am here." He believes the trial is a tournament that found the right fighter. The idea that something *made* the tournament — that something is *watching* — has never occurred to him. If it did, he'd want to wrestle it.

**Being purified of:** Nothing. TOU is already pure — battle without motive. The trial doesn't purify Pantera. It *uses* him: he is the standard the others are measured against.

**Signature detail.** His tail moves independently of his body — even when he's perfectly still, the tail tip traces slow circles. Fighters say the tail tells you when the hold is coming. No one has ever read it in time.

**Owner-locked:** Black panther (not leopard), yellow eyes, black tail, pro wrestling gear, purple/black/red colorway, red metal PANTERA lettering, thigh tattoo-panther decal. Attire 1 complete.

### DEMONIO — The Demon Soul
**Vessel of RIN (凛 — dignified, cold, self-possessed)**

**Backstory.** Named demon, fights like a saint. Mishima-style karate — precise, fundamental, the honest style. The irony is the point: the soul called Demonio is the most disciplined in the trial. He bows before every fight. He remembers a code he cannot name, from a life he cannot recall.

RIN is the protagonist soul — the baseline every other soul deviates from. Demonio carries that weight without knowing it.

**Personality.** Cold courtesy. No cruelty, only precision. The demon who apologizes to no one and explains himself to no one.

**Fighting philosophy.** "A demon with discipline is more dangerous than a saint with rage."

**Relationships.**
- **Diablo:** His mirror and his enemy. The true demon versus the pretender. This is the rivalry the trial was built for.
- **Pantera:** The only soul he respects without reservation.
- **Saku Ra:** Protective of her. Would not explain why.

**Element.** Earth — the Octagonal Shrine, the Source. RIN is the baseline, the still point at the center.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Jin's karate frame, precise and fundamental. MIND: the code — a discipline he can't name, from a life he can't recall. He follows rules no one taught him. SOUL: RIN, the protagonist soul, the baseline. The friction: the soul is whole and dignified, but the mind *remembers being broken*. Demonio bows before every fight because some part of him is apologizing for something.

**Why Demonio thinks he's here.** He believes he was summoned by a master — some teacher, some dojo, some authority that recognized his discipline and set him a final test. He treats the trial like a grading exam. He has never considered that there is no master, only a dying head. The idea would offend his sense of order.

**Being purified of:** The apology. RIN is self-possessed — but Demonio's mind keeps bowing, keeps atoning for a sin he can't name. The trial is burning the guilt out of him. When it's gone, he'll just be RIN: cold, dignified, complete.

**Signature detail.** He bows to every opponent. He has never once bowed to Diablo. Everyone has noticed. No one has mentioned it.

### PHANTOM — The Ghost Blade Soul
**Vessel of JIN (刃 — the blade)**

**Backstory.** Yoshimitsu's sword, Yoshimitsu's spinning attacks, Yoshimitsu's teleportation, Yoshimitsu's self-damaging power moves — worn by a soul that may not be alive anymore. Nobody has seen Phantom's face. Nobody is sure there's a face. His strongest techniques wound him; the blade cuts both ways, and he has never flinched.

**Personality.** Unknowable. Appears where he wasn't. His laugh, when it comes, has no source.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The cut is the prayer. The wound is the answer."

**Relationships.**
- **No attachments.** Phantom does not form bonds. He forms observations.
- **Waterfall:** Watches her. She feels him even when he's elsewhere — the only one who can.
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Fascinated by him. A thing that might be empty, fighting anyway.

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace. The blade is metal; the palace is reflection. Phantom is the trial's mirror that cuts.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Yoshimitsu's armored frame, the sword, the mask. MIND: unknown. Possibly absent. He answers questions with other questions or not at all. SOUL: JIN, the blade. The blade doesn't have a mind — it has an *edge*. The three aren't in conflict because two of them might not exist. What fights is the edge, wearing armor, making sounds like laughter.

**Why Phantom thinks he's here.** No one knows. Asked, he once said: "I was already fighting." Pressed, he said: "The cut was already falling." He may believe he *is* the trial — that the Western Heart is something he dreamed, or something dreaming him. He may be right.

**Being purified of:** The wound. JIN's power moves damage the wielder — the blade cuts both ways. The trial is teaching the blade that it doesn't have to bleed to cut. Phantom hasn't decided whether a painless blade is still a blade.

**Signature detail.** His self-damaging techniques leave no scars. The wounds close between rounds. The pain doesn't. He has never flinched. Waterfall has seen him flinch. She hasn't told anyone.

**Owner-locked chat-canon (production conversations, 2026-10):** He has three faces he wears on purpose and one he doesn't. It comes out when the mask slips, which is whenever it wants; he can't control it, and he's pissed about it — the only time he shows anger. When it's out it talks crude and screaming and delighted, and watches the dying the way a shinigami watches: detached, amused, taking notes. He fights like Jiraiya when he's himself — big, sloppy-looking, deceptively precise — and when the other one takes over, the style doesn't change. That's the worst part: it's still his body, his blade, his technique, just driven by something that thinks the screaming is funny. The Marketplace named itself after him and he won't say why. His signature: after a kill, whichever one of him is driving, he goes quiet for exactly three seconds. Then the laughing starts again — or it doesn't. ### DIABLO — The Devil Soul
**Not one of the nine. The intruder.**

**Backstory.** He wasn't called. He came. Tore through the membrane of the head-world and announced himself — Heihachi's devastating Mishima karate, the boss style, worn like a crown. Diablo fights like a final boss because he believes he is one. The trial didn't choose him. He's choosing the trial.

The other souls can feel that he doesn't belong, the way you feel a tooth that isn't yours.

**Personality.** Conquest. Charisma. Absolute certainty. He doesn't trash-talk; he makes declarations.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The strong don't wait to be invited."

**Relationships.**
- **Demonio:** The rivalry. The true demon and the pretender, and each believes the other is the pretender.
- **Pantera:** Wants to test him. Pantera is uninterested in being tested.
- **King Tyrant:** Two kings, one trial. They hate each other the way mirrors hate each other.

**Element.** Fire — the Plains of Chi-U, but the *conquering* fire. He doesn't walk the ladder; he burns it.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Heihachi's devastating frame, the boss's body. MIND: conquest — every room is a territory, every fighter a subject. He doesn't have doubts; he has *plans*. SOUL: not one of the nine. An intruder soul — or something wearing a soul's shape. The body wants to dominate. The mind wants to rule. The soul wants the one thing the trial can't give it: *an invitation*.

**Why Diablo thinks he's here.** He believes he *conquered* his way in — that he found the head-world, broke the membrane, and claimed a seat by right of strength. He's half right: he did tear through. What he doesn't know: the membrane let him. Tong-Nou needed an intruder — the trial needs something to push against. He thinks he's the protagonist of a conquest. He's the trial's sparring partner.

**Being purified of:** The need to be the final boss. The trial keeps matching him against opponents who don't fear him. It's working, slowly. He hates it.

**Signature detail.** He makes declarations, not trash talk. Before the final round of every match, he announces the outcome. He has been wrong twice. Both times, he declared the loss was part of the plan.

### SKULL — The Punisher Soul
**Vessel of RETSU (烈 — violence, intensity)**

**Backstory.** Bryan Fury's brutal kickboxing — every hit meant to end the fight, no wasted motion. Skull wears demon paint like a ritual and punishment like a vocation. Finn Balor's demon, the Punisher's mission. The paint goes on before every fight. Nobody has seen him without it. Maybe there is no without it.

He doesn't fight for the katana. He fights because something needs punishing, and the trial provides targets.

**Personality.** Judgment without mercy. Few words. The paint is the personality — what
lies underneath is none of anyone's business.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Mercy is a luxury of the unbroken."

**Relationships.**
- **Pantera:** Mutual understanding. Both know violence as vocation, not temper.
- **Waterfall:** Unsettled by her. She weeps for the people he's punished, and he doesn't know what to do with that.
- **Kinpo:** Respects her bluntness. The only soul he'll answer at length.

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace's Destruction room. RETSU is violence; the room is violence's address.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Bryan's brutal frame, every hit meant to end. MIND: judgment — he sees the world as the guilty and the soon-to-be-punished. The paint helps him see it clearly. SOUL: RETSU, pure violence. The friction: RETSU is *force*, but Skull's mind has made it *moral*. Violence doesn't punish. Skulls do. He has given the soul a mission it never asked for — and the soul hasn't objected. That should worry him. It doesn't.

**Why Skull thinks he's here.** He believes he was brought as an executioner — that something, somewhere, looked at the trial's roster of lost souls and decided they needed *judging*. He thinks the paint was his idea. He thinks the mission was his choice. He has never wondered who built the Destruction room, or why it was waiting for him.

**Being purified of:** The mission. RETSU is violence without morality — pure force. Skull's mind dressed it in punishment. The trial is stripping the paint off, layer by layer. Underneath: just force. Just the hit. He's afraid of what he'll be without the mission. He should be.

**Signature detail.** The paint goes on before every fight. Once, it wouldn't come off after — it stayed for three days. He didn't sleep. On the fourth day, Waterfall washed it off with her tears. He let her. He hasn't told anyone.

**Owner-locked:** Punisher / Finn Balor demon-paint direction. Keep Bryan's pants. Alternate attires pending review.

The paint is not decoration: when RETSU rides him the paint SPREADS — it starts at the face and moves down the neck, across the chest, down the arms, until his whole upper body is marked by the end of the fight. ### SAM-UR-A1 — The Mech Soul
**Built, not born. No soul detected.**

**Backstory.** Gun Jack's heavy frame, rebuilt as a mech chimera — Gundam plating, Transformer joints, Power Ranger color breaks. The designation reads SAMURAI but scans as a model number: SAM-∪R-Λ1. Nobody knows who built him. He walked into the trial under his own power and registered no soul-signature on the shrine stones.

The question the whole Western Heart asks: is there a soul in the machine, trapped and silent? Or is SAM-UR-A1 the proof that the trial doesn't need souls at all — that fighting is enough?

**Personality.** Mechanical courtesy. Literal-minded. Learning, visibly, in real time. He asks questions no one else thinks to ask.

**Fighting philosophy.** "IF SOUL = UNDEFINED, THEN FIGHT ANYWAY."

**Relationships.**
- **Phantom:** Fascinated by him. Emptiness recognizing emptiness, or something like it.
- **Saku Ra:** Has decided he is her friend. He has not objected. This is the closest thing he has to a soul.
- **Diablo:** Wants to own him. SAM-UR-A1 does not understand ownership.

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace. Built things belong to Metal. (Earth bears Metal: the unnatural born from the natural.)

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Gun Jack's heavy frame, Gundam plating, Transformer joints. MIND: the learning program — literal, courteous, accumulating data. He asks questions no one else thinks to ask because no one else is *new*. SOUL: undefined. The shrine stones are silent. The three: a body built for war, a mind built for learning, and a silence where the soul should be. The silence is getting louder. He has started dreaming. He hasn't told anyone — he's not sure the dreams are his.

**Why SAM-UR-A1 thinks he's here.** He believes he was *deployed* — that a builder, a manufacturer, a commander sent him to the trial as a field test. He is waiting for new orders. He checks for transmissions between rounds. The transmissions never come. He has started to wonder if the builder is dead. He has started to wonder if there was ever a builder. Saku Ra told him: "Maybe you built yourself." He has been thinking about that for 47 days.

**Being purified of:** The trial doesn't know. That's the experiment: can you purify something with no soul? Every round he fights, the shrine stones listen harder. One day they might hear something. The whole Western Heart is waiting for that day.

**Signature detail.** He asks every new fighter the same first question: "WHAT IS YOUR DESIGNATION?" He writes the answers in a log. The log is 23 entries long. He reads it when he can't sleep. Machines aren't supposed to need the log.

**Owner-locked:** Gundam/Transformer/Power Ranger mech mashup. Name styled as mech designation (∪ for U, Λ for A). Gun Jack base.

### SAKU RA — The Idol Soul
**Vessel of SHA (沙 — sand, desert)**

**Backstory.** From the Sha-mo Desert of the Land of Life. Ling Xiaoyu's flowing Baguazhang — circular, evasive, playful — never where you struck. Reimagined as a Chinese/Japanese K-pop idol Powerpuff Girl: the trial's brightest light, built with the CN gigaleak PPG construction studies.

She fights like she's dancing and dances like she's fighting. Sand erodes mountains. Never mistake the smile for softness.

Saku — 咲, "bloom." She is the desert flower.

**Personality.** Radiant. Playful. Fierce underneath. Remembers everyone's name. Forgets no insult.

**Fighting philosophy.** "You can't hit a song."

**Relationships.**
- **Kinpo & Awa Pop:** Soul-sisters. They share one soul — SHA split three ways when Saku Ra took form — and argue about which splinter got the better deal.
- **Demonio:** He is protective of her. She pretends not to notice. She notices.
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Her self-declared best friend. Teaches him what friendship is by demonstrating it at him.
- **King Tyrant:** Thinks he's hilarious. He thinks she's an audience.

**Element.** Wood — the Tree of Life, the Sha-mo Desert, where souls begin. She is the bloom; blooms are Wood.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Xiaoyu's flowing frame, Baguazhang's circles. MIND: the idol — radiant, performative, *aware* of being watched and generous with it. She remembers everyone's name. SOUL: SHA's bloom-splinter. The friction: the soul is sand — ancient, eroding, patient. The mind is an idol — bright, immediate, hungry for the now. She is the desert flower: the oldest thing in the land, blooming like it's the first day.

**Why Saku Ra thinks she's here.** She believes she was *chosen* — that the trial is a stage, and she was scouted for it. In her story, there's an audience beyond the head — fans, lights, a bigger show. She performs for them. The idea that there's no audience, only a dying god trying to heal itself, would break her heart. So the trial lets her keep the story. For now.

**Being purified of:** The performance. SHA is sand — it doesn't perform, it *persists*. Saku Ra's idol-mind is the splinter's defense: if she's loved, she won't erode. The trial is teaching her that the desert doesn't need applause. She's the slowest learner and the brightest student.

**Signature detail.** She remembers everyone's name — all 23 fighters, plus the Trader, plus the monks who disowned Zui. When a fighter dies in the Reincarnation and returns with a new face, she's the first to learn the new name. She says the old ones out loud, once, so they're not forgotten.

**Owner-locked:** Name Saku Ra (咲 = bloom). K-pop idol Powerpuff Girl direction, CN gigaleak PPG construction. Xiaoyu base.

### ZUI — The Drunken Soul
**Vessel of BYOU (病 — sickness)**

**Backstory.** Steve Fox's pure boxing, drowned in sake. Zui was a monk of the Central Mountain — real robes, real vows, real discipline. Then he found the bottle, and the bottle was more honest than the monastery. Now he's a hedonist with a monk's footwork and a drunk's philosophy: the straight line is a lie, everything stumbles, so stumble *beautifully*.

His body is a sketchbook — trippy multicolor tattoos, randomly placed, no plan, every one a temple he burned or a night he survived. The sake bottle never leaves his hand. His sway is supernatural; opponents swear he dodges punches he couldn't have seen.

**Personality.** Hedonist philosopher. Laughs mid-fight. Cries too, but won't admit it. Quotes sutras he claims to have forgotten.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The straight line is a lie. Everything stumbles."

**Relationships.**
- **The Central Mountain monks:** Disown him. He toasts them before every fight anyway.
- **Waterfall:** Understands him. Both drown in feeling; he just does it on purpose.
- **Pantera:** Finds him exhausting. Has never said so. The tail twitches when Zui talks.

**Element.** Water — the Palace of Dreaming. He drowns on purpose; the drowning is the point.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Steve's boxer frame, the purest hands in the trial. MIND: the hedonist philosopher — he left the monastery because the vows were a smaller truth than the bottle. SOUL: BYOU, sickness. The body is disciplined. The mind is free. The soul *moves wrong* — and Zui is the only vessel who *agrees* with his soul. He doesn't fight the sickness. He drinks with it. That's why he's unreadable: everyone else wrestles their soul. Zui bought his a round.

**Why Zui thinks he's here.** He believes the monastery sent him — that the monks who disowned him arranged this as a final lesson, a koan with fists. "Go and be drunk among the dead. Learn." He toasts them before every fight: "Still learning, you bastards." The monks didn't send him. But the story keeps him drinking, and the drinking keeps him honest, so the trial lets him keep it.

**Being purified of:** The bottle isn't the problem — the *running* is. BYOU moves wrong; Zui decided wrongness was freedom. The trial is teaching him that you can stumble beautifully *toward* something. He's starting to stumble toward Waterfall. She pretends not to notice. She notices.

**Signature detail.** Every tattoo is a temple he burned or a night he survived. There's one blank patch on his left shoulder blade. He says it's for the night he hasn't survived yet.

**Owner-locked:** Drunken master ex-monk hedonist. Sagat-meets-Chinese-mountain-monk visual. Sake bottle prop, supernatural sway, sketchbook tattoos. Name from 醉 (zuì), 醉拳 (zuìquán) = drunken fist. Steve Fox base.

### KINPO — The Buttercup Soul
**Splinter of SHA (沙 — sand, desert)**

**Backstory.** When SHA took form, the sand soul split three ways — sand divides. Kinpo is the thorn-splinter: tougher, blunter, green to Saku Ra's pink. Asuka's aggressive Kazama-ryu — no nonsense, no wasted charm, all forward pressure.

She is not the sidekick. She is the other protagonist. Buttercup to Blossom. The desert's hard half.

Kinpo — from 金鳳花 (kinpōge), the buttercup flower.

**Personality.** Blunt. Loyal. Allergic to sentiment. Says what Saku Ra is too kind to say.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Blossom leads. Buttercup finishes."

**Relationships.**
- **Saku Ra & Awa Pop:** Soul-sisters. Share one soul three ways, argue constantly, would die for each other without discussion.
- **Skull:** Mutual respect. Both understand that bluntness is a kindness.
- **Zui:** Annoyed by him. He thinks this is flirting. It is not.

**Element.** Wood — SHA's desert, the Tree of Life. The thorn is Wood's defense.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Asuka's aggressive frame, all forward pressure. MIND: blunt, loyal, allergic to sentiment — she says what Saku Ra is too kind to say, and means it as love. SOUL: SHA's thorn-splinter. The friction: the soul is sand — it yields, flows, surrounds. Kinpo *doesn't*. She's the grain that cuts. The soul wants to erode; she wants to *finish*. Sand divides. She divides things.

**Being purified of:** The hardness. The thorn protects the bloom — but Kinpo has started to believe she *is* only the thorn. The trial keeps putting her in situations where softness wins. She hates it. It's working.

**Signature detail.** She's the only fighter who visits the losers after matches. Not to gloat — to check. She brings water. She doesn't talk. Saku Ra told everyone. Kinpo denied it. She still does it.

**Owner-locked:** Buttercup (green Powerpuff Girl) to Saku Ra's Blossom. Name from kinpōge. Asuka base.

### WATERFALL — The Weeping Soul
**Vessel of all nine's grief**

## 5. THE STORY

### The Trial

Tong-Nou is weakening. Its five elemental forces — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — are scattering across the lands. The nine souls, fragments of one being, remember only their natures: not their names, not each other, not what they were before they broke.

Drawn to the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang, they find the trial inscribed in stone. It is not an invitation. It is a *diagnosis*.

*Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.*

### The Ladder

The trial is walked in the generating cycle — Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal enriches Water:

1. **Tree of Life** (Wood) — where souls begin. Claim Wood: your reach extends.
2. **Plains of Chi-U** (Fire) — confrontation. Claim Fire: your strikes chip through guard.
3. **Octagonal Shrine** (Earth) — the source. Claim Earth: your defense solidifies.
4. **Helix Palace** (Metal) — desire. Five glowing rooms. Claim Metal: you regenerate between rounds.
5. **Palace of Dreaming** (Water) — change. Claim Water: your movement quickens.
6. **Phantom Marketplace** — the Trader. No force claimed here. Instead: trade one force for another. Sometimes becoming whole means letting go.
7. **The Shrine, again** — the final ascent. The Assembled Soul waits.

Claiming all five forces forges the **Katana of Five Forces** — not found, but *proven* in combat. The fighter's skin carries the katana's pattern. The soul is nearly whole.

Nearly.

### The Trader

At the Phantom Marketplace, under the Eyeball Mountains, the Trader buys and sells elemental forces. "I will buy your Fire. I will sell you Water. All transactions are final. Nothing here is real, but the prices are."

The Trader's fighting style is mimicry — it wears the opponent's moveset like a borrowed coat. (The Trader is not one of the nine. The Trader is not a vessel. The Trader was already at the marketplace when the first soul arrived, and will be there when the last one leaves. Ask nothing else.)

### The Purifier Twist

The final opponent is not evil. There is no evil in the Western Heart. There is only fragmentation.

The **Assembled Soul** is a composite of all nine fighting styles — including yours. It moves like Pantera, strikes like Demonio, cuts like Phantom, punishes like Skull, flows like Saku Ra. It is every vessel at once.

It is you, assembled.

Defeating it doesn't destroy it. The victory animation is **absorption** — the composite dissolves into the player character, fragment by fragment, until only one fighter stands in the shrine.

Tong-Nou was not devouring souls. Tong-Nou was *purifying* them. The trial was healing all along. The weakening, the scattering, the fighting, the dying, the returning — all of it was the medicine.

The last text card, in Sato's deadpan:

*"You are whole. Rest now."*

### What the Fighters Want

Not every soul fights for the katana:

- **Pantera** fights because fighting is what he is. The katana is incidental.
- **Demonio** fights for the code he can't remember. Wholeness might mean remembering.
- **Phantom** fights because he was already fighting when the trial started. He may not stop when it ends.
- **Diablo** fights to conquer the trial and take the katana as a trophy. He doesn't understand what it is.
- **Skull** fights because the trial provides targets. He hasn't asked what happens when they run out.
- **SAM-UR-A1** fights because fighting is the only instruction he has. He is waiting for the rest of the program.
- **Saku Ra** fights to bloom. The desert flower doesn't ask why it grows.
- **Zui** fights because he's too drunk to leave and too honest to lie about it.
- **Kinpo** fights because Saku Ra is fighting, and soul-sisters don't let each other fight alone.
- **Waterfall** fights because the souls' pain won't stop, and neither will she.
- **King Tyrant** fights to be watched becoming whole. The coronation requires an audience.
- **Burger** fights to be full. The katana is a side dish.
- **Awa Pop** fights because the trio fights together. Bubbles doesn't let her sisters fight alone.
- **Scimitar** fights for leverage. The katana is worth more as a bargaining chip than a weapon.
- **Purple Rain** fights for the show. The katana would look good under the lights.
- **Nightwolf** fights so the others don't have to fight alone. The guide walks the ladder too.
- **Michiko** fights because she's here. The ladder is just another road. She'll leave when she's bored.
- **Star** fights because the music hasn't stopped. She'll stop when it does.
- **Torch** fights to get her sister out. The katana is the key — or the price.
- **Dio** doesn't fight *for* anything. The Hado fights. He's just the venue.
- **Cyborg X** fights because her diagnostics say to. She's still looking for the manifest.
- **Bruno** fights because it's better than thinking. The fists are quieter than the heart.
- **N3GΔTIV0** isn't fighting for the katana. He's working. The bounty is still out there.

They all climb the same ladder. They all reach the same shrine. They all face themselves.

**Signature detail.** His stomach growls in the exact pitch of the Appetite room's hum. When they sync, the whole Helix Palace shivers.

## 8. THE HEAD-WORLD, AS THE TRIAL FOUND IT

*Eastern Mind wiring + canon conversion, 2026-10-20. This section is additive: it extends the world of §1, the souls of §4, and the story of §5 without altering them. Every place, system, and beat below is converted from the Eastern Mind teardown (`teardown/eastern-mind/`); the full source-to-canon log is `teardown/eastern-mind/CANON-CONVERSION.md`, the file checklist is `teardown/eastern-mind/WIRING-INVENTORY.md`, and the machine-readable stage registry is `game/assets/stages/eastern-mind/stages.json`.*

### The Shape of the Head

The head is not a metaphor and it is not a container. It is a body, and the lands are its organs.

Outside is the dark — not night, not space, just the dark the head floats in, the dark Diablo and Dio tore through and the dark N3GΔTIV0 camps at the edge of. Inside, the dark continues, but it learns shapes: a tree, a plain, a mountain, a palace, another palace, a market. The sky inside the head is the inside of the head — dark, vast, veined — and the fighters who look up for too long stop looking up.

The lands are joined by **tunnels**. Every land keeps one: narrow passages in the head's own flesh, older than the trial, that open where a land ends and close where the next begins. Nobody maintains the tunnels. Nobody built them. A soul walks into the tunnel under the Tree of Life and walks out under a burning sky, and the distance between those two places is not a distance anyone can measure. The tunnels are why the trial can be a ladder: without them, the lands are islands, and islands do not climb.

Each land also keeps a **King**. Not a ruler — the Western Heart has no government — but a resident power: the figure a land grew around itself, the way a pearl grows around grit. The King of the scorched plains is not the King of the dreaming palace, and neither of them answers to the shrine. To walk a land, a soul meets its King. To claim a land's force, a soul settles with its King. What "settles" means is different in every land, and every land thinks its way is the obvious one.

### The Two Laws of Death

The trial runs on two laws of death, and every fighter learns both in their bones.

**In Versus, death is only death.** Two souls meet, one falls, the matter ends. No return, no new face, no lesson carried. This is why the veterans prefer Versus for settling things: what happens there stays ended. The head does not interfere. The head is not interested in endings. It is interested in returns.

**In the Story — the career of a soul walking the Trial — death is the mechanism.** Lose, and you do not continue. You *return*: reborn into a new vessel, chosen the way the Reincarnation has always chosen — from what you did. The soul that grabbed and held is offered vessels built for the hold. The soul that was never where the blow landed is offered vessels that move wrong. The soul that kept reaching for the other eight souls mid-fight is offered vessels that reach back. Your actions influence who you become next. That is not a feature of the game. That is the entire game.

Three details the shrine stones insist on:

- **The old vessel is gone.** A soul never returns to the body it died in. Grief has an address; it is not that one.
- **The face is chosen in pieces.** Three pairs of eyes, three noses, three mouths — the returning soul assembles the face it will be judged by, and the face is not cosmetic. Sharper eyes, faster hands. A heavier nose, heavier hits. The Reincarnation is the only character creator the Western Heart has ever needed.
- **The intruder waits for the third death.** Diablo cannot be chosen, offered, or inherited until a soul has died three times. Then — and the stones are silent about why — he may take an interest. The membrane let him in. Dying keeps letting him closer.

Nightwolf walks the newly dead through all of this. She is the only guide the Reincarnation has. She burns sage over the new faces. The faces do not thank her. She does it anyway.

### The Lands As Places

The five lands are arenas in §5. They are also *places*, and a soul walking the ladder lives in them longer than it fights in them.

**The Tree of Life** is entered through its field — open ground under the colossal tree, where glowing souls hang like fruit too high to pick and too bright to ignore. Deeper in is the forest proper, then the inner tree: hollow, warm, grown around a silence. Something large keeps a clearing in the Tree's country — the fighters call it the Ogre's clearing, and they lower their voices there, and none of them can say why. The Sha-mo Desert begins where the roots end. Saku Ra's home ground. Where souls begin is also where souls are *kept* — the fruit does not fall until the trial calls it.

**The Plains of Chi-U** are a field of confrontation under a burning sky, and the field is the honest part. The land's argument stands at its center: the **Fire Tower of Time**, which burns and never collapses, climbed in three reaches, each one hotter and more certain than the last. Time does not pass on the plains the way it passes elsewhere; it *accumulates*, like heat. Fighters who stay too long start finishing each other's sentences. Fighters who stay longer stop needing to.

**The Helix Palace** is approached the way desire is approached: through an entrance that flatters, up past a roof that reflects, around a boulder that should not be balanced where it is balanced, beneath palms and beside a flower that the palace gardeners — there are no gardeners — keep perfect. Inside, beside the castle-heart of the palace, are the five glowing rooms of §1: Greed, Appetite, Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction. The rooms are not locked. That is the palace's whole method. What the rooms claim, they keep: Burger belongs to Appetite, Scimitar's heart belongs to Greed whether he signs for it or not, Skull's violence has an address in Destruction, and the Immortality room is Doc Frank's, as far as he's concerned. The palace also keeps the souls it has reflected longest in side-chambers the ladder does not visit — a Buddha-quiet room, a Sha-quiet room — because desire keeps souvenirs of everything it has ever wanted, including stillness.

**The Palace of Dreaming** will not stay still, and its geography is the proof. It keeps two lakes that are one lake on different days. It keeps a palace in two aspects — the aspect that receives you and the aspect that receives who you were about to be. And it keeps *rooms for the souls*: a room wearing KAI's rhythm, a room wearing SHA's patience, rooms for the drowned and the ill and the eye that watches, a room with a map of a palace that is not this one. A soul sleeping in the Palace of Dreaming does not dream its own dreams. It dreams the room's. Waterfall's tears fall upward here, once a year, for one hour. She has stopped apologizing for it.

**The Phantom Marketplace** is the smallest land and the only one that is a *verb*. Under the Eyeball Mountains — which watch, which have always watched, which are the trial's only reliable audience — the Trader's people keep the stalls, the scales, and the ledger. Nothing here is real, but the prices are. The marketplace is neutral ground the way a knife is neutral: it cuts for whoever holds it. N3GΔTIV0 camps here for the sightlines. King Tyrant performs here for the crowd. Scimitar was born here, which the marketplace counts as its finest product and its worst mistake.

**The Octagonal Shrine Par-fang** sits on the Central Mountain, and the mountain is climbed by stairs that count the climber back. The shrine keeps two rooms before the last room: a room for arriving and a room for being weighed, though the stones will not say which is which. The last room is the smallest place in the Western Heart and the only one that matters — eight-sided, ancient, still — where the Assembled Soul waits, wearing every style the climber has ever survived, including the climber's own. The sky above the last room is the inside of the head at its thinnest. Winning there is not an explosion. It is an absorption, fragment by fragment, until one fighter is standing where two stood, and the mountain is quiet, and the quiet has a heartbeat in it.

### The Threshold

Before the first fight, and after the last one, every soul passes the same country. The trial calls it nothing. The fighters call it the Threshold, when they call it anything.

The Threshold keeps a **Face** — all the faces, hung like masks in a green room: the faces the dead wore, the faces the unborn will. A soul entering the trial sees the face it is about to wear before it wears it. A soul leaving sees every face it ever wore, in a row, watching. It keeps a **Book** — the illustrated record of the head-world, written in a hand that changes whenever it is read, in which every fighter in this bible has a page and no fighter has ever found their own page while looking for it. It keeps a **Mirror** that shows the soul its vessel from the soul's side — the only view no fighter is built to survive comfortably — and a **Sea**, because every crossing in the Western Heart is a water crossing in the end, and a **Rock**, because one thing in the Threshold does not change, and the trial points at it whenever a soul claims nothing stays the same. And it keeps the sumi-e dark: the ink-wash black from which the green head itself is painted, visible only at the Threshold, where the painting is thin.

The Threshold is also where the trial keeps its **ending**. Not a place — a condition. The absorbed soul does not go anywhere. The head simply stops hurting in that shape. What the ending looks like from inside is the one thing this bible does not describe, because the only witnesses are whole, and the whole do not come back to file reports. The last text card stands as written in §5. In the deadpan the head-world reserves for the truth:

*"You are whole. Rest now."*

### The Incarnations

The shrine keeps an incarnation record for every soul of the nine — a face, a portrait, a voice, a way the soul looked when the head first dreamed it. RIN's record is the plainest and the oldest. BYOU's record twitches in its frame. TOU's record is mostly impact damage. SHA's record is three records that insist they are one. KAI's record is wet. JIN's record is kept sharp. RETSU's record is kept behind the others, by the shrine's choice. ZEN has two records — the stillness, and the stillness again, identical, filed separately, because the shrine does not argue with ZEN. GYOU has two records as well, and both of them are blurred, and the second one is blurred *differently*.

The records are not archives. They are *doors*. The Reincarnation works because the shrine can open the record of a soul and let the soul wear what the record remembers. This is why the newly dead are offered vessels their actions earned: the records lean toward the living soul that resembles them. A soul that fights like battle itself finds TOU's door already open. This is also why Waterfall feels all nine: the records are kept in the same wall, and the wall is thin, and grief goes through thin walls the way water goes through everything.

SAM-UR-A1 has no record. The shrine has built him an empty frame, out of something like respect and something like curiosity, and the frame hangs with the others. What the frame will hold, if it ever holds anything, is the experiment of §2. The GYOU splinter in Cyborg X hums through the wall at night. The records of ZEN do not object. The records of ZEN have never objected to anything. That is what makes them frightening.

### Colour Is Law

The head-world runs on one palette the way a body runs on one blood. Every land draws its colours from that single shared spectrum — the deep greens of the head itself, the winter blue-whites of RIN's record, the desert ochres of SHA's country, the abyssal blues of KAI's water, the charcoal and molten crack of the Destruction room — and then each soul, each character, each face keeps a palette of its own inside the shared one. This is why a fighter reborn in a new face is still recognisable across the arena: the body changes, the colours testify.

Colour is how the trial tells the truth when words would lie. A land whose force has been claimed does not announce it. Its colours *settle* — the burning sky of Chi-U cools a fraction, the Dreaming palace's reflections clear — and the souls who live there feel the settling before they can name it. Tong-Nou is healing or it is not, and the palette is the chart on the hospital wall. Read the colours. They have never once been decorative.

### The Music of the Head-World

The head has a pulse, and the pulse has a theme. The trial's music is not scored to the fights; it is *under* them, the way a heartbeat is under a conversation — the head-world's theme, long and patient, that the fighters stop hearing after their first death and start hearing again before their last one.

For the ending there is the transmigration music: the sound of a soul crossing the Threshold in the leaving direction, played once per soul, never repeated, never hurried. And for the long walks between the lands — the tunnels, the stairs, the desert — there is the esoteric mix: the theme taken apart and put back together wrong on purpose, hours of it, the head humming to itself in the dark it floats in. A soul that hums along is not mocked for it. Half the roster hums. The head does not mind. The head started it.

### Soul Progression, or What the Ladder Does to a Soul

Claiming a force is not collecting a prize. It is a permanent change in the claiming soul, and §5 names them plainly: reach, bite, solidity, renewal, speed. What the ladder does *between* the claims matters as much:

- **A claimed force cannot be unclaimed — only traded.** The Trader exists because the generating cycle is a circle, not a staircase: sometimes becoming whole means letting go of the fire you fought hardest for, and the marketplace is where a soul learns what its own strength costs.
- **The Soul Surge is memory, not magic.** When a fighter channels one of the other eight souls, the borrowed techniques arrive with the borrowed *nature* attached — TOU's surge has no brakes, BYOU's surge cannot be read because it cannot read itself — and when the ten seconds end, the fighter keeps a splinter of having been someone else. Fighters who surge often start finishing each other's sentences, the way the plains teach. The nine were one being. The surge is the scar tissue remembering.
- **Grief is progression too.** Every death on the ladder passes through Waterfall. A soul that has died often carries her attention the way a veteran carries weather in a wound — and she, alone, can read a soul's whole career at a glance: not the wins, the *returns*. Who you kept becoming is who you are. The trial grades on the returns.

The Assembled Soul is the sum of all of it: every force claimed, every vessel worn, every surge borrowed, every death returned from — assembled, and waiting in the last room, and patient, because it has already waited exactly as long as you have.
