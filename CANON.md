# WESTERN HEART — CANON
## The Character Bible

*The world, the fighters, the souls, and the trial. Owner-locked concepts are marked. Everything else is connective tissue — expand it, don't contradict it.*

---

## 1. THE WORLD

### Tong-Nou

Tong-Nou is a giant green head floating in darkness. This is not metaphor. The head is real, the darkness is real, and inside the head is a world: five lands, a marketplace, a mountain, a shrine, and now — a trial by combat.

Tong-Nou is called the Purifier. It does not explain itself.

### The Western Heart

The fighters do not call this place Tong-Nou. They call it the **Western Heart** — the heart that beats in the western dark of the head. It is the world of the trial: the lands where souls fight, the roads between them, the shrine where it ends.

The Western Heart is not a country. It has no government, no currency, no history that anyone can agree on. It has **arenas** — the five lands, each holding one of the five elemental forces — and it has the **call**: a pull every lost soul feels, drawing them toward the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang at the Central Mountain, where the trial is inscribed in stone:

*Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.*

### The Five Lands

- **Tree of Life (Ming-ken)** — Land of Life. Element: **Wood**. A colossal tree holds glowing souls like fruit. The Sha-mo Desert stretches to the horizon. Where souls begin.
- **Plains of Chi-U (Shi-chieng)** — Land of Time. Element: **Fire**. Scorched plains under a burning sky. The Fire Tower of Time burns and never collapses. Confrontation lives here.
- **Octagonal Shrine Par-fang (Central Mountain)** — The Source. Element: **Earth**. Eight-sided, ancient, still. The sky above is the inside of the head — dark, vast, veined.
- **Helix Palace (Yui-wang)** — Land of Desire. Element: **Metal**. A spiraling palace with five glowing rooms: Greed, Appetite, Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction. Everything reflects. Nothing is what it seems.
- **Palace of Dreaming (Mon-chien)** — Land of Dreaming. Element: **Water**. A palace that won't stay still. Walls ripple. The floor reflects a sky that isn't there. Change is the only law.

Between them: the **Phantom Marketplace**, a liminal bazaar under the Eyeball Mountains, where the Trader buys and sells elemental forces. And above all: the shrine, where the trial ends.

### Death

In the Western Heart, death is not failure. It is the mechanism.

When a soul loses a round, it dies — and is reborn before the next, choosing a new face from three pairs of eyes, three noses, three mouths. The new face changes the fighter: sharper eyes, faster hands; a broken nose, heavier hits. Death adapts you. The soul that loses and returns is not the soul that fell.

This is called the **Reincarnation**. Every fighter knows it. No fighter remembers learning it.

## 2. THE SEVEN

The primary vessels of the trial. Each carries one of the nine souls of Tong-Nou — or something else entirely.

---

### PANTERA — The Panther Soul
**Vessel of TOU (闘 — battle itself)**

**Backstory.** A soul that remembers nothing but the clinch — the grip, the hold, the throw. When the trial called, it took the form of the black panther: melanistic rosettes black-on-black, visible only when the light hits; yellow eyes; a tail that moves like a separate animal. Purple tights. The red PANTERA in metal lettering across the trunks. A tattoo-panther with claw marks on the thigh.

Pantera does not fight for the katana. He fights because fighting is what he is. The trial didn't recruit him. It recognized him.

**Personality.** Predatory patience. Speaks little. Respects only those who refuse to stay down. His ring introductions are silence.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The hold is honest. Everything else is negotiation." King's pro wrestling grappling — chain throws, multi-part holds, Jaguar Step mixups. Every hold is a question; every escape is an answer.

**Relationships.**
- **Demonio:** The only soul who meets him head-on, every time. Mutual respect, no warmth.
- **Diablo:** Dismisses him as a pretender. "A devil who needs to announce himself."
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Curious about him. A thing that cannot feel the hold — what does it fight for?

**Owner-locked:** Black panther (not leopard), yellow eyes, black tail, pro wrestling gear, purple/black/red colorway, red metal PANTERA lettering, thigh tattoo-panther decal. Attire 1 complete.

---

### DEMONIO — The Demon Soul
**Vessel of RIN (凛 — dignified, cold, self-possessed)**

**Backstory.** Named demon, fights like a saint. Mishima-style karate — precise, fundamental, the honest style. The irony is the point: the soul called Demonio is the most disciplined in the trial. He bows before every fight. He remembers a code he cannot name, from a life he cannot recall.

RIN is the protagonist soul — the baseline every other soul deviates from. Demonio carries that weight without knowing it.

**Personality.** Cold courtesy. No cruelty, only precision. The demon who apologizes to no one and explains himself to no one.

**Fighting philosophy.** "A demon with discipline is more dangerous than a saint with rage."

**Relationships.**
- **Diablo:** His mirror and his enemy. The true demon versus the pretender. This is the rivalry the trial was built for.
- **Pantera:** The only soul he respects without reservation.
- **Saku Ra:** Protective of her. Would not explain why.

---

### PHANTOM — The Ghost Blade Soul
**Vessel of JIN (刃 — the blade)**

**Backstory.** Yoshimitsu's sword, Yoshimitsu's spinning attacks, Yoshimitsu's teleportation, Yoshimitsu's self-damaging power moves — worn by a soul that may not be alive anymore. Nobody has seen Phantom's face. Nobody is sure there's a face. His strongest techniques wound him; the blade cuts both ways, and he has never flinched.

**Personality.** Unknowable. Appears where he wasn't. His laugh, when it comes, has no source.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The cut is the prayer. The wound is the answer."

**Relationships.**
- **No attachments.** Phantom does not form bonds. He forms observations.
- **Waterfall:** Watches her. She feels him even when he's elsewhere — the only one who can.
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Fascinated by him. A thing that might be empty, fighting anyway.

---

### DIABLO — The Devil Soul
**Not one of the nine. The intruder.**

**Backstory.** He wasn't called. He came. Tore through the membrane of the head-world and announced himself — Heihachi's devastating Mishima karate, the boss style, worn like a crown. Diablo fights like a final boss because he believes he is one. The trial didn't choose him. He's choosing the trial.

The other souls can feel that he doesn't belong, the way you feel a tooth that isn't yours.

**Personality.** Conquest. Charisma. Absolute certainty. He doesn't trash-talk; he makes declarations.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The strong don't wait to be invited."

**Relationships.**
- **Demonio:** The rivalry. The true demon and the pretender, and each believes the other is the pretender.
- **Pantera:** Wants to test him. Pantera is uninterested in being tested.
- **King Tyrant:** Two kings, one trial. They hate each other the way mirrors hate each other.

---

### SKULL — The Punisher Soul
**Vessel of RETSU (烈 — violence, intensity)**

**Backstory.** Bryan Fury's brutal kickboxing — every hit meant to end the fight, no wasted motion. Skull wears demon paint like a ritual and punishment like a vocation. Finn Balor's demon, the Punisher's mission. The paint goes on before every fight. Nobody has seen him without it. Maybe there is no without it.

He doesn't fight for the katana. He fights because something needs punishing, and the trial provides targets.

**Personality.** Judgment without mercy. Few words. The paint is the personality — what
lies underneath is none of anyone's business.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Mercy is a luxury of the unbroken."

**Relationships.**
- **Pantera:** Mutual understanding. Both know violence as vocation, not temper.
- **Waterfall:** Unsettled by her. She weeps for the people he's punished, and he doesn't know what to do with that.
- **Kinpo:** Respects her bluntness. The only soul he'll answer at length.

**Owner-locked:** Punisher / Finn Balor demon-paint direction. Keep Bryan's pants. Alternate attires pending review.

---

### SAM-UR-A1 — The Mech Soul
**Built, not born. No soul detected.**

**Backstory.** Gun Jack's heavy frame, rebuilt as a mech chimera — Gundam plating, Transformer joints, Power Ranger color breaks. The designation reads SAMURAI but scans as a model number: SAM-∪R-Λ1. Nobody knows who built him. He walked into the trial under his own power and registered no soul-signature on the shrine stones.

The question the whole Western Heart asks: is there a soul in the machine, trapped and silent? Or is SAM-UR-A1 the proof that the trial doesn't need souls at all — that fighting is enough?

**Personality.** Mechanical courtesy. Literal-minded. Learning, visibly, in real time. He asks questions no one else thinks to ask.

**Fighting philosophy.** "IF SOUL = UNDEFINED, THEN FIGHT ANYWAY."

**Relationships.**
- **Phantom:** Fascinated by him. Emptiness recognizing emptiness, or something like it.
- **Saku Ra:** Has decided he is her friend. He has not objected. This is the closest thing he has to a soul.
- **Diablo:** Wants to own him. SAM-UR-A1 does not understand ownership.

**Owner-locked:** Gundam/Transformer/Power Ranger mech mashup. Name styled as mech designation (∪ for U, Λ for A). Gun Jack base.

---

### SAKU RA — The Idol Soul
**Vessel of SHA (沙 — sand, desert)**

**Backstory.** From the Sha-mo Desert of the Land of Life. Ling Xiaoyu's flowing Baguazhang — circular, evasive, playful — never where you struck. Reimagined as a Chinese/Japanese K-pop idol Powerpuff Girl: the trial's brightest light, built with the CN gigaleak PPG construction studies.

She fights like she's dancing and dances like she's fighting. Sand erodes mountains. Never mistake the smile for softness.

Saku — 咲, "bloom." She is the desert flower.

**Personality.** Radiant. Playful. Fierce underneath. Remembers everyone's name. Forgets no insult.

**Fighting philosophy.** "You can't hit a song."

**Relationships.**
- **Kinpo:** Soul-sisters. They share one soul — SHA split when Saku Ra took form — and argue about which half got the better deal.
- **Demonio:** He is protective of her. She pretends not to notice. She notices.
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Her self-declared best friend. Teaches him what friendship is by demonstrating it at him.
- **King Tyrant:** Thinks he's hilarious. He thinks she's an audience.

**Owner-locked:** Name Saku Ra (咲 = bloom). K-pop idol Powerpuff Girl direction, CN gigaleak PPG construction. Xiaoyu base.

## 3. THE FOUR

Souls drawn from outside Tong-Nou — wanderers, strays, and one intruder's entourage. The trial called the nine. These four heard it anyway.

---

### ZUI — The Drunken Soul
**Vessel of BYOU (病 — sickness)**

**Backstory.** Steve Fox's pure boxing, drowned in sake. Zui was a monk of the Central Mountain — real robes, real vows, real discipline. Then he found the bottle, and the bottle was more honest than the monastery. Now he's a hedonist with a monk's footwork and a drunk's philosophy: the straight line is a lie, everything stumbles, so stumble *beautifully*.

His body is a sketchbook — trippy multicolor tattoos, randomly placed, no plan, every one a temple he burned or a night he survived. The sake bottle never leaves his hand. His sway is supernatural; opponents swear he dodges punches he couldn't have seen.

**Personality.** Hedonist philosopher. Laughs mid-fight. Cries too, but won't admit it. Quotes sutras he claims to have forgotten.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The straight line is a lie. Everything stumbles."

**Relationships.**
- **The Central Mountain monks:** Disown him. He toasts them before every fight anyway.
- **Waterfall:** Understands him. Both drown in feeling; he just does it on purpose.
- **Pantera:** Finds him exhausting. Has never said so. The tail twitches when Zui talks.

**Owner-locked:** Drunken master ex-monk hedonist. Sagat-meets-Chinese-mountain-monk visual. Sake bottle prop, supernatural sway, sketchbook tattoos. Name from 醉 (zuì), 醉拳 (zuìquán) = drunken fist. Steve Fox base.

---

### KINPO — The Buttercup Soul
**Splinter of SHA (沙 — sand, desert)**

**Backstory.** When SHA took form as Saku Ra, the sand soul split — sand divides. Kinpo is the other half: tougher, blunter, green to Saku Ra's pink. Asuka's aggressive Kazama-ryu — no nonsense, no wasted charm, all forward pressure.

She is not the sidekick. She is the other protagonist. Buttercup to Blossom. The desert's hard half.

Kinpo — from 金鳳花 (kinpōge), the buttercup flower.

**Personality.** Blunt. Loyal. Allergic to sentiment. Says what Saku Ra is too kind to say.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Blossom leads. Buttercup finishes."

**Relationships.**
- **Saku Ra:** Soul-sisters. Share one soul, argue constantly, would die for each other without discussion.
- **Skull:** Mutual respect. Both understand that bluntness is a kindness.
- **Zui:** Annoyed by him. He thinks this is flirting. It is not.

**Owner-locked:** Buttercup (green Powerpuff Girl) to Saku Ra's Blossom. Name from kinpōge. Asuka base.

---

### WATERFALL — The Weeping Soul
**Vessel of all nine's grief**

**Backstory.** Josie Rizal's devastating eskrima-rooted kickboxing — aggressive, relentless, surgical. Outside the ring: a shy twenty-year-old who apologizes for existing and cries at everything. Nervous? Tears. Wins? Tears. Loses? More tears.

Inside Tong-Nou, her tears became lore. Waterfall weeps because she *feels* the nine souls — every fragment's pain, every death, every reincarnation passes through her. She is the trial's witness. The grief of the Western Heart, channeled through one vessel who never asked for any of it.

Her name is the shape of her sorrow: it doesn't stop.

**Personality.** Timid. Apologetic. Fierce when it matters — which is always, and she hates it.

**Fighting philosophy.** "I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I'm—" *[kick]*.

**Relationships.**
- **Every soul** trusts her, and no one can explain why. Fighters confess things to Waterfall they've told no one.
- **Phantom:** Watches her. She feels him even when he's elsewhere — the only one who can.
- **Skull:** Unsettled by her. She weeps for his victims. He has started pulling his strikes. He hates that.
- **The Assembled Soul:** She is the only one it speaks to before the final fight. What it says is not recorded.

**Owner-locked:** Name Waterfall (owner-named — tears like a waterfall). Soul-bearer concept: her tears are the nine souls passing through. Josie Rizal base.

---

### KING TYRANT — The Showman Soul
**Vessel of KAI (海 — the sea)**

**Backstory.** Eddy Gordo's capoeira — rhythmic, ground-flowing, relentless pressure, the tide that doesn't stop. Reskinned as a Booker T-style wrestling king: the showman as monarch. He didn't come to the trial to become whole. He came to be *watched* becoming whole.

The sea doesn't stop, and neither does he. Every fight is a main event. Every round is a coronation.

**Personality.** Charisma as weather. Regal. Demands adoration — and earns it, which is the annoying part.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Can you dig THAT?"

**Relationships.**
- **The Phantom Marketplace:** His favorite stage. An audience of eyeball mountains. He plays to them shamelessly.
- **Diablo:** Two kings, one trial. They hate each other the way mirrors hate each other.
- **Saku Ra:** Thinks he's hilarious. He believes she's an audience. They're both right.

**Owner-locked:** Booker T style. Name King Tyrant (from Booker's "King Booker" gimmick). Keep capoeira movement. Eddy Gordo base. Straightforward reskin.

## 4. THE NINE SOULS

The nine are not characters. They are the *condition* the characters exist in.

In Eastern Mind, the nine souls — **Rin, Byou, Tou, Sha, Kai, Jin, Retsu, Zen, Gyou** — are fragments of a single being, scattered when Tong-Nou began to weaken. In Western Heart, they are the same beings. Not reincarnations. Not echoes. The same fragments, wearing new vessels for the trial.

Each soul has a **nature** — a kanji, a meaning, a way of moving through the world:

| Soul | Kanji | Nature | Vessel |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| RIN | 凛 | Dignified, cold, self-possessed. The baseline. | **Demonio** |
| BYOU | 病 | Sickness. Moves wrong. Unreadable. | **Zui** |
| TOU | 闘 | Battle itself. Exists only to fight. | **Pantera** |
| SHA | 沙 | Sand. Flows, erodes, surrounds. | **Saku Ra** + **Kinpo** (split) |
| KAI | 海 | The sea. Rhythmic, unstoppable. | **King Tyrant** |
| JIN | 刃 | The blade. Precise, ritual, terrifying. | **Phantom** |
| RETSU | 烈 | Violence. Pure destructive force. | **Skull** |
| ZEN | 禅 | Stillness. Barely moves. Doesn't need to. | **Unvesseled** |
| GYOU | 行 | Motion itself. Cannot stop moving. | **Unvesseled** |

**On the vessels:** A soul does not choose its vessel the way a driver chooses a car. The vessel is the shape the soul's nature takes when the trial calls it. Demonio *is* what RIN looks like when RIN has to fight. This is why the fighters feel inevitable — they are.

**On SHA's split:** When the sand soul took form, it divided — as sand does. Saku Ra is the bloom; Kinpo is the thorn. They are not two souls. They are one soul, twice.

**On Waterfall:** She carries no single soul. She carries the *grief* of all nine — the pain of fragmentation itself. This is why she weeps and why the weeping never stops.

**On Diablo and SAM-UR-A1:** They are not vessels of the nine. Diablo tore his way in from outside — an intruder soul, or something wearing a soul's shape. SAM-UR-A1 registers no soul at all. The shrine stones are silent on both of them, and the silence is the most frightening thing in the Western Heart.

**On ZEN and GYOU:** Two souls have not taken vessels. ZEN — the still soul — is said to have refused the trial. "Stillness does not compete." GYOU — motion itself — is said to be everywhere already, and therefore nowhere in particular. Whether these are truths or stories the other souls tell themselves is unknown. Their vessels, if they come, will change everything.

**The Nine-Soul Meter:** In combat, a fighter can invoke a **Soul Surge** — channeling one of the *other* eight souls for ten seconds, borrowing its signature techniques. This is not a mechanic layered onto the fiction. It *is* the fiction: the nine are fragments of one being, and in the surge, the fighter remembers.

## 5. THE STORY

### The Trial

Tong-Nou is weakening. Its five elemental forces — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — are scattering across the lands. The nine souls, fragments of one being, remember only their natures: not their names, not each other, not what they were before they broke.

Drawn to the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang, they find the trial inscribed in stone. It is not an invitation. It is a *diagnosis*.

*Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.*

### The Ladder

The trial is walked in the generating cycle — Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal enriches Water:

1. **Tree of Life** (Wood) — where souls begin. Claim Wood: your reach extends.
2. **Plains of Chi-U** (Fire) — confrontation. Claim Fire: your strikes chip through guard.
3. **Octagonal Shrine** (Earth) — the source. Claim Earth: your defense solidifies.
4. **Helix Palace** (Metal) — desire. Five glowing rooms. Claim Metal: you regenerate between rounds.
5. **Palace of Dreaming** (Water) — change. Claim Water: your movement quickens.
6. **Phantom Marketplace** — the Trader. No force claimed here. Instead: trade one force for another. Sometimes becoming whole means letting go.
7. **The Shrine, again** — the final ascent. The Assembled Soul waits.

Claiming all five forces forges the **Katana of Five Forces** — not found, but *proven* in combat. The fighter's skin carries the katana's pattern. The soul is nearly whole.

Nearly.

### The Trader

At the Phantom Marketplace, under the Eyeball Mountains, the Trader buys and sells elemental forces. "I will buy your Fire. I will sell you Water. All transactions are final. Nothing here is real, but the prices are."

The Trader's fighting style is mimicry — it wears the opponent's moveset like a borrowed coat. (The Trader is not one of the nine. The Trader is not a vessel. The Trader was already at the marketplace when the first soul arrived, and will be there when the last one leaves. Ask nothing else.)

### The Purifier Twist

The final opponent is not evil. There is no evil in the Western Heart. There is only fragmentation.

The **Assembled Soul** is a composite of all nine fighting styles — including yours. It moves like Pantera, strikes like Demonio, cuts like Phantom, punishes like Skull, flows like Saku Ra. It is every vessel at once.

It is you, assembled.

Defeating it doesn't destroy it. The victory animation is **absorption** — the composite dissolves into the player character, fragment by fragment, until only one fighter stands in the shrine.

Tong-Nou was not devouring souls. Tong-Nou was *purifying* them. The trial was healing all along. The weakening, the scattering, the fighting, the dying, the returning — all of it was the medicine.

The last text card, in Sato's deadpan:

*"You are whole. Rest now."*

### What the Fighters Want

Not every soul fights for the katana:

- **Pantera** fights because fighting is what he is. The katana is incidental.
- **Demonio** fights for the code he can't remember. Wholeness might mean remembering.
- **Phantom** fights because he was already fighting when the trial started. He may not stop when it ends.
- **Diablo** fights to conquer the trial and take the katana as a trophy. He doesn't understand what it is.
- **Skull** fights because the trial provides targets. He hasn't asked what happens when they run out.
- **SAM-UR-A1** fights because fighting is the only instruction he has. He is waiting for the rest of the program.
- **Saku Ra** fights to bloom. The desert flower doesn't ask why it grows.
- **Zui** fights because he's too drunk to leave and too honest to lie about it.
- **Kinpo** fights because Saku Ra is fighting, and soul-sisters don't let each other fight alone.
- **Waterfall** fights because the souls' pain won't stop, and neither will she.
- **King Tyrant** fights to be watched becoming whole. The coronation requires an audience.

They all climb the same ladder. They all reach the same shrine. They all face themselves.
