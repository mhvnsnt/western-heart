# DIABLO — Canon

## Identity

**DIABLO.** "The Devil Soul" is a **title only** — he is **NOT one of the nine**: he is the **intruder who tore through the membrane**. One of the core Seven fighters. His universe tradition layer is **Shinto Raijin + Hagoromo** — the thunder god and the feather robe.

## Appearance / model

- **Attire 1** = the owner's own Tripo aee46f6a-3bbc-4fac-9492-96e13759672d (it replaced a rejected, broken GTA-format conversion). Owner-described, verbatim in the record: **oni Heihachi — horns; Heihachi's wing hair grown into long flowing silver locks; irezumi tattoo work across chest, shoulders and arms; purple hakama with gold dragon embroidery on the legs.**
- **Attire 2 — "Devil God"** = owner Tripo 9b096022-1e16-4a23-80bb-57c6ea7ec62b: the **Hagoromo feather robe**; the ring behind him is **Raijin's Taiko Drum Ring** — the Mitsudomoe swirl, the thunder god's halo of drums.

Owner direction on the base era: he **rejected Tekken 3 Heihachi** (too polygonal) — he wants the T5/T6/T7/TTT2-era Heihachi.

## Story

He wasn't called. **He came.** Whatever tore the membrane the first time — he followed the tear like a smell. He believes he conquered his way in, that the trial is a kingdom and kingdoms are taken. He is being purified of nothing; the trial uses him as its **sparring partner** — the thing the lost souls measure their becoming against. Signature detail: he **announces the outcome before the final round** — "You die in the next exchange" — and commits to it completely. He has been wrong twice. He remembers both times the way other men remember scars. (Repository canon.)

## Connections

- **Demonio:** THE rivalry — the true demon against the pretender costume; each believes the other is the pretender.
- **Pantera:** he wants to test him — the only soul whose strength he respects as *animal*, not technique.
- **Thor:** two uninvited kings — their fights are earthquakes.
- **King Tyrant:** two kings, one sea one land; the rivalry is older than the trial. (repo canon)

## Moves / base

Base: **Heihachi Mishima** — Mishima karate, the boss style: the patriarch's overwhelming fundamentals.

**Element:** Fire — the conquering fire (with the Raijin thunder layer in his design, above).
