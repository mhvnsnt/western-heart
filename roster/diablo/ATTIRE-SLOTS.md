## Canon — Attire 2 "Devil God" (owner 2026-10-20)

Attire 2 is the **"Devil God"** attire. Diablo wears a traditional
**Hagoromo** — the feather robe, the divine drapery — which fits
perfectly with the Japanese God aesthetic.

The circular ring floating behind him is **Raijin's Taiko Drum Ring**
(the Raijin Halo). In Shinto mythology, **Raijin** — the God of Thunder,
Lightning, and Storms — rides on clouds while surrounded by this wheel
of taiko drums. Legend says he beats the drums to create the sound of
thunder echoing across the sky.

The swirl emblem painted on each drum is the **Mitsudomoe** (三つ巴), a
three-comma design — a traditional Shinto symbol representing divine
power, the cycle of life, and elemental forces like water and thunder.
