# DOROWANI — Voice Canon (Owner-Directive, 2026-10-22)

**Status:** LOCKED by M.Dogg. Dorowani is the FIRST voice-acted character in Western Heart.

## The Rule

**Dorowani does NOT speak in sentences.**

He is a Pokemon/Digimon-style creature. He barks random Japanese words — no grammar, no full thoughts, no complete ideas. Think Pikachu energy, but with Japanese vocabulary.

## First Lines (user-written, verbatim)

> **"Konnichiwa arigato Ichiban Bankai."**

These are his first canon words. All future voice lines follow this pattern.

## Voice Direction

- **Style:** Like Squirtle — creature sounds (growls, chirps, barks) mixed with his Japanese word bursts.
- **Pattern:** Single Japanese words or short bursts. Never sentences. Never grammar.
- **Examples of valid bursts:** "Konnichiwa!" "Arigato!" "Ichiban!" "Bankai!" "Sugoi!" "Yatta!"
- **Invalid:** Any complete sentence. Any grammatical construction. Any English (except as random borrowed words, Pokemon-style).

## Voice Moments

Dorowani is voice-acted at:
1. **Win poses / taunts** — victory barks
2. **Intro sequences** — pre-fight barks (see INTRO INTERACTION SYSTEM below)
3. **Cutscenes** — real-time rendered, in-engine (future work)

## Intro Interaction System (owner directive 2026-10-22)

Tekken-style pre-fight character interactions when two fighters are picked — but VARIED, not one fixed interaction per matchup. Multiple different interactions per character pairing. Builds backstory and canon.

For Dorowani: his side of any intro is his word-bursts (e.g., opponent says their line, Dorowani barks "Konnichiwa! Bankai!"). The contrast is the comedy and the character.
