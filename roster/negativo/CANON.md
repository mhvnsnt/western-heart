# N3GΔTIV0 — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** N3GΔTIV0 (leetspeak for "Negativo") — The Hunter. **Not a soul. Not a vessel. A contractor.** Roster dir: `negativo`. Owner-locked name.

**Backstory.** Negan's bat, Negan's grin, Negan's absolute certainty that the room belongs to him — scaled up to the galaxies. N3GΔTIV0 hunts zombies across the stars. Kills them for bounties. Kills them for hire. That's the only reason — unless he just wants to kill. Sinestro's gift: fear as a weapon — he doesn't just kill the dead, he makes them *afraid* first. Black Flash's nature: death itself, unstoppable — when he decides you're dead, no reincarnation saves you. Then a bounty led him to a giant green head floating in darkness. And inside: souls that die and return. Die and return. Die and return. He looked at the Reincarnation and saw *zombies*.

**Personality.** Charismatic. Menacing. The grin that means he's already decided.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Bounty or fun? Your call. Actually — mine."

**Element.** None — and differently than Dio. Dio is *rejected* by the elements. N3GΔTIV0 is *outside* them. He camps in the Phantom Marketplace (neutral ground, good sightlines) and treats the whole Western Heart as his hunting ground. The Trader sells him information. The Trader sells everyone information.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Negan's brutal frame, the bat (barbed wire, well-maintained) an extension of the arm (base scaffolding only). MIND: the contractor — every target is a job, every job has a price, every price is negotiable except the ones that aren't. SOUL: unknown. He's from *outside the head* — the galaxies, the real dark, not Tong-Nou's interior dark. The trial's instruments don't read him. He might not have a soul. He might have something else. He hasn't said.

**Why he thinks he's here.** He followed a bounty. That's it. That's the whole story, and it's true. A soul escaped the trial — or was *taken* — and the contract led him to the head. He doesn't wonder who issued the contract. Contractors don't ask. But he's been here longer than the job should take, and the bat's handle is running out of room, and lately he's started wondering: what if the bounty isn't a soul? What if it's the *head*? He hasn't decided whether that's a bigger payday or a bigger problem.

**Being purified of:** He is not being purified. He is not lost. He is *working* — and the trial doesn't know what to do with someone who isn't broken. Tong-Nou didn't call him. He's going to find what's owed him, and then he's going to leave. That's what he tells himself.

**What he fights for (recorded):** he isn't fighting for the katana. He's working. The bounty is still out there.

**Relationships.**
- **Sun Rā:** Professional curiosity, both directions. Sun Rā eats souls; N3GΔTIV0 hunts the undead. Are they competitors? Is N3GΔTIV0 the health inspector? They've shared a drink. Neither will say what was discussed.
- **Phantom:** N3GΔTIV0 isn't sure Phantom is alive. Phantom isn't sure either. They have an understanding: N3GΔTIV0 doesn't hunt him, Phantom doesn't ask why.
- **Waterfall:** She feels the deaths he's caused — every zombie, every bounty, the weight of a galaxy's worth of endings. It nearly drowns her. He avoids her. She's the one thing that makes him feel the cost — and a bounty hunter can't afford the cost.
- **Mariposa:** He doesn't hunt her. He's not sure she's alive in the way he understands alive. She offered him nectar once. He didn't know what to do with it. He kept the cup.
- **The vessels, generally:** They whisper that he can kill *permanently* — no Reincarnation, no return. He hasn't confirmed it. He hasn't denied it. The grin says enough.

**Signature detail.** Before every match, he offers his opponent a choice: "Bounty or fun?" No one knows what the difference is. No one wants to find out. The kill-count is carved into the bat's handle. He's running out of room.

**Recorded defect note (not canon):** the shipped model has a pink patch on his face flagged for fixing in the owner's 2026-10-20 intake notes — a build note, recorded here only so it isn't lost.

**Owner-locked:** Name N3GΔTIV0 (leetspeak for "Negativo"). Zombie bounty hunter across the galaxies × Sinestro (fear as weapon) × Black Flash (death itself, unstoppable). Kills for bounties, for hire, or for fun. Negan base (scaffolding only).
