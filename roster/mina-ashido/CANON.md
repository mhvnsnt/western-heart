# RYUSAN — Canon

## Identity

**RYUSAN — The Corrosion Soul.** Not one of the nine. A mortal lost soul the call reached — and what the call found was already half-dissolved.

Her soul carries the corrosion left by the **Jotei family's soul-binding crimes**. She is living proof of what they did: the scar, walking around, furious. Where other fighters carry a soul, Ryusan carries a soul with a hole corroded through it — and the hole is where the acid lives.

She is an adult. Acid punk. Her sweat burns. Her spit burns. Where she stands still too long, the ground smokes.

## Story

The Jotei family bound souls — that is the crime at the center of her story, the same crime that scarred Chatur and set Tobi running. Ryusan was caught in the binding. It didn't take. What it left instead was the corrosion: an acid that eats everything it touches, including — she has tested this — the trial's own geography.

Tobi tipped her off. The rabbit was carrying a warning the night the family came to collect, and the warning arrived before the collectors did. Ryusan has honored exactly one debt since: Tobi's. Tobi insists there is no debt. Couriers deliver. That's all.

She heard the call the way every lost soul does — a pull toward the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang, toward the inscription in stone: *Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.* She came because the Jotei family will have to walk the same ladder, and she intends to be standing on it when they do.

## Personality

Volcanic, funny on purpose, loyal to exactly two people. She says the quiet part loud and the loud part louder. Strangers get the performance; Tobi gets the rare quiet version, which is somehow more alarming.

**Fighting philosophy:** *"You don't get to tell me to calm down."*

## Body / Mind / Soul

- **BODY:** the corrosion's vessel — acid-slick, built to survive her own chemistry. What doesn't kill her only makes her hiss.
- **MIND:** grievance, catalogued. She remembers every name, every date, every binding. The list is the only thing she keeps tidy.
- **SOUL:** not one of the nine. A mortal soul with a hole corroded through it. The trial looks at the hole the way a doctor looks at a wound — interested, and not entirely sure it can be closed.

## Element

**Unclaimed.** The five lands' forces dissolve on contact with her — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water, all of them smoke and pit where her acid lands. The Trader at the Phantom Marketplace refuses her business. The trial is still deciding what to do with her, the way it is still deciding what to do with all the ones who don't fit the nine.

## Why she thinks she's here

She thinks she's here to make the Jotei family answer. The trial thinks she's here to be purified. Both might be right. Only one of them gets to go first.

## Being purified of

The rage — or the corrosion. The trial hasn't told her which, and she hasn't asked. She suspects the answer is *yes*.

## Signature detail

Where she stands still too long, the arena floor smokes. Her intro: she spits, and the spit sizzles. Her win pose: she looks at her own hands like she's still surprised they're hers.

## Voice

Sparse, imagistic, Japanese-influenced. Short sentences. No wasted words — the acid does the elaborating.

- Intro: *"You don't get to tell me to calm down."*
- Win: *"Still smoking."*
- To Jotei's family, if they ever climb: *"I kept the receipt."*
- To Tobi, once, quietly: *"The warning arrived. That's the whole story."*

## Connections

- **Tobi:** the courier who tipped her off. The only debt Ryusan honors; the only person Ryusan won't burn.
- **Jotei:** the exiled empress. Her family's soul-binding crimes are the reason Ryusan is what she is. (Roster entry pending — intake canon 2026-10-22.)
- **Chatur:** fellow scar of the same crimes. They don't talk about it. They don't have to.

## Moves / base (fighting style, in-universe)

Dance-fighter pressure: a swaying, rhythmic stance game that breaks into relentless strings — mids, lows, back-turned mixups, a spinning dance stance she weaponizes. She fights like the music is only audible to her. On top of the base kit: the corrosion itself — acid spit, acid-tipped claw rakes, a shoulder charge that armors through punishment, and a full-body meltdown she only spends at full meter.
