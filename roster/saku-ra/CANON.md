# SAKU RA — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** SAKU RA — The Idol Soul. **Vessel of SHA (沙 — sand, desert)**, the bloom-splinter of the sand soul split three ways. From the Sha-mo Desert of the Land of Life. Saku — 咲, "bloom." She is the desert flower.

**Backstory.** Ling Xiaoyu's flowing Baguazhang — circular, evasive, playful — never where you struck, reimagined as a Chinese/Japanese K-pop idol Powerpuff Girl: the trial's brightest light, built with the CN gigaleak PPG construction studies. She fights like she's dancing and dances like she's fighting. Sand erodes mountains. Never mistake the smile for softness.

**Personality.** Radiant. Playful. Fierce underneath. Remembers everyone's name. Forgets no insult.

**Fighting philosophy.** "You can't hit a song."

**Relationships.**
- **Kinpo & Awa Pop:** Soul-sisters. They share one soul — SHA split three ways when Saku Ra took form — and argue about which splinter got the better deal.
- **Demonio:** He is protective of her. She pretends not to notice. She notices.
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Her self-declared best friend. Teaches him what friendship is by demonstrating it at him.
- **King Tyrant:** Thinks he's hilarious. He thinks she's an audience.
- **Mariposa:** The bloom and the butterfly. Saku Ra has adopted her as the trio's unofficial fourth. Kinpo pretends to object. Awa Pop braided her hair.

**Element.** Wood — the Tree of Life, the Sha-mo Desert, where souls begin. She is the bloom; blooms are Wood.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Xiaoyu's flowing frame, Baguazhang's circles (base scaffolding only — Xiaoyu is not a person in this universe; owner canon law 2026-10-23). MIND: the idol — radiant, performative, aware of being watched and generous with it. SOUL: SHA's bloom-splinter. The friction: the soul is sand — ancient, eroding, patient. The mind is an idol — bright, immediate, hungry for the now. She is the desert flower: the oldest thing in the land, blooming like it's the first day.

**Why she thinks she's here.** She believes she was *chosen* — that the trial is a stage, and she was scouted for it. In her story, there's an audience beyond the head. The idea that there's no audience, only a dying god trying to heal itself, would break her heart. So the trial lets her keep the story. For now.

**Being purified of:** The performance. SHA is sand — it doesn't perform, it *persists*. Saku Ra's idol-mind is the splinter's defense: if she's loved, she won't erode. The trial is teaching her that the desert doesn't need applause. She's the slowest learner and the brightest student.

**What she fights for (recorded):** to bloom. The desert flower doesn't ask why it grows.

**Signature detail.** She remembers everyone's name — all the fighters, plus the Trader, plus the monks who disowned Zui. When a fighter dies in the Reincarnation and returns with a new face, she's the first to learn the new name. She says the old ones out loud, once, so they're not forgotten.

**Owner-locked:** Name Saku Ra (咲 = bloom). K-pop idol Powerpuff Girl direction, CN gigaleak PPG construction. Xiaoyu base (scaffolding only).
