# PURPLE RAIN — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** PURPLE RAIN — The Performer Soul. **A lost soul of the Helix Palace's Desire rooms.** Name is the Prince reference (owner-locked).

**Backstory.** Lee Chaolan's flawless technique — the Mishima-style precision without the Mishima rage, all polish and cruelty — worn by a soul that treats combat as choreography. Michael Jackson's footwork. Prince's command of a stage. Slickback's pimp-strut certainty. He doesn't fight opponents. He *performs* victories, and the opponent is just the audience that bleeds. The Helix Palace's Desire rooms made him: not one room but all of them — Greed, Appetite, Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction — reflected in a single mirrored soul.

**Personality.** Flamboyant. Magnetic. Every entrance is a concert. Every kick lands on the beat.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The fight is the show. You're just the stage."

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace, Desire itself. He reflects everything and reveals nothing.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Lee's exquisite technique, every strike placed like a note (base scaffolding only). MIND: the performer — he doesn't know who he is offstage, and he's afraid to find out. SOUL: a mortal soul, lost in the Desire rooms, reflected until the reflection became the person. The three agree on one thing: the show must go on. They disagree on everything else.

**Why he thinks he's here.** He believes he's *headlining* — that the trial is a tour, and this is just the latest venue. Bigger stage, weirder crowd, same show. He keeps asking when the tour moves on. The answer — that there's no tour, no next venue, just the head — is the one thing he refuses to hear. The show must go on. It will. That's the tragedy.

**Being purified of:** Performance. Learning to fight for himself, not the audience. The trial keeps emptying the arena for his matches. He keeps performing anyway.

**What he fights for (recorded):** the show. The katana would look good under the lights.

**Relationships.**
- **King Tyrant:** Showman rivalry. Two performers, one spotlight. Their feud is the trial's most entertaining sideshow — both of them know it, which makes it worse.
- **Saku Ra:** Fellow idol. He wants to collaborate — a duet, a double act, a *moment*. She's not sure he's serious. He is serious about the art. He's never serious about anything else.
- **Star:** Movement artists. He choreographs; she improvises. They've never performed together. The trial holds its breath.
- **Silvertongue:** Fellow showmen. They have a standing bet on who bows lower after a win. The bows keep getting lower. Someone will get hurt.
- **Kiko Tanaka / Rave:** Two showmen, mutual admiration, professional jealousy. Their entrances keep getting longer. The Trader has started selling tickets.
- **Finxsse (external — Bannon sub-universe):** Showmen recognize each other. Purple Rain performs; Finxsse promos. He once asked to borrow Finxsse's cross for an entrance. Finxsse said no faster than he's ever moved in the ring.

**Signature detail.** He fights in 4/4 time. Listen: the kicks land on the beat. Opponents who find the rhythm can predict him. No one has ever found the rhythm twice.

**Owner-locked:** Name PURPLE RAIN (Prince reference). MJ × Prince × Slickback — flamboyant but dangerous. Lee Chaolan base (scaffolding only).
