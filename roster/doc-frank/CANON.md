# DOC FRANK — Canon

## Identity

**DOC FRANK — The Builder Soul.** Also **Android 9**, or just **9**. Born **Franklin Bauzzkoh** — he shortened it to **Frank Bozko** for business and recognition: "nobody funds a Bauzzkoh." (He was first called "Dr. Boscoe" in production, then renamed DOC FRANK by the owner.) He is the universe's **Dr. Frankenstein and Dr. Gero in one coat** — the mad scientist who started building androids and didn't stop until he'd turned *himself* into one. He is an android/cyborg/robot himself (owner correction: not "soundboards" — that was a transcription error).

Naming rule (owner-locked): the Android name belongs to Doc Frank — he is Android 9. **No "Android X" anywhere** (that mistaken name belonged to the Cyborg X confusion; Alisa is Cyborg X).

## Appearance / model

Reskin direction: almost like Android 13 from Dragon Ball Z, with multiple skins based on every android from DBZ. Owner forefront rule: **attires 1–5 are his Tripo models**; the Tekken-version reskins go towards the back, never slots 1 or 2 — and proofs for Doc Frank must show the Tripo models. Owner correction on provenance: the **machine-shop attire is a texture reskin** of the base model — NOT a Tripo — and is Attire 1's pre-transform form in that lineage.

## Forms — every attire transforms

All Doc Frank attires have mid-match transformations, and the transformation is **Hulk-style gradual growth**: one arm grows, then the other, legs one at a time, body parts swelling in sequence — not an instant swap. Mad-scientist body-horror growth.

- **Attire 1:** the old man → a bigger, Roshi-style form.
- **Attire 2 — "Wowomomo":** Dr. Gero × Mojo Jojo × Dr. Bosconovitch × Dr. Weird. Full transparent glass dome, green see-through fluid at partial fill with sloshing physics, his brain floating in the fluid — **never add an extra brain**. Transformed form: brain sticking out of the top of his head, no dome.
- **Attire 3 — "Hood scientist":** old mob boss scientist going out on a club night.
- **Attire 4 — "FRANKENSTEIN'S CYBORG"** (owner's title), with its own transformation form.

The full ladder lives in `roster/doc-frank/ATTIRE-SLOTS.md`.

## Story

He **built Cyborg X and SAM-UR-A1** — the three built souls: two machines who wonder if they have souls, and the man who built them because he wasn't sure *he* did. In one of his per-character story arcs he gets hold of Pantera and turns him into a panther cyborg (PANTXRΛ-88), grafting Heavy Leomon weapon pieces onto him. He believes the trial is *peer review* — that Tong-Nou called him to evaluate the soul as an engineering problem, and he'll publish. He is being purified of the god complex. Signature detail: he takes notes mid-fight, on a clipboard, in crayon — detailed, accurate, and deeply insulting.

## Connections

- **Cyborg X (Cape Jones):** his creation — his daughter, his masterpiece, his proof. She has detachable parts and his stubbornness. She won't know her lab origin until she runs into him.
- **SAM-UR-A1:** his other build — the question he built to ask itself. It keeps asking what it fights for; he keeps saying "data." They're both lying.
- **Pantera:** the cyborg story arc (above).
- **DXMMII:** the patchwork wants no fixing. Doc Frank wants to fix it anyway.

## Moves / base

Base: **Dr. Bosconovitch** (Tekken) — he needs Bosconovitch's FULL moveset **plus Dr. Gero (DBZ)-style moves**.

**Element:** Metal — the Helix Palace, the Immortality room. The room is *his* room, as far as he's concerned.
