# RAVE / KIKO TANAKA — Canon

## Identity — one person, two names

**RAVE is the main game/display name in Western Heart. Kiko Tanaka is the same person.** Owner-locked (2026-10-19, re-confirmed 2026-10-23):

> "Kiko is rave in this game and Kiko in others, Rave is his Raver personality from our canon."
> "Merge in and remember, Kiko, the ghost tanaka is Rave."

Also known as: **Kiko**, **Ghost**, **Kiko Tanaka**. Book-canon nickname: **"The Ghost"** (the JPCW run). He/him. The unrobed, shrouded-face **"ghost Tanaka"** variant is the Rave form. His canon file lives here, under `roster/kiko-tanaka/` (the `roster/rave/` concept doc is the same person's design record).

Real-life basis: **Keiji Mutoh — "The Great Muta"** — one of the owner's favorite wrestlers EVER: "the mist, the paint, the mask, the entrance as art." Kiko is a co-created character (the owner co-wrote with him); the memorial song **"Encore Anyway"** was built from Kiko's own line: **"The theater is empty. Encore anyway."**

Character note (private canon): a demon/kaiju/oni monster **in public**; a sweet, vulnerable human underneath ("I miss Daddy" is the tell); he may go ghost — absent without announcement — as a person as well as a presence. Voice direction: **mostly Japanese, occasionally broken English; he is played straight**, not campy. His insanity has rules.

## Story

He didn't hear the trial's call. He heard there was an **audience**. He came to perform the Reincarnation, not undergo it — the only soul who treats the trial as a venue. He is being purified of **the mask**: not the paint, the *need* — the belief that without the performance there is no one underneath worth meeting. (Repository canon.)

## Imported canon — from AshLane

Kiko Tanaka is an AshLane character before he is a Western Heart fighter; his AshLane record adds the working life behind the paint:

- **Role: "The Detective"** — stealth specialist, Level 4 (Dynasty Authority designs). Great Muta basis; fighting-style reference Lei Wulong — Hong Kong police detective, Jackie Chan *Police Story* energy. Styles: Five Animals kung fu (Dragon, Snake, Tiger, Leopard, Crane) **plus drunken fist (Zuiquan)** — unpredictable, flowing.
- **AshLane uniform:** detective trench coat (navy/black, worn open), plainclothes underneath (dark shirt, tactical pants), night-vision goggles pushed up on the forehead, shoulder holster (detective-style, not a duty belt), fingerless tactical gloves, quiet-soled boots; optional subtle face-paint markings — a Muta nod, tactical not theatrical.
- **Signature moves (AshLane canon):** **The Shining Wizard** (Muta's iconic knee strike — owner confirmed), **The Phantom Lock** (submission — "by the time you see him, it's already on"), **The Shadow Slip** (rapid dodges — "makes him seem invisible"), plus stance-switching between animal forms.
- **Five attires (owner, 2026-10-05):** 1. **Street** (no face paint) — detective trench coat, normal. 2. **Face paint** — Great Muta style, theatrical. 3. **Kiko the Ghost Tanaka** — full face paint + Great Muta mask. 4. **Police** (no face paint) — tactical police gear. 5. **Police Ghost** — face paint + Great Muta mask + police tactical.
- **The 9th Robed Council Member — OCCASIONAL** (AshLane story bible): book canon, JPCW — "The Ghost." He is NOT a permanent council member; he appears only sometimes. Plot function: mystery member — his identity is a reveal moment. The record also settles a naming ghost of its own: **"KG Mudo" was a speech-to-text mishearing of "Keiji Mutoh"** — the inspiration for the council attire, NOT a separate character. (This is the council form recorded above: white fur, dragon tights, gray beard, no paint and no mask when revealed.)
- **Voice (owner direction, 2026-10-11):** Kiko's AI voice is Keiji Mutoh / The Great Muta. He speaks Japanese with Mutoh's voice and accent when speaking English — Tosh-style code-switching: Japanese with subtitles sometimes, broken English sometimes. Mutoh's real voice is a calm, soft-spoken, medium-deep baritone; he barely speaks English (his interpreter was Sonny Onoo), and Japanese interview audio is the secured reference.
- **The dedication on "Encore Anyway":** the memorial song built from his own line (see Identity, above) carries end-of-video dedication cards in the owner's exact text: 武藤敬司のお父さんに捧ぐ / "Dedicated to Keiji Mutoh's father" and グレート・カブキ (米良明久) に捧ぐ / "Dedicated to Great Kabuki (Akihisa Mera)". The owner cried while writing the dedication and asked that it be documented — the most personal beat in the Kiko program.
- **Why the likeness is guarded:** Mutoh is one of the owner's favorite wrestlers EVER — "I had to make sure you get Kiko right because of that and who he's based on" (2026-10-09). A set of ~10 Kiko correction cards was ordered 2026-10-06 requiring Mutoh's facial likeness across painted variants, unpainted, masked versions, the entrance robe with face-covering hood, young Keiji (black hair) and old Keiji (bald, grey goatee) — each in wrestling, civilian, and police-detective (Lei Wulong-inspired) wear. The owner's direction for the character's quiet moments: the monster with humanity underneath — the demon is the armor, the person underneath is real (the same affection he holds for Muta and for Kane).
