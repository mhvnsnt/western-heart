# DEATHFANG — Canon

## Identity

**DEATHFANG** — all caps, one word (owner-locked spelling). A new proprietary character based on **Iori Yagami**. He is a prisoner — to his own grief and emotions — chained and shackled, emo, mixed with the "NO FUTURE" face-tattoo character from Soul Eater. Iori's own inspirations include **Free the werewolf** (Soul Eater), and Deathfang is deeply based on both.

- **Human form** (owner-dictated): an emo loner, really misunderstood — people see him and think **"this dude can't be who they say he is."**
- **Werewolf form** (owner-dictated): the monster that terrorizes towns — bloodlust. That's why he got put in prison. When people see the wolf form: **"oh shit, we get it."**

## Appearance / model

His chains are part of the design: collar, wrist and ankle irons, chains hanging from his body — and he keeps the **chain details under his knees like Iori**. **NO FUTURE is tattooed on his face.**

The owner made his own Tripo models for BOTH forms, and they **supersede the Iori reskin as the canon attires** (the reskin is preserved back in the ladder as legacy):

- **Attire 1 (human):** Tripo 6486151e-acfe-49fe-b0a1-503dc3ff202b.
- **Attire 2 (werewolf):** Tripo b4094ecf-df5a-4255-a26c-6c49734fcc3a — the werewolf face/eyeballs are CORRECT on this one.

Eye status (recorded, unresolved): the human form's open eye shipped with an untextured, skin-colored eyeball; the owner's verdict on the painted-eye fix was rejection — **"that eye looks so funny and it's not placed right; he also needs eyelashes and eyebrows."** The eye must be a REAL eye placed through Character Customization, with eyelashes and eyebrows. The current eye is provisional only.

## Story

He **transforms mid-fight, human ↔ werewolf**. The werewolf form's bloodlust — terrorizing towns — is why he was put in prison; the chains are the sentence he carries into every fight. (From the repo character bible: the transform fires at a full nine-soul meter — WEREWOLF UNLEASHED — and the prison he escaped was built by people who knew what he was.)

## Connections

No connections to other roster fighters are recorded in the owner's material. His recorded ties are to his source inspirations only (Iori Yagami; Free the werewolf).

## Moves / base

Base: **Iori Yagami** — keep Iori's moves, stance, and animations (animation bank: DEATHFANG ← Iori). **Iori's stance + full moveset is the base for BOTH forms.** The werewolf form adds werewolf/beast mix-in moves on top — in **attacks** only: even in werewolf form, his **stance and normal locomotion are Iori's** (same idle, same walk, same basic movement as the human form); a more werewolf-flavored run cycle is the only locomotion exception on record.
