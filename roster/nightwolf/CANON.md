# NIGHTWOLF — Canon

## Identity — and who she is for

**NIGHTWOLF — The Guide Soul.** A Native American spirit warrior, connected to land and ancestors. She is a **spirit-walker who came deliberately** — the trial's hospice worker, the only soul here that isn't broken; every soul needs a door, and she came to be one.

Nightwolf is a **tribute** (owner-locked, 2026-10-21): she is for the owner's late Native American friend **SERENA MISAE BROWN**, who died in a car accident and liked playing Mortal Kombat. The owner's words:

> "This game is for that game of Mortal Kombat me and Serena never got to play before she died in that car accident in Tulsa. For Serena, Serena Misae Brown, Nightwolf."

One night Serena wanted to come over and stay the night at the owner's and his wife's apartment and play Mortal Kombat with him, on a night he was up late alone; he didn't invite her over, and he carries that. Her name goes into this character's bio/lore in the roster docs, and **she heads the credits dedications** (story endings + settings credits). The owner's dedication list, verbatim: "for serena, for Rosetta, for Emory, for mars, for ken, for marquisha, for bink".

## Story

She came through ritual, **on purpose** — the only soul who did. Everyone else fell through the membrane or tore it; she *walked* through, properly, with permission from things the others haven't seen. She finds the newly dead where they land, sits them down, tells them what's happening, **burns sage over their new faces**. She is being purified of nothing. She *is* the purification. (Repository canon.)
