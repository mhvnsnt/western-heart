## Written 2026-10-23 — built on the recorded foundations (owner's to revise)

- **Who he is in the trial:** Chatur is a demon the trial did not call and could not keep out — not an intruder like Diablo (who tore in to conquer) or Dio (who tore in from outside the metaphysics), but a *creditor*. Somewhere in the old stories he is owed a meal by a god, and he followed the debt into the head. He is polite about it. Rakshasas in the stories are never rude before dinner. He treats the ladder as a menu he is reading slowly, and the Marketplace as the only honest room in the head: the one place where appetite admits what it is.
- **Element (written):** Metal — the Helix Palace, where he haunts the Greed and Appetite rooms' shared wall, arguing with the architecture about which room a demon belongs to. Burger is the Appetite room's curse; Chatur is its *critic* — he finds the curse wasteful. "He starves in a palace of food. Amateur."
- **Relationships (written):** Scimitar is his only peer — two ledgers, two appetites for position; the vizier collects debts, Chatur collects *creditors*, and each is trying to end up owning the other's book. Dio unsettles him professionally: a demon-shaped hunger with no name, no manners, and no accounts — Chatur files him under "force of nature, do not invoice." Mariposa watches him the way she watches all leaks; he is the only demon who bows to her — he knows a keeper when he smells one.
- **Branch thread — the Invoice:** in Story mode, Chatur's route opens with a choice no other fighter gets: present his invoice to the Trader (the debt the old stories owe him) and skip the Tree of Life rung at double price later — or climb honestly like a mortal and keep the invoice unspent, growing interest at every rung. At the shrine, the unspent invoice is the one thing the Assembled Soul doesn't have a copy of. What he buys with it is the branch the run remembers.
