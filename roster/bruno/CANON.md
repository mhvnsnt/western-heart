# BRUNO — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** BRUNO — The Heartbreak Soul. **A mortal soul, drowned in feeling** — one of the lost souls, not of the nine. Owner-locked: name BRUNO (owner: "Miguel is Bruno already").

**Backstory.** Miguel Caballero Rojo's street-brawler fury — the raw, emotional, all-power-punches-and-pain style, fighting like every hit is personal — worn by a soul that died of heartbreak. Not metaphorically. The heart stopped because it couldn't stand another beat of the ache. The Weeknd's 3AM despair. Bruno Mars' silk over the wound. Boomhauer's mumble — talking around the thing that hurts. Johnny Bravo's swagger — the armor. He's a beautiful mess, and he knows it, and he'd rather die again than say so out loud.

**Personality.** Moody. Smooth. Mumbling. Vain. A heartbreak in a leather jacket.

**Fighting philosophy.** "You hit like my ex. Everybody hits like my ex."

**Element.** Water — the Palace of Dreaming, emotion, change. He drowns differently than Zui: Zui drowns on purpose, Bruno drowns because he never learned to swim.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Miguel's brawler frame, every punch thrown from the gut (base scaffolding only). MIND: the swagger — the Weeknd's mystery, Bruno Mars' showmanship, Johnny Bravo's vanity, all of it armor over the wound. SOUL: a mortal soul, broken the ordinary way — by love, or the lack of it. The body wants to fight. The mind wants to be *seen* fighting. The soul wants to stop hurting. None of them are getting what they want.

**Why he thinks he's here.** He believes he *died* — and he's right, but he thinks it's the ordinary kind. In his story, this is the afterlife, and the afterlife is a fighting tournament, because of course it is. He hasn't considered that it's not the afterlife — it's a *hospital*. The idea that he's being healed would embarrass him. He'd rather be damned. Damned is cooler.

**Being purified of:** Heartbreak. Learning to feel without drowning. The Palace of Dreaming keeps showing him the moment it broke. He keeps looking. The trial thinks this is progress. He's not sure.

**What he fights for (recorded):** because it's better than thinking. The fists are quieter than the heart.

**Relationships.**
- **Waterfall:** She understands his heartbreak — she feels it every time he's near. He hates being understood. He keeps coming back.
- **Zui:** Zui keeps offering him drinks. Bruno keeps accepting. They're becoming friends. Both pretend otherwise.
- **Michiko:** She recognizes the outlaw in him — he ran from love the way she ran from a throne. She doesn't say it. He knows she knows.

**Signature detail.** His mumble is actually crystal clear. He's saying exactly what he means. You just have to lean in. No one leans in. That's the whole thing.

**Owner-locked:** Name BRUNO (owner: "Miguel is Bruno already"). The Weeknd × Bruno Mars × Boomhauer × Johnny Bravo. Moody R&B mystery + smooth showmanship + mumbling drawl + vain pretty-boy swagger. Spanish brawler. Miguel base (scaffolding only).
