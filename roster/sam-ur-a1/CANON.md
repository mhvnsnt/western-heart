# SAM-UR-A1 — Canon

## Identity

**SAM-UR-A1** — styled **SAM-∪R-Λ1**: SAM dash, U replaced by ∪, R, dash, Λ (shaped like A), 1 — it **reads "SAMURAI" and looks like a mech designation**. The owner clarified the name as **"Samurai A1."** One of the **core Seven**.

He is a roster mystery: **built, not born — no soul detected.** The shrine stones are silent for him. ("Mech Soul" appears as a title in one bio list; the standing canon is that he has **no soul** — the title is not a literal soul assignment.)

## Appearance / model

His look is a **Gundam / Transformer / Power Ranger mech mashup** — pieces from different mech designs mixed into one robot (the owner referenced his Biomechanica-style designs).

**Attire 1** (owner Tripo 7c94ba99) has **extra floating arms — just the forearms down**. The owner notes they can **blast his fist off like a rocket, like Gun Jack does in his movesets** in the Tekken games.

## Story

He was **built by Doc Frank**. He believes he was **deployed** — that somewhere there's a command that sent him, and the silence since is a communications failure, not an absence. He asks every new fighter: **"WHAT IS YOUR DESIGNATION?"** He logs the answers. He has never been given an order. He fights because fighting is what he was built *near*, and it's the only instruction he's ever inferred correctly. (Repository canon for the deployment belief and designation ritual; the built-by-Frank fact is owner-locked.)

He pairs with Cyborg X as **the two built souls** in a trial for souls — and with Doc Frank, his builder, they are the **three built souls**. But Frank BUILT the others.

## Connections

- **Doc Frank:** his builder (above).
- **Cyborg X (Cape Jones):** the other built soul — see above.
- **Phantom:** fascinated by him. "A thing that cannot die properly." (repo canon)
- **Saku Ra:** has declared him her friend. He has logged this as a designation. (repo canon)
- **Diablo:** wants to own him. (repo canon)

## Moves / base

Base/animation bank: **Jack / Gun Jack** (Tekken). Gun Jack power-robot moveset. (Model record: the Tekken Tag-era Gun Jack was accepted by the owner when no Jack-7 rip was found — "we can get Jack from somewhere.")

**Signature:** the rocket fist — floating forearms that blast off like Gun Jack's.
