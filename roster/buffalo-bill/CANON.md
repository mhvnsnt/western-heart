# BUFFALO BILL — CANON

### BUFFALO BILL — The Horned Messenger (Bonus)

**Category.** Bonus fighter. Not one of the nine souls. A mortal lost soul — lost the ordinary way.

**The two names.** Unrobed, he is **BUFFALO BILL**. Robed — hooded, grey, burnt down — he is **ASHES**. Same man. Two aspects. The robe is not a disguise; it is a decision about which of him you get to face.

**Backstory.** He came out of the Sha-mo Desert with the horns already on him — dread braids wound and coiled into ram horns, curled and heavy, the shape his soul took when the call took it. Canon-wise he carries the strength of a thousand gods in a strongman's frame; the braids are the only delicate thing about him. Before the head-world, he was a voice in an underground: not the words, the weight under them. Distorted. Low. The kind of sound you feel in your teeth. He never called himself a fighter then. He called himself a creator, and the noise was only ever the outlet. The message was always bigger than the medium.

In the Western Heart, the medium changed. The message didn't.

He named himself Buffalo Bill for the great horned beast of the plains — the animal that does not run from the storm but turns its horns toward it and stands. The horns were not chosen. They were already there. They had always been there. The name was the one thing he got to pick.

**Malakor City.** He leads the wizard gang — nine wizards of Malakor City, and he is their head. Malakor City is where the hooded go: medieval stone, cosmic dark, deep purple and blue, eyes floating where faces should be. He learned the robe there. Ashes is what the city made of him; Buffalo Bill is what he was before the city got its hands on him.

**The nine wizards of Malakor City** (owner's roster, 2026-10-23, verbatim):
- **ASHES** (Buffalo Bill, leader) — red robe
- **CIPHER** — yellow robe
- **STATIC** — blue robe
- **ECHO** — pink robe
- **ONYX** — green robe
- **HOLLOW** — orange robe
- **SOMBRA NEGRA** — black robe
- **THEORY** — purple robe
- **KIKO TANAKA / GHOST / RAVE** — white fur robe

Six of the nine walk the Ashlane road as well (Static, Cipher, Echo, Onyx, Hollow, Sombra Negra) — the gang and the 28 are tangled at the root. The nine answer to the horns.

**Powers.** He controls magic flame and ash. Not the honest fire of the Plains of Chi-U — a worked fire, a wizard's fire, called and shaped. The ash is what it leaves: grey, fine, everywhere, in the lungs of everyone who stood too close. When he fights unrobed, the flame comes off his hands like breath. When he fights as Ashes, the ash comes first and the flame follows — you don't see the second one coming through the first.

**Personality.** Dark the way a closed room is dark — not empty, full. Aggressive the way weather is aggressive: no malice in it, only pressure. He fights the way he used to release: volume, output, no stillness between rounds. As Buffalo Bill he is the brawler — forward, loud, horns-first. As Ashes he is the wizard — patient, hooded, the ash doing the talking before he does. He does not talk much in either aspect. When he does, it lands like a dropped amp.

**Fighting philosophy.** *"You hear me before you see me."*

**Relationships.**
- **The Trader:** Buffalo Bill has sold at the Phantom Marketplace, under the Eyeball Mountains. What he sold, he won't say. The Trader remembers every transaction. *All transactions are final. Nothing here is real, but the prices are.*
- **BRUNO:** Two brawlers from the outer dark. They recognize the weight in each other. Neither one moves first at the table.
- **The nine wizards:** His gang, in their colored robes. They follow the horns. What they get in return, only Malakor City knows.
- **The Ashlane crew:** He did not arrive alone. The ones who came with him know the old noise. They don't talk about before.

**Element.** Fire — the Plains of Chi-U, land of confrontation, under the burning sky. His fights live there even when the arena doesn't. The ash is fire's memory.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** Body: the horns, the mass, the forward pressure — a buffalo's argument made flesh; and the robe, when he chooses it, which changes the argument entirely. Mind: the message, bigger than the fight, carried by it — and the wizard's patience underneath the brawler's noise. Soul: mortal, unvesseled by any of the nine. The shrine stones are silent on him. He prefers it that way.

**Why he thinks he's here.** He thinks the trial is a stage. He is not wrong.

**Being purified of:** The need to be heard. He hasn't noticed the need yet.

**Signature detail.** The robe is the tell. Hood up, grey, ash on the hem — that is Ashes, and the fight will be slow until it isn't. Beneath the shrouded cowl, his face is gone: all you can see is a permanent smile and two little sparkly eyes. The ram horns are gone too — unbound, they hang as plain braids from beneath the shroud. Hood down, horns coiled and bare — that is Buffalo Bill, and the fight starts loud.
