# WATERFALL — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** WATERFALL — The Weeping Soul. **Vessel of all nine's grief** — she carries no single soul; she carries the pain of fragmentation itself. Owner-named — tears like a waterfall.

**Backstory.** Josie Rizal's devastating eskrima-rooted kickboxing — aggressive, relentless, surgical. Outside the ring: a shy twenty-year-old who apologizes for existing and cries at everything. Nervous? Tears. Wins? Tears. Loses? More tears. Inside Tong-Nou, her tears became lore: Waterfall weeps because she *feels* the nine souls — every fragment's pain, every death, every reincarnation passes through her. She is the trial's witness. The grief of the Western Heart, channeled through one vessel who never asked for any of it. Her name is the shape of her sorrow: it doesn't stop.

**Personality.** Timid. Apologetic. Fierce when it matters — which is always, and she hates it.

**Fighting philosophy.** "I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I'm—" *[kick]*.

**Relationships.**
- **Every soul** trusts her, and no one can explain why. Fighters confess things to Waterfall they've told no one.
- **Phantom:** Watches her. She feels him even when he's elsewhere — the only one who can.
- **Skull:** Unsettled by her. She weeps for his victims. He has started pulling his strikes. He hates that.
- **Nightwolf:** The two witnesses. Nightwolf sees what the dead become; Waterfall feels what they suffered. Between them, no soul goes unwitnessed.
- **Zui:** Understands him. Both drown in feeling; he just does it on purpose. She is who he is starting to stumble toward.
- **Bruno:** She understands his heartbreak — she feels it every time he's near. He hates being understood. He keeps coming back.
- **Mariposa:** Two women carrying things too heavy to name. Mariposa lands lightly on Waterfall's shoulder and stays until the tears slow. It works. Nobody knows why.
- **Dio:** She cannot weep for him. There is no grief in him to feel. This frightens her more than anything in the Western Heart.
- **N3GΔTIV0:** She feels the deaths he's caused — a galaxy's worth of endings. It nearly drowns her. He avoids her.
- **The Assembled Soul:** She is the only one it speaks to before the final fight. What it says is not recorded.

**Element.** Water — the Palace of Dreaming. Obviously. Her name is the element. (Recorded also: her tears fall upward in the Palace of Dreaming once a year, for one hour. She has stopped apologizing for it.)

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Josie's devastating kickboxing, surgical and relentless (base scaffolding only). MIND: the shy twenty-year-old — apologetic, anxious, kind beyond reason. SOUL: not one soul but the *grief* of all nine. She is the trial's open wound: the one place where the pain isn't being purified, just *held*. Someone has to hold it. She volunteered by existing.

**Why she thinks she's here.** She believes she's being *punished* — that the tears are a sentence for something she did. She can't remember the crime. She's apologized anyway, preemptively, to everyone. The truth — that she's the trial's witness, its most sacred function — would sound like a lie to her.

**Being purified of:** Nothing. She isn't being purified — she *is* the purification, or part of it. The grief has to go somewhere while the souls heal. She holds it. When the trial ends, the grief ends. What happens to her then is the one question the trial won't answer.

**What she fights for (recorded):** because the souls' pain won't stop, and neither will she.

**Signature detail.** The Assembled Soul speaks to her before the final fight. What it says is not recorded. But afterward, she always stops crying — for exactly one hour. Then it starts again.

**Owner-locked:** Name Waterfall (owner-named — tears like a waterfall). Soul-bearer concept: her tears are the nine souls passing through. Josie Rizal base (scaffolding only).
