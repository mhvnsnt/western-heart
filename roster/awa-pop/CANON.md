# AWA POP — Canon

## Identity

**AWA POP — The Bubble Soul.** The third splinter of **SHA** (sand — the Desert Soul), split three ways: **Saku Ra is the bloom, Kinpo is the thorn, Awa Pop is the bubble.** Her name is 泡 (**awa** — Japanese for "bubble") + **Pop**.

She is the **"Bubbles"** of the PPG-style K-pop idol trio: Saku Ra = Blossom (the leader), Kinpo = Buttercup (the tough one), Awa Pop = Bubbles (the sweet one).

Record correction (owner): Awa Pop is **Nina Williams**. The owner once misremembered her as Lili — corrected in the record: **Lili was NEVER renamed**; Awa Pop is Nina.

## Story

She believes she was **invited** — that someone saw the trio's act and booked them for the trial, and any day now the contract will be explained. She is being purified of **the assassin's detachment**: she is a trained killer who processed the detachment into cute, and the trial is teaching her that detachment isn't lightness. **She hums while she fights.** It is not a taunt. She doesn't know she's doing it. (Repository canon.)
