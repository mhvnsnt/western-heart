## Written 2026-10-23 — built on the recorded foundations (owner's to revise)

- **Who she is in the trial:** Aotsuki is the trial's rare event wearing a fighter's shape — a gentle, enormous presence who only fully arrives under the Palace of Dreaming's water-moon, drawn by the same sea-soul weather that moves KAI's two moods. Where King Tyrant is the tide performing and Star is the current flowing, Aotsuki is the *moon over both* — not a third splinter of KAI (the record locks KAI at two moods) but the thing their rhythm answers to. She came the ordinary mortal way and stayed strange: the lands' animals go quiet when she passes, the way forests do for weather.
- **Temperament:** unhurried to the point of comedy in a tournament of killers. She fights like something that has never needed to hurry — immense, deliberate, kind in the way gravity is kind: it holds you, and it will not catch you. Her gentleness is real and so is the record of it: fighters who underestimated the blue moon's patience have described the loss as "being set down by the sky."
- **Relationships (written):** Star feels her before anyone — the current knows when the moon is up, and Star's never-ending motion eases, just slightly, in her presence (the drummers hate it). King Tyrant respects her as the only audience member who has never once applauded — and performs, privately, a little *for* her. Gashadokuro (written) rattles at her and she is the only one who answers bone with courtesy — two remainders of different kinds, hers chosen, his not. Nightwolf walks with her between rounds sometimes; neither explains it.
- **Branch thread — Once in a Blue Moon:** in Story mode, Aotsuki's route isn't a climb so much as a *season*. Her rung events trigger on return visits — die once in a land and come back, and her branch marks it; die a third time in the same land and her shrine scene plays: she asks the run's fighter the only question she ever asks — "again?" — and the answer (yes: a force traded upward; no: the run rests, healed, one rung back) is the branch. She is the trial's mercy mechanic wearing a moon.
