# ZUI — Canon

## Identity

**ZUI — The Drunken Soul.** Vessel of **BYOU** (病 — sickness), one of The Four. His name is from 醉 (zuì) — "drunk"; 醉拳 (zuìquán) is "drunken fist."

Zui was a monk of the Central Mountain — real robes, real vows, real discipline. Then he found the bottle, and the bottle was more honest than the monastery. Now he's a hedonist with a monk's footwork and a drunk's philosophy. He is the **only vessel who agrees with his soul**: everyone else wrestles theirs; Zui bought his a round. That is why he's unreadable.

Name hygiene (owner-resolved): there is no character named "Zooey" — that was a speech-to-text error for Zui. Steve Fox is his base body only (transitional scaffolding), not his identity; BYOU's vessel is Zui, and earlier placements of BYOU's moves on Rave/Kiko Tanaka are superseded.

## Appearance / model

The design is the owner's own idea: **Sagat × Chinese mountain monk × drunken master**. Owner-locked look: bald monk head, saffron robe, indigo pants, prayer beads, purple sash. The sake bottle/gourd never leaves his hand. His sway is supernatural — opponents swear he dodges punches he couldn't have seen.

His body is a sketchbook: trippy multicolor tattoos, randomly placed, no plan — every one a temple he burned or a night he survived. One blank patch on his **left shoulder blade**: he says it's for the night he hasn't survived yet.

Attires: Attire 1 = the Drunken Monk Tripo (owner's own file); Attire 2 = rework v2; Attire 3+ = other Steve Fox attires; any T3 legacy goes last.

## Story

He believes the monastery sent him — that the monks who disowned him arranged the trial as a final lesson, a koan with fists: "Go and be drunk among the dead. Learn." He toasts them before every fight: **"Still learning, you bastards."** The monks didn't send him. He's being purified not of the bottle but of the *running* — the trial is teaching him he can stumble beautifully *toward* something. He's starting to stumble toward Waterfall.

- **Pantera:** finds him exhausting. Has never said so.

- **Michiko:** she drinks with him without pity — she recognizes a fellow runner.
- **The Central Mountain monks:** disowned him. He toasts them anyway.

## Moves / base

Base: **Steve Fox** — pure boxing, the purest hands in the trial — **plus Drunken Fist taken from Lei Wulong's moves**, with working stance switching (boxing ↔ Steve's other stances ↔ Drunken Fist) on Tekken-style touch inputs. Fighting philosophy: "The straight line is a lie. Everything stumbles."

**Element:** Water — the Palace of Dreaming.
