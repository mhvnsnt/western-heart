# GYOU — Canon

## Identity
**GYOU (行)** — one of the Nine Souls. Motion itself. Cannot stop moving.
**Title:** Flame Lord (Flaming Angel)

## Vessel / Fighter Structure

Gyou is the spirit floating behind — stand-style. The **spirit body is the fighter** (the playable character). Gyou's spirit floats behind the spirit body the way Zen floats behind his, but Gyou moves around **more aggressively and sporadically** than Zen does.

## Flame Emperor — Oversoul Transformation

The Flame Emperor mode (spirit body's level 1 transformation) is the transformation that **begins the oversoul state**.

**Key difference from Zen:** Zen's spirit face takes over his spirit body's head. Gyou does NOT do that. Instead, Gyou **becomes the flames engulfing the spirit body** — the armor, the cape, the flames themselves. Gyou doesn't replace the head; he becomes the fire surrounding the fighter.

## Guardian of the Flame — Spirit Body Transformation

Switchable with **God of the Flame** — the player can toggle between the two forms.

- **Higher defense** — the tanky form (God of the Flame is the offense form)
- **Knight-like:** face shield, helm, face plate
- **0.5x bigger than God of the Flame**

## The Flame Eternal — Susanoo-Type Giant Form

The Susanoo-type GIANT form. Gyou's **biggest size**.

- **Gyou's face becomes VISIBLE on the oversoul** — the first time his face appears (contrast: in Flame Emperor/God of the Flame, Gyou is only the engulfing flames, never a face)
- **Giant 5x oversized hands, 7 fingers on each hand**
- **Flaming wings**
- A giant flaming Susanoo beast
