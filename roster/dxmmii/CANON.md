# DXMMII — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** DXMMII — The Rebuilt Soul. **Not one of the nine. The remix.** Name locked 2026-10-15. Pronoun rule (owner 2026-10-18): DXMMII is **it/its** until the owner decides otherwise.

**Backstory.** Mokujin was a wooden dummy that learned to fight by copying everyone it faced — no style of its own, every style at once. DXMMII is what happened when the dummy kept the copies and started *mixing* them. Patchwork body — different colored panels stitched together, each one a fight it survived, a night it played. X-shaped eyes like a dead cartoon character. Tongue hanging out. The crash-test-dummy target circle on its chest: aim here, it says. Everyone does. Nobody's collected. It's a DJ now. The trial is the biggest venue it's ever played, and it plays the crowd the way it fights — everybody's style, its beat.

**Personality.** Chaos with a tempo. Plays the room. Remembers every sample, forgets every name.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Everybody's style is my style."

**The four-arm system (owner-locked chat-canon, production conversations 2026-10).** DXMMII's arms are retractable into the body — nobody knows if it's a robot or a cyborg or an android or just a DJ in a robotic patchwork crash-test-dummy costume, and that mystery is the character: it is NEVER to be answered. The arms switch stances like Tekken stance fighters: four arms OUT is gyro brawler, arms IN is human Tekken style, arms FLOATING detached is its own thing — same inputs as the Tekken stance-switch fighters. Canon precedent: Bannon canonically had Goro from Mortal Kombat — four arms and all — with Goro rig files to study. The limb-system build is on main at `tools/limb-system/dxmmii/`.

**Relationships.**
- **SAM-UR-A1:** Fellow built thing. SAM-UR-A1 studies its patchwork like a manual. DXMMII has started leaving new patches where SAM-UR-A1 can find them. Neither calls it friendship.
- **Doc Frank:** Wants to "fix" it — proper panels, clean seams, *improvements*. DXMMII has hidden his toolbox three times. The patches stay crooked. The patches are the point.
- **Saku Ra:** The idol recognizes a performer. They've discussed a collaboration. The trial is not ready.

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace. A thing assembled from want, at home in the house of desire. The Destruction room keeps breaking it. It keeps coming back. The room is starting to take it personally.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the patchwork — stitched panels, X-eyes, the tongue, the target. Every rebuild left a seam. MIND: the DJ — reads the crowd, rides the beat, never plays the same set twice. SOUL: not one of the nine. A *copy* that became an original — the mimic soul. The trial keeps asking what it is underneath the patches. It's starting to wonder if there's an underneath. There's a beat, though. The beat is its.

**Why it thinks it's here.** It believes the trial booked it — biggest venue, longest set, crowd that literally *dies* for the music. The idea that it's here to be purified has never occurred to it. Purification doesn't have a dance floor.

**Being purified of:** The copy. Every patch is someone else's style. The trial keeps stripping the patches away — not the panels, the *habit* — asking what's left when it's not imitating anyone. What's left has a beat. It's its. It's learning to hear it.

**Signature detail.** Its X-eyes pulse with the beat — whatever it's playing, whatever it's fighting. When the music stops, the tongue goes back in. The other fighters have learned: tongue out, you're entertainment. Tongue in, run.

**Owner-locked:** Name DXMMII (locked 2026-10-15). Patchwork X-eyed tongue-out crash-test-dummy DJ, multi-colored panels, crash-test symbol on chest. Mokujin base (scaffolding only). Attire 1: default patchwork DJ look; T3 Mokujin legacy at the back of the list (per `roster/dxmmii/CONCEPT.md`).
