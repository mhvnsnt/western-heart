# PHANTOM — Canon

## Identity

**PHANTOM — The Ghost Blade Soul.** Vessel of **JIN** (the blade), one of the core Seven. He **may already be dead** — kept ambiguous on purpose, in the record and in-fiction. He belongs to the **Helix Palace (Metal)**. The **Phantom Marketplace** is named after him. He won't say why.

## Appearance / model — and the face question

Owner spec (2026-10-16): an **oni face mixed with the Fiend**, **Japanese curved top-and-bottom canine teeth (fangs, always showing)**, **long white hair past the waist** like Jiraiya. Owner Tripo on record: white spiky/wild Jiraiya mane, oni-like face, ornate dark armor with red/gold, swords on the back. His current live portrait is **owner-approved and stays as-is**.

**The face ruling (owner, 2026-10-19) — this governs:** nobody has seen his face, and nobody is sure there IS a face. What looks like a face could be a **mask** (Fiend / oni-yokai style). Under the mask would be the **demon the mask depicts, unskinned** — like Kakashi, like ANBU, like the samurai-yokai: a mask over a demon/oni face, where taking it off would be like tearing skin off.

Open conflict (harvest C2, owner's call): an earlier delivered bio described a specific face in detail (broad jaw, heavy brow, skin like old bone). The owner ruled those specifics were the **AI's interpretation of his words, NOT his verbatim canon**. The fangs, the white hair, and the oni × Fiend conception stand as owner spec; the jaw/brow/bone-skin specifics do **not**. The mystery stands.

## The three men in him

- **Public: Jiraiya** — loud, laughing, pervy-sage energy, the big performance.
- **Private: Kakashi** — quiet, reads, watches; files everything under *seen*.
- **The third one: the Fiend × Mankind inside him** — uninvited and uncontrollable; it possesses him sometimes and he is pissed about it. It talks like Ninja Ninja mixed with the Hormone Monster, and it watches the dying the way a shinigami watches.

**Signature:** exactly **3 seconds of silence after a kill** — then the laughing starts again. Or it doesn't.

## Story

He walks between the five lands like he belongs to all of them; doors open for him that don't open for others; he has never explained how he crosses. He believes he **volunteered** for the trial — that somewhere there's a contract with his name on it, signed in his hand, that he doesn't remember signing. He is being purified of **watching** — the sin of seeing everything and touching nothing. (Repository canon.)

**Element:** Metal — the Helix Palace.
