# DEMONIO — Canon

## Identity

**DEMONIO — The Demon Soul.** Vessel of **RIN** (凛 — dignified, cold, self-possessed), one of The Seven. RIN is the protagonist soul — the baseline every other soul deviates from. Named demon, fights like a saint: the soul called Demonio is the most disciplined in the trial. He bows before every fight. He remembers a code he cannot name, from a life he cannot recall.

Naming rule (owner): the playable character is **never called Jin**. "Jin" survives only as the Eastern Mind soul name; every Tekken-side reference was renamed to Demonio.

## Appearance / model — three forms

Demonio has **3 forms** (owner canon):

1. **Unwinged demon** — the base form (Attire 1), normal Demonio moveset.
2. **Winged devil** — the transformation UP, mid-match: winged, with demon bat hands bursting from his palms/wrists (3-fingered extra bat hands per palm).
3. **Human** — the rare transformation DOWN from the base demon form: a human with a demon face on his chest/torso possessing him. Also selectable as a starting form.

Transformation sequence (owner canon): purple haze → chest/arm markings form → wings BURST from the back → bat hands BURST from the wrists → muscle expansion → shockwave. Personalized, Devil Jin-style — it reflects who he is.

## Story

He believes he was summoned by a master — some teacher, some authority that recognized his discipline and set him a final test. He treats the trial like a grading exam. He has never considered that there is no master, only a dying head. He is being purified of **the apology**: his mind keeps bowing, keeps atoning for a sin he can't name. When the guilt is burned out of him, he'll just be RIN — cold, dignified, complete.

Signature detail: he bows to every opponent. He has **never once bowed to Diablo**. Everyone has noticed. No one has mentioned it.

## Connections

- **Diablo:** his mirror and his enemy — the true demon versus the pretender. This is the rivalry the trial was built for. Each believes the other is the pretender.
- **Pantera:** the only soul he respects without reservation.
- **Saku Ra:** he is protective of her. Would not explain why.
- **Nightwolf:** mutual respect of the disciplined — she is the only one he bows *to*.

## Moves / base

Base: **Jin Kazama** — Mishima-style karate, precise and fundamental. Winged form: **Devil Jin** moveset base. Fighting philosophy: "A demon with discipline is more dangerous than a saint with rage."

Record correction (owner ruling, 2026-10-15): the Steve Fox-bodied Demonio seen in early builds was a stand-in and **wrong canon** — Demonio must be a real Jin model. The Steve Fox body belongs to Zui.

**Element:** Earth — the Octagonal Shrine, the Source.
