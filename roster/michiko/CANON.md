## Story

She's a mortal who heard the call and came — but she won't climb. She wanders the five lands like she wandered her own life, free and unclaimed, and the trial lets her because it can't decide where she'd land. She is being purified of **running** — not of freedom, of *flight*. One day she'll stand still for something. The trial is waiting to see what. (Repository canon, owner-directed expansion.)
