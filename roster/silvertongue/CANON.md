# SILVERTONGUE — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** SILVERTONGUE — The Heiress Soul. **Not one of the nine. The tourist.** Her frame is Lili's Tekken 7 frame, re-owned and reskinned as Silvertongue — her proprietary Eastern Mind/Western Heart name; the old name is retired (see `roster/lili/CANON.md`, obsolete notice).

**Backstory.** Emilie De Rochefort — Monaco heiress, the Rochefort fortune's only daughter, the girl who treated the King of Iron Fist Tournament like a debutante ball and nearly won it. In the Western Heart, she is exactly what she appears to be: a rich girl who wandered into a trial for lost souls because the invitation looked *exclusive*. She was never called. She *came*. Tong-Nou didn't summon her; she followed the pull the way she follows everything interesting — because it was there and she was bored. Boredom is the one opponent Emilie De Rochefort has never beaten.

**Personality.** Effervescent. Spoiled. Genuinely kind in the way only people who've never needed anything can afford to be. Treats the trial like a particularly long party.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Elegance is a weapon. So is money. I brought both."

**Relationships.**
- **Kinpo:** She wears the face of Silvertongue's oldest rival (the record's phrasing — the Asuka-lineage rivalry the source scaffolding carries). Silvertongue has tried to befriend her eleven times. Kinpo has declined eleven times. Silvertongue considers this a rivalry. Kinpo considers this a Tuesday.
- **Purple Rain:** Fellow showmen. They have a standing bet on who bows lower after a win. The bows keep getting lower. Someone will get hurt.
- **Zui (the boxer):** She adores him — the boxer, the charm, the *story* (recorded under the boxer's source name; in this universe the boxer is Zui before the monastery — see `roster/zui/CANON.md`). He finds her exhausting and hasn't told her. She knows. She doesn't mind.

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace. Wealth is the Greed room's native language, and Silvertongue is fluent.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Silvertongue's acrobatic elegance — the cartwheels, the impossible angles, the fight as dance recital. MIND: the tourist — everything here is *fascinating*, nothing here is *real*, and that is the most dangerous thing about her. SOUL: not one of the nine, not claimed by any room. A soul that wandered in off the street and stayed for the show. The trial doesn't know what to do with her. Neither does she. That's why she's winning.

**Why she thinks she's here.** She believes the trial is the most exclusive tournament in existence and that she was *selected* — scouted, the way the good schools scout. The idea that Tong-Nou is a dying god trying to heal itself would bore her. So she keeps the story. The trial, for once, is the one being entertained.

**Being purified of:** The game of it. Silvertongue treats lives like amusements — not cruelly, just carelessly. The trial keeps putting her across from souls who are *drowning*. She's starting to notice. It annoys her. That's the purification working.

**Signature detail.** She curtsies before every match — deep, perfect, infuriating. After a win, she blows a kiss. Fighters who've taken the kiss say it lands like a slap.

**Owner-locked:** Silvertongue is her proprietary name; the source name is retired in this universe (owner canon law, 2026-10-23: source originals get no canon — see `roster/lili/CANON.md`).
