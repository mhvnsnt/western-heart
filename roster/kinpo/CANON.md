# KINPO — Canon

## Identity

**KINPO — The Buttercup Soul.** The **thorn-splinter of SHA** — the sand soul split three ways: **Saku Ra is the bloom, Kinpo is the thorn, Awa Pop is the bubble.** Her name is from **金鳳花 (kinpōge — buttercup)**. She is the **Buttercup** (green) of the PPG-style trio, to Saku Ra's Blossom. ("Kempo" in speech transcripts = Kinpo.)

## Personality

**Blunt, loyal, allergic to sentiment.** Fighting philosophy: **"Blossom leads. Buttercup finishes."** The owner found base Asuka "a little boring" as-is; the fit is that Asuka's aggressive Kazama style *is* Buttercup. Signature detail: she is the **only fighter who visits the losers after matches** — brings them water, doesn't talk, denies it if asked.

## Story

She believes she was **dragged in as Saku Ra's plus-one** — that the trial wanted the Desert Soul and she came as packaging. She's wrong: SHA split because the trial needed all three of them, and the thorn was never the accessory. She is being purified of **the hardness** — not the strength, the *armor*; the trial is teaching her that the thorn protects the flower, and she is not only the thorn. (Repository canon.)
