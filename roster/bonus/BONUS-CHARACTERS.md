# Western Heart — Bonus Characters (Tekken 7 rips)
Owner-directed 2026-10-12. These are outside the core 7 but approved for reskinning.

## ZUI (Steve Fox base)
- **Direction:** Drunken master boxer. Ex-monk who left the monastery, became a hedonist.
- **Visual:** Sagat (Street Fighter) meets Chinese mountain monk meets drunken master.
- **Key features:** Sake bottle in hand, supernatural sway, trippy multicolor tattoos
  all over body like a sketchbook — randomly placed, different colors.
- **Name origin:** 醉 (zuì) = "drunk" in Chinese. 醉拳 (zuìquán) = "drunken fist."
- **Customization notes:** Steve is shirtless boxer base — good canvas for tattoos (texture work).
  Monk pants to replace boxing shorts. Prayer beads accessory. Sake bottle prop (geometry add).
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — drunken monk archetype fits the five lands perfectly.

## KINPO (Asuka Kazama base)
- **Direction:** Buttercup (green Powerpuff Girl) to pair with Saku Ra.
- **Name origin:** 金鳳花 (kinpōge) = "buttercup" flower in Japanese. Shortened to Kinpo.
- **Dynamic:** If Saku Ra is Blossom (leader), Kinpo is Buttercup (the tough one).
- **Style fit:** Asuka's kazama martial arts is already aggressive, no-nonsense — IS Buttercup.

## WATERFALL (Josie Rizal base)
- **Base character:** 20, Filipina, office worker, does kickboxing to support family.
  Painfully shy, apologizes constantly, cries when nervous, cries when she wins, cries when she loses.
  Aggressive fighter, anxious personality — the contrast is the character.
- **Western Heart direction:** Her tears become lore — she cries because she feels the nine souls.
  Spirit medium / soul-bearer concept. Emotional gimmick turned into Eastern Mind mythology.
- **Name:** Waterfall (owner-named 2026-10-12 — tears like a waterfall, fits the soul-bearer concept)

## KING TYRANT (Eddy Gordo base)
- **Direction:** Booker T style wrestler. Keep the capoeira movement, reskin as wrestling showman.
- **Name:** King Tyrant (owner-named 2026-10-12, from Booker's "King Booker" gimmick)
- **Notes:** Easy reskin — Eddy already has flashy showman movement. Straightforward.

## BURGER (Bob Richards base, Tekken 6)
- **Direction:** Gluttonous, fast, insatiable. Always hungry, never full. Impossibly fat yet impossibly quick.
- **Name:** BURGER (owner-named 2026-10-12)
- **Curse:** His hunger is a curse — a gluttonous transformation. The fat form is the cursed form.
- **Uncursed attire:** Slim Bob (Tekken Tag 2 — not a separate character in Western Heart) — the uncursed form, freed from hunger. Available as an alternate attire, not a roster slot.
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — a soul consumed by appetite, one of the hungriest ghosts. The curse/uncurse dynamic mirrors the soul purification theme.

## AWA POP (Nina Williams base)
- **Direction:** Bubbles (blue Powerpuff Girl) to complete the trio with Saku Ra (Blossom) and Kinpo (Buttercup).
- **Name origin:** 泡 (awa) = "bubble" in Japanese. Last name "Pop" — Awa Pop.
- **Dynamic:** Saku Ra is Blossom (leader), Kinpo is Buttercup (tough one), Awa Pop is Bubbles (the sweet one). K-pop idol aesthetic like Saku Ra.
- **Style fit:** Nina's assassin grace + Aikido translates to Bubbles' deceptive sweetness — cute but deadly.
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — completes the PPG trio as soul vessels.

## SCIMITAR (Shaheen base, Tekken 7)
- **Direction:** Saudi Arabian fighter who wields a scimitar. Military precision meets desert warrior.
- **Name:** SCIMITAR (owner-named 2026-10-13 — named for his weapon)
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — the blade from the desert lands. Pairs with Phantom (sword) as the two blade fighters.
