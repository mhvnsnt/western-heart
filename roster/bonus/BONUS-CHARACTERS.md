# Western Heart — Bonus Characters (Tekken 7 rips)
Owner-directed 2026-10-12. These are outside the core 7 but approved for reskinning.

## ZUI (Steve Fox base)
- **Direction:** Drunken master boxer. Ex-monk who left the monastery, became a hedonist.
- **Visual:** Sagat (Street Fighter) meets Chinese mountain monk meets drunken master.
- **Key features:** Sake bottle in hand, supernatural sway, trippy multicolor tattoos
  all over body like a sketchbook — randomly placed, different colors.
- **Name origin:** 醉 (zuì) = "drunk" in Chinese. 醉拳 (zuìquán) = "drunken fist."
- **Customization notes:** Steve is shirtless boxer base — good canvas for tattoos (texture work).
  Monk pants to replace boxing shorts. Prayer beads accessory. Sake bottle prop (geometry add).
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — drunken monk archetype fits the five lands perfectly.

## KINPO (Asuka Kazama base)
- **Direction:** Buttercup (green Powerpuff Girl) to pair with Saku Ra.
- **Name origin:** 金鳳花 (kinpōge) = "buttercup" flower in Japanese. Shortened to Kinpo.
- **Dynamic:** If Saku Ra is Blossom (leader), Kinpo is Buttercup (the tough one).
- **Style fit:** Asuka's kazama martial arts is already aggressive, no-nonsense — IS Buttercup.

## WATERFALL (Josie Rizal base)
- **Base character:** 20, Filipina, office worker, does kickboxing to support family.
  Painfully shy, apologizes constantly, cries when nervous, cries when she wins, cries when she loses.
  Aggressive fighter, anxious personality — the contrast is the character.
- **Western Heart direction:** Her tears become lore — she cries because she feels the nine souls.
  Spirit medium / soul-bearer concept. Emotional gimmick turned into Eastern Mind mythology.
- **Name:** Waterfall (owner-named 2026-10-12 — tears like a waterfall, fits the soul-bearer concept)

## THOR (Ganryu base, Tekken 7)
- **Direction:** The literal Norse god of thunder. Not inspired by Thor, not a tribute to Thor — IS Thor. Ganryu's sumo frame is the mountain made flesh, and every sumo slap (harite) lands as a thunder clap. The sky answers him because it has to. But the afterlives grew thin one after another — Nirvana's stillness grew hollow, Valhalla's halls grew quiet, Niflheim's mists grew thin — none of them were enough for him. In Nirvana he found the extinguishing of all striving, and the thunder in him refused to be extinguished. In Valhalla he fought every fight they offered, drunk every horn they poured, and still the thunder in him asked for more. In Niflheim he walked the mist world of ice and fog, the cold realm that swallows the unworthy — and Niflheim opened its mouth to swallow him. So Thor swallowed the thunder first. He opened his own mouth and took the storm whole, the mist world's ice and fog and cold silence, and carried it down inside him. He swallowed the thunder itself. Then he heard it: 不死の戦い — the immortal battle. A fight that never ends. A god of thunder hears those words the way a starving man hears a dinner bell.
- **Name:** THOR (owner-named 2026-10-13)
- **Body / Mind / Soul:** BODY — Ganryu's sumo mass, the heaviest honest frame in the trial; every open-hand slap is a thunder clap — and not metaphorically, because he swallowed Niflheim's storm whole and it's still inside him. The arenas of the Western Heart have learned to flinch at the wind-up. MIND — the god's certainty; he doesn't argue, he declares, and arguments end when he speaks. SOUL — not one of the nine. When Thor stepped to the shrine stones, they went silent. Gods don't carry souls; they are what souls wish they were. The only answer came from the two souls that refused vessels — ZEN and GYOU — and they answered with silence too. Something is waiting.
- **Element:** None of the five lands claim him. The trial tried to seat him in Metal — thunder is the hammer's ring, the forge's voice — but the storm is his land, and it follows him between arenas. Where Thor stands, the weather is Thor.
- **Why Thor thinks he's here:** Nirvana was not enough. Valhalla was not enough. Niflheim was not enough. Nirvana offered the extinguishing of all striving, and the thunder in him refused to be extinguished. Valhalla offered every fight the Norse afterlives had, and the thunder in him still asked for more. Niflheim offered the mist world's cold swallowing silence — and the mist world tried to swallow him, so he swallowed the thunder first, and carried Niflheim's storm out of the mist in his own body — so when he heard of 不死の戦い, the immortal battle, he followed it here. Crackling with swallowed thunder, he said the words he'd once overheard a small, round, impossibly hungry sun speak — "Immortal Qombat?" — and went there. He believes he's climbing to fight whatever sits at the top of the Central Mountain. He has no idea the top is a shrine, the fight is purification, and the "opponent" is himself. He thinks the trial is a tournament. It is not. And somewhere along the way he overheard a small, round, impossibly hungry sun say the words "Immortal Qombat" — and he has been saying it back ever since, always as a question, always slightly wrong in a way that makes it funnier each time: "Immortal Qombat?" He doesn't know what it means. He heard it once. He likes the sound of it.
- **Relationships:**
  - **BURGER:** The only fighter who can match Thor's appetite — and the only one Thor has ever lost an eating contest to (or so Burger claims; Thor claims the horn was rigged). Sumo brothers. Two mountains that decided to be friends instead of a landslide.
  - **DIABLO:** Two uninvited kings. Diablo tore his way in to rule the trial; Thor walked in because he heard fighting and assumed he was invited. Diablo demands Thor kneel. Thor doesn't understand the sentence. Their fights are the trial's earthquakes.
  - **SKULL:** The violence god and the thunder god. Skull's RETSU is force from below; Thor's thunder is force from above. They understand each other completely and have nothing to say about it.
  - **KING TYRANT:** The showman of the sea knows the storm always wins — and performs anyway. Thor respects that. It reminds him of mortals.
  - **ZUI:** The only vessel who ever offered Thor a drink and meant it as an equal. Drinking companions — the monk who left the monastery and the god who never needed one.
  - **SUN RĀ:** Thor heard the soul-eating sun say "Immortal Qombat" exactly once — and has been repeating it back as a question ever since, comically, like a man trying out a foreign phrase he doesn't understand. "Immortal Qombat?" Sun Rā, who permits no one else to speak his English words, finds the god of thunder's mangled echo... amusing. Or insulting. The sun has not decided. Thor has not noticed.
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — the god who wandered into a trial for lost souls and broke its seating chart. The trial purifies souls. Thor doesn't have one to purify. That should be a problem. It isn't, yet.

## YUKIHYŌ (Armor King base, Tekken 8)
- **Direction:** The snow leopard masked ninja. A silent snow-leopard-masked ninja — based on G.I. Joe's Snake Eyes (the black ninja) but more based on Storm Shadow (the white ninja, Snake Eyes' rival) — wearing the snow leopard as his face.
- **Name:** YUKIHYŌ (owner-named 2026-10-13 — ユキヒョウ, "snow leopard" in Japanese, spelled in English as Yukihyō)
- **Personality:** Storm Shadow's white-ninja precision with Snake Eyes' silence — says nothing, strikes from the whiteout. The snow leopard mask is his face; what's underneath is nobody's business.
- **Visual:** Snow leopard mask/head. Black/white colorway — striped/spotted digital camo in white across the attires. The ghost of the mountains to Pantera's shadow of the jungle.
- **Dynamic:** Pairs with Pantera as the two big-cat wrestlers — Pantera is the black panther (night, melanistic rosettes), Yukihyō is the snow leopard (white, black rosettes on pale fur). Same wrestling soul, opposite coats.
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — the mountain soul. The snow leopard stalks the high cold places between the five lands.

## SLICE (Dragunov base, Tekken 7)
- **Direction:** The maniacal secret agent. Dragunov's cold, methodical sambo-striking frame twisted into something unhinged — a blade that walks like a man.
- **Name:** SLICE (owner-named 2026-10-13)
- **Personality:** Maniacal secret agent who slices up his enemies with his blade — chops and strikes, surgical and savage at once. The mission is the excuse; the cutting is the point.
- **Visual:** Usually wears a skull mask to hide his identity and his scars. Sometimes the mask comes off and his face is seen — scarred, smiling wrong. The mask is the professional; the face underneath is the maniac.
- **Universe fit:** Eastern Mind — the blade that cuts souls. Pairs with Phantom and Scimitar as the third blade fighter: Phantom is the supernatural teleporting blade, Scimitar is the scheming desert blade, Slice is the unhinged surgical blade.
