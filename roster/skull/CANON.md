# SKULL — Canon

## Identity

**SKULL — The Punisher Soul.** Vessel of **RETSU** (烈 — violence, intensity), one of The Seven. He doesn't fight for the katana. He fights because something needs punishing, and the trial provides targets.

## Appearance / model

Under the paint is **Devitt** — the Finn Bálor / Prince Devitt likeness: sharp-boned, cold-eyed, "the kind of handsome that looks like it was carved for wanted posters." Over it, the paint: a skull in the Punisher's geometry. Owner's Tripo Attire 1 (own-eyes verified from the owner's model): skull face paint/mask, skeleton ribcage body paint on a bare muscular torso, digital-camo cargo pants, black combat boots, fingerless gloves. **Keep Bryan's pants** (owner direction). Paint versions on record: yellow+black carnage-looking, blue+black, chromatic (Street Fighter-style alt colors), Punisher-looking venom type.

## The paint — current owner ruling

The paint is a **living symbiote-type thing** — like Venom, like Sasuke's curse mark (owner ruling, 2026-10-19, the latest word): it absorbs him, drips off him, comes apart from him. Nobody has ever seen him put it on, and nobody has seen him without it. When RETSU rides him, the paint **spreads** — it starts at the face and moves down the neck, across the chest, down the arms, until his whole upper body is marked by the end of the fight. (An earlier same-day description of the paint as a pre-fight ritual is superseded by the symbiote ruling; the harvest flagged the nuance as conflict C4, reconciled as: the paint spreads as RETSU rides him, and he has never been seen applying it.)

Mix: Prototype (the game) + Finn Bálor/Prince Devitt + Venom/Carnage symbiote.

## Story

He believes he was brought as an **executioner** — that something looked at the trial's roster of lost souls and decided they needed judging. He thinks the paint was his idea. He thinks the mission was his choice. He is being purified of **the mission**: RETSU is violence without morality, pure force, and his mind dressed it in punishment. He **counts** — after every fight he knows exactly how many bones broke, and he remembers the number of every man he ever fought. "That's the RETSU in him. Retribution keeps books."

Signature: he doesn't celebrate. When the round ends he stands over the spot where it ended and looks down at it, like he's making sure it stays down.

## Connections

- **Pantera:** mutual understanding. Both know violence as vocation, not temper.
- **Waterfall:** unsettled by her. She weeps for the people he's punished; he has started pulling his strikes. He hates that.
- **Kinpo:** respects her bluntness. The only soul he'll answer at length.
- **Thor:** listed among Thor's drinking-companion circle in Thor's record (the grouping in that entry is loose).

**Element:** Metal — the Helix Palace's Destruction room.
