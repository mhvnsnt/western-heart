# PANTERA — Canon

## Identity

**PANTERA — The Panther Soul.** Vessel of **TOU** (闘 — battle itself), one of The Seven. Named after the band Pantera (owner: "We can call King Pantera"). A soul that remembers nothing but the clinch — the grip, the hold, the throw. He does not fight for the katana; he fights because fighting is what he is. The trial didn't recruit him. It recognized him.

He speaks only in big-cat growls (King-style) — no real words — but his lines get captions. The captions transcribing his growls sometimes misspell other fighters' names (Zui becomes "Zooey").

## Appearance / model

Owner-locked: **black panther — NOT leopard, NOT jaguar.** Melanistic rosettes stay, rendered black-on-black, visible only when the light hits. Yellow eyes. Black tail that moves like a separate animal (one tail skeleton across all attires; even when he is perfectly still, the tail tip traces slow circles). Pro wrestling gear: purple tights, black elbow/knee pads and boots, red accents; the red **PANTERA** in metal lettering across the trunks; a tattoo-panther with red claw marks on the thigh.

No mouth/facial rig, ever — King's mouth is just open. His hands must be active, articulated, fully detailed like real Tekken models.

Attires (owner ladder rule, 2026-10-21 — supersedes the earlier reskin-first ladder): his **Tripo originals take slots 1–5**, Tekken reskins go to the back. Recorded forms include Attire 2 "Leo man" (Leomon become King/Pantera — full lion head and mane, PANTERA in purple on the trunks, furry leg warmers, paw hands/feet, tail with golden tuft) and the snow-leopard **Leopard King** Tripo, which is Pantera's (owner clarified: not King Tyrant's). **PANTXRΛ-88** is his cyborg form/name: robot panther head from Heavy Leomon, robot left arm on his actual left arm, Heavy Leomon robot right shin and foot — a humanoid cat-like robot cyborg.

## Story

TOU is already pure — battle without motive — so the trial doesn't purify Pantera; it *uses* him: he is the standard the others are measured against. Asked by SAM-UR-A1 why he's here, he said: "The mat is here. I am here." In one of Doc Frank's per-character story arcs, Doc Frank gets hold of Pantera and turns him into a panther cyborg, grafting robotic/weapon pieces from Heavy Leomon and other weaponed Leomon variants onto him (the PANTXRΛ-88 form).

## Connections

- **Demonio:** the only soul who meets him head-on, every time. Mutual respect, no warmth.
- **Diablo:** Pantera dismisses him as a pretender — "A devil who needs to announce himself."
- **SAM-UR-A1:** curious about him — a thing that cannot feel the hold; what does it fight for?
- **Yukihyō:** the two big-cat wrestlers — black panther vs snow leopard, opposite coats.
- **Zui:** Pantera finds him exhausting. Has never said so. The tail twitches when Zui talks.
- **Doc Frank:** the builder who turns him into a cyborg in his story arc (above).

## Moves / base

Base: **King** — Tekken 7 King is the primary base; Tekken 3 King is a retro alt only. King's pro wrestling grappling: chain throws, multi-part holds, Jaguar Step mixups. Fighting philosophy: "The hold is honest. Everything else is negotiation."

**Element:** Fire — the Plains of Chi-U.
