# PANTERA — Identity Document

## Base Character
- **Tekken counterpart:** King (Tekken 7 primary, Tekken 3 as retro alt)
- **Body donor:** King

## Name
- **Pantera** — named after the band Pantera

## Visual Identity

### Head
- Black panther head (NOT leopard, NOT jaguar)
- Yellow eyes
- **Spots:** Melanistic rosettes — dark spots on dark fur, visible only when light hits correctly. NOT painted over. The pattern is there, just black-on-black.

### Body
- Black panther tail
- Pro wrestling gear

### Attire Notes
- Starting from King's Tekken 7 default: blue tights, yellow trunks with KING logo, gold boots, arm bands, jaguar mask
- To be reskinned to Pantera's look (details TBD by owner)

## Fighting Style
- King's pro wrestling grappling
- Chain throws, multi-part holds, Jaguar Step mixups

## Universe
- Western Heart universe (not Tong-Nou, not Tekken)
