## Written 2026-10-23 — built on the recorded foundations (owner's to revise)

- **How a crew crosses:** Torch's recorded canon says the membrane only opens for love or a name spoken correctly. The Gang is the third case the trial doesn't advertise: a *family* — people bound tightly enough that the membrane counted them as one soul with nine faces. They arrived together, which is why they fight as attires and echoes inside other men's stories (Static in Realizzt's throat) more often than as themselves: the trial filed them as a unit and keeps trying, politely, to separate them into rungs. It hasn't managed. Whatever the Gang is in the head, it is the one faction the Reincarnation has never split across lives.
- **Their branch weight:** in Story mode, any fighter wearing a Gang robe into a rung fight (the faction attires) is not alone in the tunnel afterward — a second robe is simply *there*, the way family is. No mechanical sermon; the weave point is that the Gang is the game's standing counter-argument to its own thesis: the trial says every soul climbs alone; the Gang climbed in holding onto each other, and the mountain has not yet found the rule that makes them let go.
