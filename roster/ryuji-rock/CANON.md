# CORNERSTONE (ryuji-rock) — Canon

## Identity

**CORNERSTONE — The Obnoxious One** (bonus fighter). Internal roster id stays **`ryuji-rock`**. He is **Ryuji Yamazaki (King of Fighters) × The Rock in his early Attitude Era**: Yamazaki's design and menace with the Rock's showmanship on top. The Rock is his secondary inspiration — that is what separates him from the roster's other Yamazaki.

Record guard: `ryuji-rock` and `ryuji` are **two distinct fighters** — `ryuji` is the pure Yamazaki entry; CornerStone is the Yamazaki × Rock hybrid. Never merge them.

## Appearance / model

Design from the Yamazaki reference: **black shirt, blonde top hair**. Model: owner's Tripo 20c1fd2e-40d0-4500-9be6-50f6494ab5e1.

## Personality

Obnoxious like Ryuji — he sticks his tongue out at people. Yamazaki's stance, tongue-sticking-out taunts **and** attacks and antics carry over wholesale, with the Rock's electricity layered on.

## Moves / base

- **Base: Yamazaki** — his stance, taunts, moveset, and antics.
- **Signature: the uranage / ROCK BOTTOM** — the Rock's finisher, sourced from **King's version of the move in Tekken** + the **WWE All Stars** version + the Rock's own animations in **No Mercy / WrestleMania XIX**.
- Also on record: Serpent Slash, Sand Kick, Knife Slash, Bai Gaeshi, Bakudan Pachiki, Todome, Guillotine, Pit Viper, Drill; People's Elbow, Sharpshooter, Brahma Bull Lariat, Layin' the Smackdown; and King's jumping diving elbow.
- **Second inspiration: Younger Toguro's fingernail blast** (Yu Yu Hakusho) — recorded as IF a YYH game is in holdings; that source is a named gap, not yet in hand. The WWE All Stars teardown itself was queued/disk-gated at record time.
