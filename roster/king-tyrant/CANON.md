# KING TYRANT — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** KING TYRANT — The Showman Soul. **Vessel of KAI (海 — the sea)**, the tide-splinter of the sea soul. Name from Booker T's "King Booker" gimmick (owner-locked); Booker T style.

**Backstory.** Eddy Gordo's capoeira — rhythmic, ground-flowing, relentless pressure, the tide that doesn't stop — reskinned as a Booker T-style wrestling king: the showman as monarch. He didn't come to the trial to become whole. He came to be *watched* becoming whole. The sea doesn't stop, and neither does he. Every fight is a main event. Every round is a coronation.

**Personality.** Charisma as weather. Regal. Demands adoration — and earns it, which is the annoying part.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Can you dig THAT?"

**Relationships.**
- **Star:** Same soul (KAI), different moods. The tide and the current. He thinks she's his backup dancer. She lets him think it. They move together in the team rounds and clash constantly everywhere else.
- **The Phantom Marketplace:** His favorite stage. An audience of eyeball mountains. He plays to them shamelessly.
- **Diablo:** Two kings, one trial. They hate each other the way mirrors hate each other.
- **Saku Ra:** Thinks he's hilarious. He believes she's an audience. They're both right.
- **Purple Rain:** Showman rivalry. Two performers, one spotlight. Their feud is the trial's most entertaining sideshow.
- **Pablo (external — Bannon sub-universe):** The Showman Soul adores Pablo's entrances — the theater, the timing, the commitment. He has never asked about the dark paint. Showmen know not to ask about other showmen's paint.
- **El Toro de Oro (external — Bannon sub-universe):** Fellow showmen. An unspoken agreement about entrances; neither has said it aloud, both honor it.

**Element.** Water — the Palace of Dreaming. The tide performs; the palace is change.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Eddy's capoeira, the tide that doesn't stop (base scaffolding only). MIND: the showman-king — he doesn't just want to win, he wants to be *seen* winning, and he's honest about it. SOUL: KAI's tide-splinter. The friction: the sea doesn't perform. It just *is*. King Tyrant's mind has dressed the tide in a crown. The soul goes along — the tide doesn't care about crowns. It'll still drown you.

**Why he thinks he's here.** He believes he was *booked* — that the trial is a promotion, and he's the main event. In his story, there's a promoter, a contract, a payday. He keeps asking the Trader about the gate receipts. The Trader keeps selling him answers. The idea that there's no promoter, no contract, no payday — just a dying god — would end him. So the show goes on.

**Being purified of:** The audience. KAI is the sea — it doesn't need witnesses. King Tyrant's need to be watched is the splinter's wound: the tide, separated from the current, performing to prove it's still the ocean. The trial keeps emptying his arena. He keeps playing to the eyeball mountains. They're a good crowd.

**What he fights for (recorded):** to be watched becoming whole. The coronation requires an audience.

**Signature detail.** He has never fought without an entrance. Even in the training room, alone, he does the full coronation — the pose, the turn, the roar. Star once walked in on it. She didn't laugh. She's the only one who didn't.

**Owner-locked:** Booker T style. Name King Tyrant (from Booker's "King Booker" gimmick). Keep capoeira movement. Eddy Gordo base (scaffolding only). Straightforward reskin.
