# SCIMITAR — Canon

## Recorded canon

**Identity.** SCIMITAR — The Vizier Soul. **Born in the Phantom Marketplace. The Trader's vizier.** Named for his weapon (owner-locked). Scar × Jafar — scheming, manipulative, regal, sinister.

**Backstory.** Shaheen's military precision — the disciplined soldier's body, the desert warrior's economy — worn by a soul that was never lost, because it was never anywhere else. Scimitar was born in the Phantom Marketplace, under the Eyeball Mountains, to the sound of haggling. He is the Trader's vizier: the one who smiles while the deal closes around you. He doesn't want the katana to become whole. He wants it as *leverage*.

**Personality.** Regal. Sinister. Smiles with his mouth; his eyes smile only when he's already won.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Every fight is a negotiation. I always collect."

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace's Greed room is his true home, though he'd never admit the Palace owns him the way it owns Burger.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: Shaheen's soldier frame, precise and coiled (base scaffolding only). MIND: the marketplace — every interaction is a transaction, every kindness has a price. SOUL: marketplace-born, not trial-called. He has no fragmentation to heal. What he has is *appetite for position* — and the trial doesn't know what to do with a soul that isn't broken.

**Why he thinks he's here.** He doesn't think he was pulled in at all. He was *born* here — the Marketplace is his home, the trial is just the new management. In his story, he's the only one who isn't lost, which makes him the only one thinking clearly. He's right that he wasn't pulled in. He's wrong that he's thinking clearly. The Greed room has him. He'd kill anyone who said so.

**Being purified of:** The need to own everything. The trial is waiting. So is he.

**What he fights for (recorded):** leverage. The katana is worth more as a bargaining chip than a weapon.

**Relationships.**
- **The Trader:** His patron, his master, his mark. The vizier serves the merchant — and keeps a private ledger of the merchant's weaknesses.
- **Phantom:** The other blade. Professional rivalry: whose cut is cleaner, whose steel is truer. They've never fought. Both are waiting.
- **Diablo:** Fellow schemers. They recognize each other instantly and trust each other not at all.
- **Michiko:** She sees through him completely. He hates her for it. He can't stop watching her. (From her side: he tries to use her; she uses him back. It's a game. She's winning.)
- **Torch:** He wants to hire her. She's considering it — the money is good, the targets are interesting.
- **Cyborg X:** Wants to buy her from whoever built her. She is not for sale.

**Signature detail.** He keeps a ledger. Every fighter owes him something — a favor, a debt, a secret. The ledger is written in a hand no one else can read. (Recorded also: Scimitar was born in the marketplace, which the marketplace counts as its finest product and its worst mistake.)

**Owner-locked:** Name SCIMITAR (named for his weapon). Scar × Jafar — scheming, manipulative, regal, sinister. Saudi military precision × desert warrior. Shaheen base (scaffolding only).
