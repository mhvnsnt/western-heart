# TOBI — Canon

## Identity

**TOBI — The Escape Soul.** Rabbit courier. Fastest in the roster — not a boast, a job description.

She was carrying messages between the lands before the trial had a name for her. Long ears, long legs, longer routes. If something needs to be somewhere, Tobi has already been there twice.

## Story

She carries one letter she never delivered. It is still in the satchel. Her left hand never leaves the strap.

She tipped off Ryusan — the one delivery that mattered more than all the delivered ones. The warning went out the night the Jotei family came to collect, and it arrived before the collectors did. Ryusan has honored the debt ever since. Tobi insists there is no debt. Couriers deliver. That's all.

She heard the call the way every lost soul does — a pull toward the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang, toward the inscription in stone: *Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.* She read it the way she reads everything: as a delivery address. She came to see what was waiting at the end of the route.

## Personality

Fast, dry, gone. She speaks in short bursts, like she's being charged by the word — which, as a courier, she usually is. The humor is deadpan and constant; the sentimentality is buried under six layers of schedule. Nobody has ever seen her sleep. The satchel has never been seen open.

**Fighting philosophy:** *"Don't wait for it. Be already gone."*

## Body / Mind / Soul

- **BODY:** a courier's body — long legs, long ears, built for distance. Everything about her is aerodynamic, including the attitude.
- **MIND:** routes. Every room is a route, every opponent is a stop, every fight is a delivery. She has never been lost in her life and doesn't intend to start.
- **SOUL:** not one of the nine. A mortal soul that learned to outrun everything — except the one thing in the satchel.

## Element

**Water — the Palace of Dreaming.** Change is the only law there, and she is change with legs. (Claim Water: your movement quickens — she claimed it before the trial finished explaining the rules, and the shrine stones are still catching up.)

## Why she thinks she's here

She thinks she's here because the call looked like a delivery address. The trial thinks she's here to be purified of the withholding. Both might be right. She's in no hurry to find out — hurry is for people going *toward* things.

## Being purified of

The undelivered letter. Whatever is in it. Who it was for. Why she kept it. She has never said, and the trial — which purifies by fighting, dying, and returning — has not yet managed to make her sit still long enough to ask properly.

## Signature detail

Her left hand never leaves the satchel strap — not in intros, not in win poses, not mid-combo. Her left-side strikes land as shoulder-barges and elbow checks; the hand stays where it is. In her win pose she checks the strap first, then the opponent, in that order.

## Voice

Sparse, imagistic, Japanese-influenced. Courier shorthand. She talks like a telegram and fights like the reply.

- Intro: *"Don't wait for it."*
- Win, checking the strap: *"Delivered."*
- To Ryusan, the only soft line she has: *"The warning arrived. That's the whole story."*
- On the letter, if asked (don't ask): *"…withheld."*

## Connections

- **Ryusan:** the one she tipped off. The only debt anyone owes her, and the only one she refuses to collect. Ryusan would burn the world for her; Tobi would outrun the fire and pretend not to notice.
- **The Jotei family:** she carried warnings about them the way she carries everything — fast, and without reading. What she didn't read, Ryusan remembers for both of them. (Roster entry pending — intake canon 2026-10-22.)

## Moves / base (fighting style, in-universe)

Relentless kick pressure: strings that switch feet with nearly every hit, one-legged flamingo stances held mid-stride, running attacks that start before the opponent finishes blinking. She fights like a route — no wasted motion, no second visits. On top of the base kit: the courier's own moves — the Longleap, the satchel swing, the shoulder-barge she throws without ever letting go of the strap, and the one super she only spends at full meter, when she finally opens the satchel.
