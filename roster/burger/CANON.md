# BURGER — Canon

## Identity

**BURGER — The Hunger Soul.** Claimed by the **Appetite Room** of the Helix Palace. He is the appetite character — gluttonous, fast, insatiable, **always hungry, never full, very fat**.

He is a **shapeshifter cursed with INFINITE HUNGER and INFINITE STARVATION**: no matter how much he eats he never gets full, keeps eating, stays fat but also stays skinny. The fat form is his hunger/gluttony **curse**.

## Forms — the most transformations of any character

Burger has both **UPGRADE and DOWNGRADE** transformations, multi-stage like Frieza. The recorded chain: (possible) lower form → base/chill human → cursed human → **Getting Hungry** → **Feral Demonic Hunger** → **Speed Demon** → **The Mouth** → **Mini Tank** → more demon forms → **final form** (the biggest demon — Attire 1, a mix of ALL the smaller forms' abilities plus exclusives).

- **Attire 1 = Demon mode, possessed by greed** (owner Tripo b3eed68b).
- **Cursed human form** = as chill as he gets; faster but weaker; transforms FROM the Attire 1 demon mode.
- **Getting Hungry:** big eyes — next after human; stronger but slower. "Gets his eyes on you — sometimes his eyes are bigger than his stomach, sometimes the other way around."
- **Feral Demonic Hunger:** the fastest human form before full demon possession; fastest and most feral; the strongest HUMAN form (weaker than full demon).
- **Speed Demon:** next after Feral; the fastest mode (besides a smaller form he'll make later); the weakest DEMON form, still stronger than any human. Spikes pop out, track pants shred to a loincloth, tattered wristbands. Human-sized demon mode.
- **The Mouth:** second demon form — head overgrows the body, slows him, topples often, clumsy; strong bite + head/mouth attacks; weak normal attacks.
- **Mini Tank:** after The Mouth — big head but body more proportional and giant, short legs, not too fast, hell of a bite, less clumsy but still kind of clumsy / not the most accurate.
- Size variants on record include small golem size with a big head, and giant titan size.

**Slim Bob is NOT a separate character** — he is Burger's **uncursed attire**, the form freed from the hunger/gluttony curse. (Owner correction: Slim Bob's source game is Tekken Tag Tournament 2.)

Inspirations: Bob + **Youkai Kusaregedo** + Gollum/Smeagol + cursed demon yokai/oni + Attack on Titan-style transformation. Correction (owner-confirmed): Kusaregedo is from **SAMURAI SHODOWN V/VI**, NOT Onimusha — an older memory line naming Onimusha is superseded.

## Story

He believes he **ate his way in** — that somewhere in a feast there's a door and he found it by biting. Nobody corrected him. He might be right. He is the only soul who isn't lost; he's just hungry. (Repository canon.)

## Connections

- **Zui:** fellow hedonists of the body — they drink and eat together between matches. (repo canon)
- **Thor:** sumo brothers, eating-contest rivals.
- **Waterfall:** weeps for his hunger. He doesn't understand why. (repo canon)
- **The Trader:** his dealer — he trades things for food no one else can get. (repo canon)
