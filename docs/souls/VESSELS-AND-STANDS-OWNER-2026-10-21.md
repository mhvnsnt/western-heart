quoted. Where this file and older docs disagree, the newer owner word wins.

## The rule

> "the people like Byou and Zen and stuff like that they have a vessel ... when they have the vessel
> and they're attached, we need to select them and they're attached to the vessel, then it's going
> to be like a modified, like celestial version, like JoJo's Bizarre Adventure with a stand ... he
> doesn't have a body so he needs like a host person to exist and be like a stand too"

A soul of the nine has no body of its own. In a fight it exists through its **vessel** (the host
fighter's body fights) and shows itself as a **celestial stand** with the host.

## Unvesseled souls (ZEN, GYOU)

> "Follow canon, he is unvesselled and will need his own spirit body tripo model plus a stand
> version of himself behind it maybe a way how Sasuke and those guys get their spirit samurai guys
> ... mixed with a jojo stand and maybe some stuff from shaman king."

So an unvesseled soul fights in its own **spirit body** (a Tripo model) with a large spectral
avatar of itself behind it: Susanoo-scale (Naruto), stand behaviour (JoJo), spirit-possession
flavour (Shaman King's Over Soul).

- GYOU has a spirit-form Tripo model already (`roster/gyou/attires/spirit-form/model.glb`,
  key `gyou-spirit`; a fiery head, not a full body).
- ZEN has **no spirit-body model yet**. Gap, named for the owner; nothing is invented in its place.

## Related owner rule (same day): source characters become WH characters

> "It's like this for each character that comes from other games like Tekken, Dead or Alive,
> Street Fighter, Soul Calibur, Urban Reign ... their attire is going to ladder back but their name
> and their persona is going to become the ... Western Heart character"

Examples: Julia → Nightwolf, Heihachi → Diablo, Lei → Rave, Dragunov → Slice. The source fighter
does not exist separately any more; its look stays as a lower attire slot of the WH character.
