## 1. CORE AESTHETIC STATEMENT

**Western Heart looks like a sacred text that learned to fight.**

The visual identity is **psychedelic-ontological**: Buddhist and Hindu sacred
geometry (mandalas, yantras, thangka composition), rendered at cosmic scale
(nebulas, galaxies, fractal depth), filtered through the game's own cosmology
— Tong-Nou, the giant green head floating in darkness, containing five
elemental lands, nine souls, and a trial by combat.

**In one breath:** kaleidoscope symmetry + sacred geometry + color flames,
smoke, and clouds + deep-space cosmos + the specific weirdness of the
Tong-Nou universe (organic head-interior, soul-fruit, the Trader's liminal
bazaar, the Purifier's stillness).

### What it IS
- Luminous darkness — deep blacks and void tones with light *blooming* out of them
- Radial symmetry and geometric order containing chaotic energy
- Vivid, saturated color used with symbolic intent (see: thangka color law)
- Cosmic scale — everything feels larger than the frame
- Spiritual anatomy — bodies as vessels of light, energy, geometry
- East-meets-cosmos: temple iconography rendered as deep-space phenomena

### What it is NOT
- Grimdark (no desaturated brown-grey misery, no edgy-for-edgy's-sake)
- Generic fantasy (no Tolkien castles, no Warcraft shoulder pads)
- Cyberpunk neon-for-neon's-sake (neon must serve the sacred, not the street)
- Minimalist flat-design UI (the UI is *ornate* — it just stays readable)
- Photorealistic grit (stylization over realism, always)
- Cultural costume (Hindu/Buddhist forms are *logic*, not decoration — see §3)

## 3. SPIRITUAL-PSYCHEDELIC VISUAL LANGUAGE

### 3a. The Sri Yantra — the master diagram
The single most important geometric reference in this bible.

- **Structure:** Nine interlocking triangles (4 upward = Shiva/consciousness,
  5 downward = Shakti/energy) forming 43 smaller triangles around a central
  **bindu** (point of pure potential). Encircled by lotus-petal rings, enclosed
  in the **bhūpura** — a square with four gates opening to the cardinal directions.
- **Why it matters for WH:** It is simultaneously a map of the cosmos, a map of
  consciousness, and a meditation device. Our UI should *work* the same way:
  geometric frames that are also functional (health as yantra rings, meters as
  petal counts — see §6).
- **Direct applications:**
  - Character select background: a vast yantra with each fighter's portrait
    seated in one of the 43 chambers
  - Soul Surge effect: the yantra blooms outward from the fighter, bindu at
    the heart
  - Victory screen: the yantra completes itself (draw-on animation)

### 3b. Mandalas — impermanence engines
- Tibetan **sand mandalas**: built grain by grain, then *swept away*. The
  aesthetic lesson: intricate order that knows it is temporary.
- **Application:** Round-transition effects — a mandala assembles during
  "ROUND 1" and *dissolves* on KO. Stage backgrounds with mandala elements
  that slowly rotate and decay over the course of a match.
- Never static wallpaper. Mandalas in WH are *alive and dying*.

### 3c. Sacred geometry toolkit

|---|---|---|
| Flower of Life | Genesis pattern | Stage floor inlays, loading screens |
| Metatron's Cube | All platonic solids | Hit-effect impact frames |

### 3d. Thangka law — compositional canon
Tibetan thangka painting gives us hard compositional rules (researched from
monastic canon — these are *rules*, not suggestions):

1. **Hierarchical symmetry** — the most spiritually important figure is
   largest and centered. Retinue figures orbit in concentric rings, smaller
   with distance. → *Character select, versus screens, boss reveals all obey this.*
2. **Central deity + retinue rings** — every composition has a center that
   *holds* and rings that *serve*. → *HUD: fighter portrait central, meters as rings.*
3. **No empty space without intent** — every region carries symbol (clouds,
   flames, lotuses). Negative space is *ma* (see writing guide), not vacancy.
4. **Wrathful vs. peaceful palettes** — thangkas code deity nature in color:
   peaceful deities in cool luminous tones, wrathful deities in hot saturated
   reds/blacks with flame aureoles. → *Fighter visual coding: not good/evil,
   but nature (see soul signatures §5).*
5. **Flame aureoles (prabhamandala)** — the ring of fire behind wrathful
   deities. → *Direct reference for Rage/Soul-Surge auras: geometric flame
   rings, not blob glows.*

### 3e. Thangka color law (symbolic, not decorative)
From the researched canon — five root colors, each bound to meaning:
- **White** — peace, purity → *healing effects, shrine elements, Megami*
- **Blue** — wrath (in the transformative sense), vastness, Medicine Buddha →
  *water land, Pond, deep-space grounds*
- **Red** — power, transformation, energy → *fire land, hit sparks, Jotei*
- **Green** — activity, accomplishment → *wood land, Tong-Nou's own hue*
- **Yellow/Gold** — prosperity, earth, divine illumination → *UI accents,
  Trader gold, victory*

**Rule:** When we use these colors, we use them *in their symbolic register*.
Gold is never just "shiny" — it is illumination, price, the Trader's metal.

### 3f. Hindu deity iconography
- **Multi-armed forms** (Durga, Kali, Vishnu): each arm holds an attribute;
  multiplicity = multiplicity of power. → *Chatur's four arms are rendered in
  this lineage: each arm's gesture (mudra) distinct, each carrying symbolic
  weight — NOT body-horror, NOT sci-fi extra limbs.*
- **Cosmic scale** (Vishvarupa): the deity containing universes within the
  body. → *Tong-Nou itself is this image inverted: the universe containing
  the deity.*
- **Vivid saturation + dark grounds**: deity figures luminous against deep
  blue-black. → *Our default portrait lighting.*

### 3g. Fractal / kaleidoscope
- Self-similarity across scales (the part contains the whole) = the game's
  core ontology (souls are fragments of one being; the head contains the world).
- **Application:** Stage backgrounds with recursive detail (zoom reveals more
  structure, never pixelates into noise). Hit-stop frames with kaleidoscopic
  mirroring. The Reincarnation (death/rebirth) transition as a fractal bloom.

## 5. WESTERN HEART VISUAL MAPPING

### 5a. Tong-Nou — the head in darkness
- **The image:** A colossal green head, floating in absolute darkness. Not a
  planet, not a god — a *head*. The scale must disturb: too large for the
  frame, always partially out of view.
- **Rendering notes:**
  - Skin: deep jade/emerald (`#0E5B3E`–`#1A7A4F` range), subsurface scattering
    suggesting inner light — the lands *glow faintly through* the skin.
  - The darkness around it is not empty: faint nebula wisps, distant
    soul-lights (like plankton in a night ocean).
  - Veins of gold light trace the head's surface — the "veined" sky described
    in canon (fighters inside see the head's interior as their sky).
  - Eyes closed. Always closed. (If they open, that is a story event, not a
    background.)
- **Reference blend:** Grey's anatomical luminosity + deep-space photography
  (Hubble/ JWST nebula palettes) + thangka wrathful-deity scale.

## 6. UI/UX ART DIRECTION

### Design law
**The UI is sacred geometry that happens to be functional.** Every bar,
frame, and transition obeys thangka composition (hierarchical symmetry,
central focus, symbolic margins) AND passes the readability test (see below).

### Health bars
- **Form:** Not straight bars — **arc segments of a yantra ring** encircling
  each fighter's portrait at screen top. Health depletes as the arc shortens.
- **Why:** The ring is the soul's circle; damage visibly *breaks the circle*.
- **Readability guard:** Arc thickness ≥ 14px at 1080p; high-contrast
  soul-color fill on void-black ground; numeric HP available on toggle.
- **Chip damage / grey health:** rendered as *cracked* arc segments (kintsugi
  logic — the break is visible and golden).

### Meters (super/soul)
- **Form:** Lotus-petal count — a ring of petals around the portrait that
  *bloom* (fill with light) as meter builds. Full bloom = ready.
- **Soul Surge ready:** the bindu at the portrait's center ignites.

### Character select
- **Layout:** Thangka assembly — a vast Sri Yantra field; each fighter's
  portrait seated in a chamber. Cursor movement *illuminates* the geometric
  path between chambers.
- **Fighter card:** Portrait + name in two registers (WH name large;
  epithet — "The Panther Soul" — small, in muted gold). No stats clutter;
  details on focus-expand.
- **Background:** Slow-rotating mandala at 5% opacity + land-palette nebula.
  Never static. Never distracting.

### Versus screen
- **Form:** Vesica piscis — two circles (one per fighter) overlapping at
  center. The overlap region shows the stage sigil. Portraits face each other
  across the intersection.
- **Transition in:** The circles draw themselves (sacred-geometry draw-on).

### Hit effects (replacing the prototypes)
The user directive: Tekken's hit effects get pulled and *replaced* — but the
replacement must be WH-styled, not Tekken-cloned. Target vocabulary:
- **Impact frame:** Metatron's Cube flash — geometry blooms from the contact
  point for 2–3 frames, then collapses. Color = attacker's soul-color.
- **Sparks:** Not generic particles — *petal shards* (lotus geometry) and
  *ember glyphs* (tiny alchemical symbols, Malakor thread).
- **Heavy hits:** Brief yantra-ring expansion + screen-space radial symmetry
  pulse (kaleidoscope mirror for 4 frames).
- **KO:** The loser's soul-color mandala assembles and *shatters* (sand
  mandala logic — built to be broken). The winner's aureole ignites.
- **Block:** Concentric ripple rings (Dreaming-land logic), cyan-white.

### Round transitions
- "ROUND 1 / FIGHT" — mandala assembles from bindu outward as the announcer
  speaks; on FIGHT it *shatters outward* into the arena.
- "K.O." — time dilates; the impact geometry freezes mid-bloom; gold
  blackletter "K.O." (Malakor thread) stamps over.

### Victory screen
- Winner centered, thangka-hierarchical (largest figure). Yantra completes
  behind them (draw-on). Soul-color aureole. Name in large type, epithet in
  gold. The defeated fighter small, in the retinue ring — honored, not humiliated.

### Typography
- **Display/headings:** A serif with engraved/occult character (think
  Trajan meets grimoire). Blackletter unicode accents ONLY for: Trader,
  trial inscriptions, boss reveals, Purifier text (Malakor thread, §2).
- **Body/UI text:** High-legibility humanist sans. Sacred doesn't mean
  unreadable.
- **Numbers (timer, HP):** Tabular figures, generous tracking, always with
  dark outline or backing plate.

### Readability law (non-negotiable)
1. No critical information carried by color alone (pair with shape/position).
2. All text holds contrast over the brightest and darkest backgrounds
   (test both).
3. Effects never obscure fighters for more than 4 frames.
4. The UI must be legible to a colorblind player (simulate deuteranopia).

## 8. COMPOSITIONAL PRINCIPLES

1. **Radial default.** When in doubt, compose outward from a center
   (bindu logic). UI, effects, portraits, stages.
2. **Hierarchical scale.** Most important = largest = centered. (Thangka law.)
3. **Geometry contains chaos.** Wild energy (flames, smoke, nebula) always
   *inside* a geometric frame (ring, yantra, aureole). Order holding ecstasy.
4. **Darkness is a material.** The void is not empty background — it has
   depth, texture (nebula wisps, soul-motes), and *weight*.
5. **Light comes from within.** Fighters, sigils, UI elements are luminous
   *sources*, not lit objects. (Grey's law.)
6. **Symmetry with a break.** Perfect symmetry, then ONE deliberate
   asymmetry (a cracked petal, an off-center sigil) — the wabi-sabi gate
   that keeps compositions alive.
7. **Motion is sacred.** Nothing important is static: mandalas rotate,
   aureoles breathe, geometry draws itself on. Stillness is reserved for
   ZEN, the Shrine, and the moment before FIGHT.
