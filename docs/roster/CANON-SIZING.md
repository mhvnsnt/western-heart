# Canon Sizing

Owner rule (2026-10-23): every fighter and every version or transformation is sized from canon, bio and transformations, not one global height. Burger's default is Bob-sized (big and wide). Data: `game/data/canon-sizing.js`; runtime: `game/js/canon-sizing.js` (measures the in-fight stance height once per model and scales the root, so nothing is cut); test: `test/canon-sizing.test.mjs`. Calibration: Pantera (King body, 200 cm) at scale 1 is the reference. `applied = yes` means switched on in-game with proof; `no` means documented baseline awaiting its own proof PR.
