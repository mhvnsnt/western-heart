# IMPORTED CANON — Index (2026-10-23)

Owner instruction (2026-10-23): *"you gotta bring over any of the stories and bio for any of the characters that came from stuff outside of Western Heart like wizard gang and anybody else. They need their canon filled in from stuff outside of them. Like any of the off the top rope stuff or any of the bannon stuff. Any of the Ash Lane shit. Any of that."*

This index covers the 29 fighters whose canon files already live on western-heart main (bios batch 1 + the four pre-existing files). For every fighter with material in the owner's other projects, that material was harvested faithfully and appended to their `roster/<dir>/CANON.md` under a labeled `## Imported canon — from <project>` section, with per-item sources. Imported material is **recorded owner canon housed in other projects** — it is never labeled "Written," and no connective tissue was invented; the rare bridge line is marked as an editorial note inside the section.

Companion rules applied in this import (owner, 2026-10-23):
- **Obsolete-in-canon:** a source-game original whose identity/moveset was reassigned to a proprietary character gets NO canon as themselves (see the table below). Their material, where relevant, is recorded as the successor's foundation inside the successor's file.
- Bases are scaffolding, never canon subjects (restated for Star: Xiaoyu and Christie are bases only — anything imported for Star attaches to STAR).
