## THE UNIVERSE

**The North Star (owner, 2026-10-20).** The universe is deliberately built
by layering many traditions: "we're mixing a lot of different folklore,
mythology, different ontologies, religions, different viewpoints and
perspectives, the Oni and Yokai thing, along with the whole Eastern Mind,
Tong-Nou thing, all for what is creating the Western mind universe and
canon." Tong-Nou / Eastern Mind is the cosmology and skeleton; each fighter
carries another tradition's layer where it rhymes with their base (Diablo →
Raijin + Hagoromo; Dio → oni/yokai; the Abel brothers → wrestling
mythology). Adopted articulation, saved to the repo at the owner's order:
"Western Heart isn't a crossover — it's one universe that digests the
others." Full text: `CANON.md` §North Star and `docs/NORTH-STAR.md`.

**Tong-Nou.** A giant green head floating in darkness; inside it, a world:
five lands, a marketplace, a mountain, a shrine — and a trial by combat.
Tong-Nou is called the Purifier. The fighters call the place the **Western
Heart**.

**The Five Lands** (full text in `CANON.md` §1): Tree of Life (Wood),
Plains of Chi-U (Fire), Octagonal Shrine Par-fang / Central Mountain
(Earth, the Source), Helix Palace (Metal — five rooms: Greed, Appetite,
Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction), Palace of Dreaming (Water).
Between them: the Phantom Marketplace under the Eyeball Mountains, where
the Trader buys and sells elemental forces.

**The Trial.** Inscribed at the shrine: *Fight. Die. Return. Claim the
forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.* Claiming all five forces forges
the Katana of Five Forces. The final opponent is the **Assembled Soul** — a
composite of all nine fighting styles, including the player's. Victory is
absorption, not destruction: Tong-Nou was purifying the souls, not
devouring them. Last text card: *"You are whole. Rest now."*

**Death and Reincarnation.** In Versus, death is only death. In Story,
death is the mechanism: the soul returns in a new vessel **chosen from what
it did** — player actions influence who you become (owner design,
2026-10-13; expanded in `CANON.md` §8). The face is assembled in pieces
(three pairs of eyes, three noses, three mouths) and is not cosmetic:
sharper eyes, faster hands; a broken nose, heavier hits. The old vessel is
gone forever. Diablo cannot be inherited until a soul has died three times.

**The Nine-Soul Meter / Soul Surge.** In combat a fighter can invoke a
Soul Surge — channeling one of the other eight souls for ten seconds,
borrowing its signature techniques. It is the fiction, not a layer on it:
the nine are fragments of one being, and in the surge the fighter
remembers.

## THE SEVEN

### PANTERA — The Panther Soul
- **Identity.** Vessel of TOU, battle itself. The standard the others are
  measured against: body, mind, and soul all want the hold — nothing to
  resolve. He fights because fighting is what he is.
- **Appearance / model.** Black panther (not leopard), yellow eyes, black
  tail that moves independently — the tail tip traces slow circles when he
  is still; fighters say the tail tells when the hold is coming. Purple
  tights, red metal PANTERA lettering, tattoo-panther with claw marks on
  the thigh. Owner-locked colorway purple/black/red. Cyborg attire concept
  recorded: **PANTXRΛ-88** — robot panther head from Heavy Leomon, robot
  left arm, Heavy Leomon robot right shin and foot (owner chat-canon,
  2026-10; Doc Frank's story arc turns Pantera into a panther cyborg).
- **Attire ladder (owner, 2026-10-21, superseding the 2026-10-17 order).**
  His Tripo originals take slots 1–5 (as many as exist); Tekken reskins go
  to the back. Landed ladder (PR #448): A1 T7 King reskin default (at the
  time) · A2 Leo man · A3 Snow Leopard · A4 LEOPARD KING Tripo · A5 Crimson
  Rage · A6 T7 P2.
- **Voice / presentation.** Pantera speaks in **growls** — big-cat noises,
  King-style, no real words — with **captions** transcribing his lines.
  Running gag (owner, 2026-10-18): the captions misspell Zui's name as
  "Zooey." Rig rule (owner, 2026-10-18): King-based = **no mouth/facial rig
  ever** (King's mouth is just open); articulated hands/fingers like real
  Tekken models, yes.
- **Story.** Remembers nothing but the clinch. The trial didn't recruit
  him; it recognized him. "The mat is here. I am here."
- **Connections.** Demonio — the only soul who meets him head-on; mutual
  respect. Diablo — dismissed as a pretender. SAM-UR-A1 — curious about
  what a thing that cannot feel the hold fights for.
- **Base / moves.** King (Tekken). Pro-wrestling grappling: chain throws
  (2–4 linked), multi-part holds, Jaguar Step mixups. Grappler archetype
  under the owner's grappler split (2026-10-09). Real T3 King clips are his
  fight animations (PR #144). Golden reference (owner, 2026-10-20): the
  Oct 14–15 setup — Pantera with King's exact movements/attacks/stance
  working perfect — is the level every fighter must reach, then exceed with
  newest-era data (T3 is fallback only).
- **Status record (2026-10-22).** Rig rebuilt at the source (PR #560); 79
  missing King rows baked — every move entry now plays its real clip
  (PR #572). T3 King decode verified clean; no segment flip exists in the
  Pantera path. Owner directive: keep improving him, keep giving him more
  moves, prove moves on another fighter who receives them correctly
  (two-fighter interaction proof is his standing proof format).
- **Open gaps.** Feet splayed sideways — owner ordered a fix across all
  his models (2026-10-21); defect map recorded (forearms/hands and
  waist-down stretch/weight issues; hand weighted to tail on one build).

     879b6dae-52e4-4df9-a6cc-e3c4e59bd13b, normal Demonio moveset.

### PHANTOM — The Ghost Blade Soul
- **Identity.** Vessel of JIN, the blade. A soul that may not be alive
  anymore. Element: Metal, the Helix Palace.
- **Face canon (owner, 2026-10-19) — the mystery stands.** Nobody has seen
  his face; nobody is sure there's a face. What looks like a face could be
  a mask (Fiend/Bray Wyatt, Oni/Yokai). The detailed description that
  circulated (broad jaw, heavy brow, bone-like skin, long white hair) was
  an AI interpretation of the owner's words — **not canon**. The refined
  record: that description was of his **mask**; underneath is a demon/oni
  face (Kakashi/ANBU/samurai-yokai) — you can't tell if the face IS the
  mask; the under-mask face reads as an unskinned version of the mask
  demon (muscle-red, skin-colored, blue, green variants); taking off the
  mask is like tearing skin off his face. `CANON.md` carries an OPEN
  QUESTION flag on this (conflict C2 in `docs/bios/BIOS-HARVEST-2026-10-19.md`)
  — owner decides which stands.
- **Chat-canon (owner direction, 2026-10).** Three faces he wears on
  purpose and one he doesn't: publicly Jiraiya (loud, laughing, pervy-sage
  energy); privately Kakashi (quiet, reads, watches). The third — the
  Fiend mixed with Mankind — lives inside him uninvited, comes out when
  the mask slips, talks crude and screaming and delighted, and watches the
  dying like a shinigami. It is the only time he shows anger: he can't
  control it. After a kill, whichever one of him is driving goes quiet for
  exactly three seconds. The Phantom Marketplace named itself after him;
  he won't say why.
- **Connections.** Koreshi — best friends; their dynamic mirrors Jiraiya
  and Kakashi (owner, 2026-10-19). Waterfall — he watches her; she can
  feel him even when he's elsewhere, the only one who can. SAM-UR-A1 —
  fascinated by him. Scimitar — the other blade; professional rivalry,
  never fought.
- **Base / moves.** Yoshimitsu (Tekken): the sword, spinning attacks,
  teleportation, self-damaging power moves. His strongest techniques wound
  him; the wounds close between rounds, the pain doesn't. Philosophy:
  "The cut is the prayer. The wound is the answer."
- **Open gaps.** Face/mask contradiction above is the owner's call.
  Portrait note: his current live portrait is owner-approved and stays
  as-is (2026-10-20).

### DIABLO — The Devil Soul
- **Identity.** Not one of the nine. **The intruder.** He wasn't called —
  he tore through the membrane of the head-world and announced himself.
  Believes he is the final boss. Element: Fire, but the conquering fire.
- **Appearance / model.** Heihachi's frame, worn like a crown. Model
  history: the shipped model was a broken community Heihachi conversion
  (texture rendered as a brown smear; verified 2026-10-20). The owner
  rejected T3 Heihachi (too polygonal). **Attire 1 = the owner's own Tripo**
  (aee46f6a-3bbc-4fac-9492-96e13759672d, made by the owner 2026-10-20);
  the conversion ladders back, not deleted.
- **Attire 2 — "Devil God" (owner canon, 2026-10-20; merged PR #419).**
  He wears a traditional **Hagoromo** (feather robe, divine drapery). The
  circular ring floating behind him is **Raijin's Taiko Drum Ring** —
  Raijin, Shinto god of thunder, lightning, and storms, rides on clouds
  surrounded by this wheel of taiko drums, beating them to make thunder.
  The swirl on each drum is the **Mitsudomoe** (三つ巴) — three-comma
  Shinto symbol of divine power, the cycle of life, and elemental forces
  like water and thunder. Tripo 9b096022-1e16-4a23-80bb-57c6ea7ec62b.
- **Story.** He makes declarations, not trash talk; before the final round
  he announces the outcome (he has been wrong twice; both times he
  declared the loss was part of the plan). What he doesn't know: the
  membrane *let* him in — the trial needs something to push against.
  Being purified of: the need to be the final boss.
- **Connections.** Demonio — the rivalry the trial was built for. Dio —
  Diablo believes he is the final boss; Dio doesn't think about Diablo at
  all, which enrages him more than any defeat. King Tyrant — two kings,
  one trial. Michiko — she abdicated, he conquers; mutual contempt.
- **Base / moves.** Heihachi (Tekken), Mishima karate, boss style. Note
  from the T3 wiring record (`CANON.md` appendix): his moveset is fully
  wired, but Heihachi's T3 animation bank is the one Seven bank not yet
  decoded into the game (empty vertex rows block the shared bake) — his
  motion is the gap.
- **Open gaps.** Hi-res re-render of the Devil God attire owed (the
  delivered render was 512×512; drums/Mitsudomoe not legible at that
  size).

### SKULL — The Punisher Soul
- **Identity.** Vessel of RETSU, pure violence. Judgment without mercy —
  but the soul is force, and Skull's mind dressed it in a mission.
  Element: Metal, the Helix Palace's Destruction room.
- **The paint (owner, 2026-10-19).** The demon paint is a **living
  symbiote-type thing** (Venom / Sasuke curse mark): it absorbs him, drips
  off him, comes apart from him. Nobody has seen him without it. Recorded
  versions: yellow+black carnage-looking, blue+black, chromatic (SF-style
  alts), Punisher-looking venom type. When RETSU rides him the paint
  SPREADS — face, neck, chest, arms — until his whole upper body is marked
  by the end of the fight; nobody has ever seen him put it on. Mix:
  Prototype (the game) + Finn Balor/Prince Devitt + Venom/Carnage. Under
  the paint is Devitt — "sharp-boned, cold-eyed, the kind of handsome that
  looks like it was carved for wanted posters."
- **Signature.** He does bone-breaking kickboxing and he **counts** —
  after every fight he knows exactly how many bones broke, and remembers
  the number for every man he ever fought. "That's the RETSU in him.
  Retribution keeps books." He doesn't celebrate: he stands over the spot
  where the round ended, making sure it stays down. Owner spec: cargo
  pants and combat boots; keep Bryan's pants.
- **Story.** He believes he was brought as an executioner. Once the paint
  wouldn't come off for three days; on the fourth day Waterfall washed it
  off with her tears, and he let her. Being purified of: the mission.
- **Connections.** Pantera — mutual understanding; violence as vocation.
  Waterfall — she weeps for the people he's punished; he has started
  pulling his strikes. Kinpo — the only soul he'll answer at length.
- **Base / moves.** Bryan Fury (Tekken) — brutal kickboxing, every hit
  meant to end the fight. Philosophy: "Mercy is a luxury of the unbroken."

### SAM-UR-A1 — The Mech Soul

- **Base / moves.** Gun Jack (Tekken). Philosophy: "IF SOUL = UNDEFINED,
  THEN FIGHT ANYWAY." Element: Metal.
