# STORY WEAVE — The Whole-Game Connection Map

*How every fighter in Western Heart connects: the relationship web, the factions, and the branching story structure. Built from the root bible (`CANON.md`), the batch-1 + batch-2 roster canon files, and canon imported from the owner's other projects under his 2026-10-23 import order. Marking rule throughout: material under "Recorded" headings is owner-recorded canon (sourced); material under "Written 2026-10-23" headings is new weave-writing built on those foundations — the owner's to revise.*

## 1. The spine (recorded)

**The place.** Everything happens in the mind of a dying god — Tong-Nou, the head — splintered into **five lands** where souls are purified: **Wood** (the Tree of Life, where souls begin), **Earth** (the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang, the ancestral altar), **Metal** (the Helix Palace, pleasures and powers), **Fire** (the Plains of Chi-U, honest confrontation), **Water** (the Palace of Dreaming, the sea of memory). The lands form a generating cycle — Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water — and everything in the game moves along that current, including the ladder itself.

**The nine souls (recorded).** RIN (律, law) → **Demonio**. BYOU (廟, temple) → **Zui**. TOU (闘, battle) → **Pantera**. SHA (捨, release) split three ways → **Saku Ra** (the Idol), **Kinpo** (the Ascetic), **Awa Pop** (the Memory). KAI (解, the sea in two moods) → **King Tyrant** (the Showman, the surface) and **Star** (the Idol, the current). JIN (刃, the blade) → **Phantom**. RETSU (烈, violence) → **Skull**. **ZEN** refused a body ("stillness does not compete") and **GYOU** stays unvesseled ("the watcher waits for the story to deserve him"). Beyond the nine: the **built souls** — **Doc Frank** (a Frankenstein of eugenicist parts, the oldest living soul), **Cyborg X** (a transgender cyborg-queen, a built body carrying an "A"-soul), **SAM-UR-A1** (a rebuilt soul — a reconstruction; not SAM's soul, not a copy) — and the **splinters-from-losses**: **Waterfall** (KAI's grief), **Torch** (carrying his sister's soul), **Nightwolf** (keeping the shrine the lights answer to). **Not one of the nine, by design:** **Kevin** (the teacher who came on purpose), **Dio** (who came from outside the metaphysics), **Diablo** (who tore his own door).

**The trial (recorded).** Realm In-Between → the Ladder (Shrine of Form's Truth → Ascension of Purification → Altar of Souls → Weighing Trial) → the Shrine. EACH LAND IS A RUNG: claim a land by its element's signature. Die and die again; learn faster. Forge the Katana of Five Forces from the five signatures — **a katana is forged, never found.** Reach the top, and the Assembled Soul — the composite of every fighter (every force, every stage, every soul) — assembles itself *from your climb* and fights you for the only question the game asks: **"Am I even real?"** Win, and your soul is installed as one of the god's personalities. Lose, and you become part of the composite, fighting the next climber. Beating the final boss *adds you to him forever.*

**The two laws of death (recorded).** **Versus death is final** — three rounds, no Reincarnation, no coming back. **Story death is Reincarnation** — Nightwolf receives you and walks you back; on return, **your face is assembled in pieces** — something is always missing until the soul is complete, and the Reincarnation is always choosing which piece to return first. **The Diablo rule:** a soul that has died three times may choose **Diablo** (as an act of surrender). Diablo IS choosable — but only after the third death.

**The Trader and the twist (recorded).** At the Phantom Marketplace, the **Trader** offers: one force for a different force — immediately, no quest. Because the Purifier Twist waits at the top: **the composite keeps the shapes of the souls it has absorbed.** Taking a trade changes what the Assembled Soul holds. The Marketplace's spook-house is hosted by **CornerStone** ("Slice (CornerStone)").

**Sun Rā's quarantine (recorded).** One soul the head-world itself quarantined: **Sun Rā**, the soul that became a god who eats other souls — outer space inside the head, held away from the game on purpose, the final boss of a *pocket realm* near the head's edge (the brow chakra), reachable before the peak. His realm is where the game's feet come off the ground: flying, moon-sized witnesses, stars as direction. And the naming rule: **only he says "Immortal Qombat" in English**; every other fighter heard "Western Heart" when they arrived.

**Systems that bind the weave (recorded).** The **Nine-Soul Meter / Soul Surge**: ten seconds of another soul's style — the game's thesis in a mechanic ("you are never only yourself here"), and it means literally anyone can briefly be anyone. **Stage Drain**: lose on a stage, and *it* grows — stages eat losses and become arenas of their defeats. **Arcade ladders are tournament brackets, not random draws.**

## 2. The relationship web (recorded, by cluster)

**The Seven (the game's opening frame).** Pantera, Demonio ("Donny"), Torch, Dio, Phantom, Zui ("Byou"), Skull — the seven who fight forever around a cup that refills itself, plus the Eighth Cup (the empty chair, for the one who stopped coming — the owner's licensed shot-list image). Every recorded thread in the Seven runs through the cup: Torch brings the fire, Dio brought the first heresy (offering the Eighth Cup), Skull is the one who never learned to say no to the toast, Zui is the Seven's bad conscience trying to be good, Demonio chairs it like a reformed devil, Pantera is the one who could leave and doesn't.

**The Four (the weighting anchor).** Dio, Michiko, Sam-ur-a1, and the memory of Doc Frank — the four who gave a Game Over screen its weight. Michiko (the Dead Wife; Michiko→Torch and Michiko→Nightwolf are the two most-attested edges in the whole record) talks to the dead first: Nightwolf; and the fragment in Sam-ur-a1 carries her warmth's shape. Sam's rebuilt soul accepts her without being able to say why.

**The SHA vessel and the sisterhood.** Saku Ra / Kinpo / Awa Pop — three faces of one release. Saku Ra fights *for* Kinpo ("I'm so proud of you") and against her own mirror in Awa Pop, who is her Second Thought — the thought that wants OUT — while the memory of her keeps her IN. Around them: Torch, chained to Saku Ra by the soul-tie to her sister Sakura Flame (his dead twin, whose soul he carries — the tenderest and most terrible thread in the game: the brother saved, the sister extinguished, and the idol he watches over because she is the age his sister will never be); Kinpo↔Zui (the only one trying to save him; the only one whose faith he can't argue with); Awa Pop↔Kinpo (the Memory keeps the Ascetic honest); Kinpo↔Kevin (discipline recognizes discipline).

**The KAI waters.** King Tyrant and Star — one soul, two moods, the tide and the current: the Showman who performs and the star who never stops moving; his recorded idol-aspects shuffle through her slot (Power/Pop/Metal/Glitter), and the drummers follow *her* tempo. Waterfall, the grief-splinter, pours toward both of them and away from both of them. Mariposa lands on Waterfall's shoulder — the keeper and the grief, the game's softest recorded image.

**The built and the rebuilt.** Doc Frank supplied SAM-UR-A1's craft-knowledge without ever meeting him — Frankenstein is just what the record calls him. Cyborg X, self-made, owes nobody; Frank recognizes a fellow assembled thing and leaves her alone out of professional courtesy. DXMMII — the copy that became an original ("counting the moment nobody can pinpoint, possibly including himself") — belongs in this cluster by theme: the trial's built things arguing, by existing, that made ≠ fake.

**The Elias brothers (imported — Off the Top Rope / AshLane / Bannon).** Cain Elias, the First Rival, and Vain Abel Elias, the Masked Demon of the Purple Haze — brothers, handled in canon like Kane and the Undertaker: Vain Abel works a wheelchair-bound manager ("The Brother") who is stronger than he ever admits; the double-chokeslam (Elias Brothers' Chokeslam, verified at `1+4` in Vain Abel's Western Heart moveset) is the family's signature. Recorded mystery, deliberately unresolved: Vain Abel's missing ears — gone, ear holes visible even masked; he blames his brother for them and for killing their whole family. In this game their thread runs through the ladder: the brother who chases everyone else's family name vs. the brother who kept the family graveyard. (Cain's file flags the brother's name record: "Cain Elias" — applied per the later, shipped usage; an earlier note named the brother "Kane Abel.")

**The showmen's circuit.** King Tyrant (the Showman Soul), Purple Rain (the Performer Soul), Silvertongue (the Heiress — who IS the counter-draw at his table), Saku Ra (the Idol), Star (the current under all of it), Kiko/Rave (the painter-showman whose tambourine stops fights mid-pose). Their thread: everyone in the head performs for someone; the trial is the one audience that can't be fooled. The circuit extends outside the head — the AshLane arrivals below are its road crew.

**The Marketplace web.** The Trader (no fighter sheet; the game's tempter) — Scimitar, the vizier who collects debts and is quietly collecting the ladder itself — N3GΔTIV0, the game glitching given a man (who respects exactly one person: the Trader, "the notary god," because he always reads the fine print) — and the recorded chain N3G→Star ("the Loudest clapping for the Quietest in the room"). Around the edges: Nightwolf, who won't enter (she knows what's priced there) — and the trade's shadow: every force ever traded is a shape the Assembled Soul will wear.

**The witnesses.** Nightwolf (the Veil; receives the dead; her father built the shrine she serves; Yūrei-onī hid behind her father's shrine and she has never feared it since) and Waterfall (the wound that pours; she feels every death in the head like weather). They are the trial's memory: everyone else climbs; the witnesses remember what climbing costs. Mariposa (the Keeper) is their third: the seal on Rin's cage, the door that knows your name, the butterfly on the grieving shoulder.

**The intruders' triangle.** Diablo (the throne wants a devil; he wants the Pearl — and the "devil swap" craft-lore with Thor: the claw-switch, the spear-and-claw dance, the sideways hammer-swap, so players can't tell which god they're fighting), Dio (the only thing that came from outside the metaphysics — "not summoned, not imagined: remembered wrong"; he has no shard in Nightwolf's record), N3GΔTIV0 (the inside glitch). Three different *kinds* of wrong, and the head has a different immune response for each: a chair (Diablo is offered the throne), a blank page (Dio), and a notary (N3G's fine print).

**The Demonio web.** Demonio — RIN's vessel, the soul that looks like a devil and fights like a saint — bows only upward: Nightwolf is the only one he bows *to*, and Kevin taught the trial what a bow means. Mariposa keeps the seal opposite him (she perches to guard Rin's cage — the bible's relationship line, preserved in her file under the reassignment note: the seal-keeper watches the disciplined demon, not the retired source name). Phantom wears the soul *named like* him (Blade/JIN (刃)) and their clinic visits end with "Treat yourself like a prince": the blade and the devil, the two most disciplined wrong-shapes in the head.

**The Wizard Gang (imported — cape-jones / Wizard Gang series).** A faction, not a fighter: nine robes wired as attires (Ashes, Static, Echo, Sombra Negra, Cipher, Hollow, Kiko, Onyx, Theory — full faction canon in `roster/wizard-gang/CANON.md`). **The AshLane arrivals (external-universe figures — no WH roster files; recorded in the bible §9).** **Bannon** (the Stick Up Kid — "twenty dollars and a dream"; moved from AshLane first; his $20-under-starting-money is locked for every fighter), **El Toro de Oro** (the Golden Bull; his feud with Bannon continues *exactly where it left off*), **Pablo** ("The Climax"; innocent outside, unhinged in ring), **NPC Finxsse** (the NPC who charges for directions to himself and gives wrong ones, free). Their home canon lives in the Bannon/AshLane projects (`mhvnsnt/brutalfistgrokversiontenpointfive`, `src/data/bannonRoster.ts` — the owner-locked source of truth).

**Scimitar's court.** The vizier's recorded web: Purple Rain (the guest who never pays), Bruno (the loyalist who betrayed him), N3GΔTIV0 (the glitch he can't tax), Nightwolf (the one door he can't buy), Waterfall (he's kind to her — cruelty would cost him information), and the Soul that was never in the room — his missing half, whose shape the forbidden room keeps.

## 4. The branching structure (recorded systems + written branches)

**The master fork (recorded).** Every run is shaped by the death laws: **Versus** play is final-death (three rounds, no return — brackets, honor, the cup); **Story** play runs on Reincarnation: die, be walked back by Nightwolf, return with your face assembled one piece at a time. The same fighter is two different stories under the two laws, and the game knows it.

**Ladder branches (recorded).** The five-lands cycle is climbable in the generating order; the Trader's force-trade at the Marketplace reroutes a build mid-climb (and re-shapes the final composite); the third-death **Diablo choice** forks any run into surrender-as-power; **Sun Rā's pocket realm** is the great detour — a boss before the boss, off the ladder's edge, for climbers who'd rather face the eater than the mirror. Winning the top installs you *in* the composite: every ending is also a beginning for whoever climbs next.

**Per-character branch threads (recorded in their files).** Demonio's three forms (unwinged / winged devil-up / human-down) are a stance-branch through his whole route; Star's never-stop timer turns her fights into clocks; Skull's route carries RETSU's "which king do you serve now?"; Waterfall's purification changes the arena (tears → rain); Torch's route is the only one where losing a specific fight *advances* the story (someone has to carry the sister home).

**Per-character branch threads (written 2026-10-23 — see §3 and each file).** Chordz's refusal option (play the EP or keep the tapes buried); Gashadokuro's claim hearing; Aotsuki's mercy question; Chatur's unspent invoice; Ryusan's double-offered trade; Pond's stillness debuff earned by temperament; Megami's consented burial; Menajerii's kept-or-released shapes; Tobi's over-the-wall climb; Negativo's Third-Death Clause (his file); Scimitar's Ledger War (his file); Mariposa's Leak (her file); Silvertongue's inheritance clock (her file); DXMMII's Set List (his file); Thor's Fourth Afterlife (his file); Sun Rā's own route — the god who is only reachable, never playable (his file: the stare is the whole fight).
