## Method actually used

- **Census (all 195 MODEL_URLS GLB headers + all 26 T3 bank skeletons):** shipped rigs' rest head-vs-chest yaw is 0.0° for kinpo/zui/yukihyo — the twist is not in the models. Composing each bank's own neutral stance on its own skeleton gives a large CONSTANT head-vs-chest rotation in 24 of 26 banks (heihachi 114.6°, yoshimitsu 96.5°, jin 90.9°, jun 85.9°, bryan 77.2°, eddy/forestlaw/roger 74.5–74.9°, gunjack 73.5°, julia 72.0°, raven 69.3°, xiaoyu 61.1°, law 60.9°, lei 54.6°, kuma 54.4°, paul 53.6°, armorking 45.6°, anna 44.5°, luckychloe 40.1°, ogre 37.5°, nina 37.4°, gon 63.3°, hwoarang 65.3°). King = 0.0° (the one canonically baked bank; Pantera is the reference-good control — his retargeted head delta exactly equals his bank's). Bosconovitch 16.2°.
- **Root cause:** the head is the one driven joint whose swing correction A(c) is identity in retargetT3Clips, so the bank's baked head-frame constant lands raw on every rig. The neck chain takes the chest driver, the head takes the twisted driver — a twisted neck — and the skin between shears (the "face stretch during match": kinpo's head-region vertex distortion measured 17.0% mean in stance before the fix via real skinning). Corroboration: in every canonical bake the Neck track is exactly the Chest's rotation (chest→neck = 0.00°, 19/19 banks) — the offset is a coordinate frame, not acting; bryan's per-clip constants cluster at ~34° while king's vary 0–54° like real acting.
- **Fix (game/js/t3anims.js, the file's own bankFacing/standingIdle pattern):** `estimateHeadOffset` measures the bank's constant from its own stance clip at the first full retarget call (circular mean of G(Chest)⁻¹·G(Head), composed exactly as the frame loop composes G), cached on the skeleton; the frame loop counter-rotates the head's world rotation by it in the chest frame — G′(Head) = [G(Chest)·offset⁻¹·G(Chest)⁻¹]·G(Head) — preserving every clip's variation around the constant. Threshold 20° (king/bosconovitch untouched); 't7:' skeletons exempt (T7 king's 40° is real side-on-stance acting on a jointed-rig bake; its fighters are owner-approved).
- **Ruled out with measurements (in AUDIT.md):** rest-pose twist (0.0° on the named rigs), clip scale keys (retarget emits none; embedded CMU Head/Neck scale tracks read back 1.000–1.000), canon-sizing (burger/custom only), procedural look-at (none exists).
- **Yukihyō resolved honestly:** his bank (king) is the clean one — stance and rows measured head-vs-chest yaw 0–4° in the target frame, so he has no twist to fix. His stretch is the second component: auto-rig weight bleed in the shipped GLB (Head max weight 0.48; head-area verts carry Neck/shoulder weights up to 0.46) smeared by the source's genuine head turns. It is a model-side weight property, not an engine artifact — quantified in AUDIT.md as a follow-up for the rig-repair weight-transfer pipeline, not papered over here.
