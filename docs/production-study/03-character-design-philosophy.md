# Character Design Philosophy — The Riot Method

**Source:** Public interview with Alex Jaffe, Lead Champion Designer on Riot's
Project L (now 2XKO), at Evo 2023 (vg247.com). Public material, summarized here.

## The Method

**1. Go wide, early.**
For each character, write TEN one-paragraph kits. Each paragraph is a totally
different character — different gameplan, different core mechanics. Pick the winner.
For Ekko, the 10th paragraph ("Runback Ekko") won.

**2. Set goals before mechanics.**
"What should a good Ekko LOOK like regardless of how the kit works?" He needs to
feel like a time-traveling genius, easy to pick up, not frustrating to fight.

**3. Prototype the core mechanic first.**
Build the signature mechanic early, put it in players' hands, ask "do we have
juice here?" Build all other moves around the proven core.

**4. Don't cannibalize design space.**
With a big roster, guard each character's territory. "Darius is an axe character
— we might put other big-weapon characters in the game one day." Constraints
force creativity. If two character pods explore the same direction, they talk
and the better fit keeps it.

**5. Design the REAL character, not the abstraction.**
Jaffe's key insight: "Imagine that the character in League is an abstraction of
the real character, and you as a designer are designing the REAL character."
Don't translate mechanics one-to-one (it feels "dry"). Take the ESSENCE of what
makes the character fun — what they FEEL like — and elaborate: "Of course Ahri
would have this weird sweep. It just feels natural. We want players to feel that
this has been Ahri all along — they just didn't know it."

## How This Maps to Western Heart

This is EXACTLY the chimera-build character philosophy, stated by a pro:

- **Tekken's King is the abstraction. Pantera is the real character.**
  King was always a black panther wrestler — Tekken just showed us the jaguar-mask
  abstraction. Pantera is who he really is.
- **Go wide on each Western Heart fighter.** For each of the 44 unnamed fighters,
  the owner names them one at a time — but for each, we should explore what the
  REAL character is before locking the design.
- **Prototype the signature first.** Each fighter's defining mechanic/move gets
  built and felt before the rest of the moveset fills in around it.
- **Guard design space.** 51 fighters is a big roster. Pantera owns the
  panther-wrestler space; no other fighter gets a cat head. SAM-∪R-Λ1 owns the
  mech space. Etc.
- **Essence over literal translation.** When retargeting a Tekken moveset to a
  Western Heart fighter, keep what FEELS like the character, not every frame
  of the original data.
