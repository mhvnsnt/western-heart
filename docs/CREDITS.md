# WESTERN HEART — CREDITS & DEDICATIONS

*Owner directive, 2026-10-20. These dedications appear in the finished game in two places: at the end of some stories (story-ending credits) and in the credits in the settings. The machine-readable record the credits surfaces consume is `game/data/credits.json`.*

## Dedications

**for Serena, for Rosetta, for Emory, for Mars, for Ken, for Marquisha, for Bink**

## For Serena — the heart of the dedications

**Serena Misae Brown.** Nightwolf is her tribute (see the NIGHTWOLF section of `CANON.md`).

In the owner's words:

> "This game is for that game of Mortal Kombat me and Serena never got to play before she died in that car accident in Tulsa. For Serena, Serena Misae Brown, Nightwolf."

The story: one night Serena wanted to come over and stay the night at his and his wife's apartment, and play Mortal Kombat with him, on a night he was up late alone. He didn't invite her over. He carries that. Nightwolf — and this game — is for the match they never got to play.

## Placement

- **Story-ending credits:** the dedications run at the end of story completions.
- **Settings credits:** the dedications appear in the credits shown from the settings screen.

As of this record (2026-10-20), no live credits screen exists in `game/` yet — this document and `game/data/credits.json` are the saved record the story-ending and settings credits will read from when those surfaces are built.
