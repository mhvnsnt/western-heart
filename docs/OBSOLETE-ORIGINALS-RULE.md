# OBSOLETE ORIGINALS RULE — READ THIS BEFORE TOUCHING THE ROSTER

## ⚠ THE RULE — OBVIOUS AND EXPLICIT, OWNER'S ORDERS (2026-10-23)

**A source-game character whose moveset and identity were given to a
proprietary character of this universe is OBSOLETE IN CANON. They are not a
character in this universe. They get NO canon file, NO story, NO writing, NO
roster entry. Only the proprietary character they became exists here.**

The owner, verbatim:

> "anybody that we took from other games that we gave their moveset and
> identity to another character they come off the list and they don't get
> canon in our universe... they became other characters in our universe
> that are proprietary to our universe. Their characters that they used to
> be don't get any canon or writing. They actually get written next to them
> that they are obsolete in the canon and became new proprietary characters
> anything you wrote for them you can keep it just put that at the top of
> their files."

And again, same day:

> "people like Zangief, Blanka, Vega, Zafina, Xiaoyu, any of the characters
> that became other people they come off the list and they don't get canon
> written in for them."

## What this means operationally — no exceptions

1. **No canon for originals.** Never write a canon file, bio, story,
   backstory, relationships, or story threads for an obsolete original as
   a character of this universe. Not in `roster/`, not in the bible, not
   in the archive, not in the story weave.
2. **Existing text is KEPT, never deleted — and topped with the notice.**
   If text about an original already exists somewhere (an old section, a
   harvested bio), it stays, but the obsolete notice (template below) goes
   at the very top of that file or section so nobody mistakes it for
   living canon.
3. **The successor carries everything.** Identity, story, connections,
   branching threads, relationships — all of it belongs to the proprietary
   character. When in doubt, write for the successor.
4. **Base names are scaffolding only.** An original's name may appear
   inside the successor's own file as a base/moves reference (e.g. "Base /
   moves: Jin Kazama (Tekken)") and in mapping/teardown documents. That is
   a transitional asset reference, never an identity.

## CHARACTER SELECT + ATTIRE LADDER

The owner, verbatim:

> "Those characters shouldn't even show up on the character selection
> list... they should only be wired up as laddered attires for whoever
> they became like way later in the attire list like five six seven eight
> nine... those are going to be the front ones the Tripo 3D models and the
> proprietary reskins that I made like Pantera... everyone else they're
> all their attires and stuff just become ladders... that should be
> documented and explicitly said in the repo too."

Stated as rules:

1. **Obsolete originals NEVER appear on the character select list /
   roster as entries.** Not selectable, not listed, not counted as
   fighters. There is no roster slot for an original, ever.
2. **An original's models and skins may exist ONLY as laddered attires of
   their proprietary successor** — placed LATE in that successor's attire
   ladder (slots 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and beyond), behind the successor's own
   attires.
3. **The FRONT of every attire ladder is fixed:** the character's Tripo
   3D models first (slots 1–5, as they exist), then the owner's own
   proprietary reskins (e.g. Pantera's Leopard King line — the owner's
   reskin work). Original-game attires and reskins ladder behind those,
   never in front of them.
4. This section generalizes the owner's standing ladder directives:
   the Pantera ladder reorder (2026-10-21 — Tripo originals take slots
   1–5, Tekken reskins to the back) and the Doc Frank forefront rule
   (Tripo models in slots 1–5; reskins never in slots 1 or 2). What was
   ordered per-character there is ordered for the whole roster here.

## The marking template — copy this exactly

Wherever a file or a section's subject is an obsolete original, this
notice goes at the very top of that file or section, above all existing
text (which is kept, unchanged, below it):

> **OBSOLETE IN CANON — <ORIGINAL> is not a character in this universe.
> Their moveset and identity became <SUCCESSOR>, a proprietary character
> of this universe. No canon is written for <ORIGINAL>. See
> roster/<successor-dir>/CANON.md.**

*Rule recorded 2026-10-23 at the owner's direct order. This document is
the authority on obsolete originals for everyone working on the game;
where any other game document conflicts with it, this rule wins and the
conflict gets flagged to the owner.*
