## How the banks were built (repeatable method)

1. Slot mapping from the characters' own TTT2 motbin records via the join
   tables only (anim_ref = (bank<<14)|index; bank 1 = per-char data005
   table — Roger @90,926,084 ×509, Jun @193,068,196 ×596; bank 2 = data004
   @0x100 ×1,875). No guessed mappings anywhere: every slot below names
   its join record (move name → anim name → bank/index).
2. Decode with the shipped decoders (tools/ttt2_mota_decode64.py for 0x64,
   the 0xC8 decoder for 0xC8). No decoder-variant clip was forced: the
   ~10% encoder-variant families are reported by the decoder, and no slot
   in either bank resolved to one.
3. Sampler convention (the missing piece the decoder spec left open):
   the game's i16 rotation sampler (Default.xex func 0x82289228, a sin/cos
   polynomial) composes limb rotations as ZXY-Euler((−t₁, t₂, t₀)/2) of
   the raw i16 track triples — found by exhaustive convention search
   (order × inverse × permutation × signs × scale) scored on stance/walk
   anatomy (15/15), the two top candidates proven the identical map
   (max diff 5.6e-16). Root orientation uses the same family with its own
   calibrated affine. Per-joint calibration A(j)·Ĝ(j)·B(j) was fit on
   helper-posed 0xC8 clips (all 20 tracked joints ≤1.7e-7 rad) and the
   numpy synthesizer reproduces Blender + TekkenAnimHelper poses to
   ≤4.7e-07 across full clips, so Blender is not needed in the bake loop.
4. Wrap handling: the game's pose accumulator wraps at ±π in decoded
   space (period 2π); the bake unwraps each rotation track by choosing,
   per frame, the euler representative (per-component ±2π offsets plus
   the euler-alias branch) whose rotation is closest to the previous
   frame's, frame 0 anchored to the character's own stance triples, with
   a backward-anchored retry kept only when it gates strictly better.
5. Conversion to wh-anim/1 is the shipped t7_c8_stance_bake convert()
   verbatim; every baked clip passes its FK self-check at 0.000000 m and
   the in-game plausibility gate from t7_move_bake verbatim, plus the
   disclosed toe-graze second pass under Deviations.

## Roger bank — 15 baked / 5 skipped

Baked (join record → clip): neutral (Kangaskhan stance state, 270f),
medium, heavy (gate note), hit, walk_fwd (gate note), walk_back
(gate note), dash_fwd, backdash, crouch, crouch_hold, jump_up,
jump_fwd, jump_back, sidestep_a, grab. The grab slot's record was
corrected during CI verification: the first pick (ThrmvLB2_00 →
naco_tukami2l, bank 2 idx 849) failed the gate (LeftToeBase 0.08 m on
frame 20, second pass rejected), so the slot takes the motbin throw
block's standard front-throw record Thrmv_B1_00 → Tco_ntukami
(bank 1 idx 385) — the same anim family as Jun's grab — which bakes
clean with no gate note (40f, FK 0.000000). Full record-level detail:
docs/proof/2026-11-04/ttt2-banks/roger-bake-report.json.

Skipped (each logged in the bake report):
- block — the shared guard clip (bank 2 idx 424, Zco_PSgu02) has a root
  height track of exactly 0.0 for all frames: the engine supplies root
  height at runtime for this clip, so baked raw it stands 1.02 m under
  the floor. Limbs pose a correct guard when lifted; structural skip.
- light — his jab clip starts in a lowered stance (RootRot −π/2 at
  frame 0, hip dips then rises into a clean jab by f16–20): fails the
  gate's "starts upright" rule as a raw clip.
- ko — a launch reaction (root height rises 1066→1301 mm, root rotation
  constant (0, π/2, π/2)): fails "starts upright" as a raw clip.
- hit_launch / sidestep_b — single-toe graze 0.08–0.09 m past the
  second-pass criteria (see Deviations); left unfilled rather than
  shipped past the stated rule.

## Jun bank — 7 baked / 7 skipped / 6 join gaps

Baked: neutral (Jn_IKI00 → amju_kamae_test, bank 1 idx 139, 97f), grab
(Thrmv_B2_00i → Tco_ntukami, bank 1 idx 126; passed the gate only on the
backward-anchored unwrap), jump_up / jump_fwd / jump_back (shared
Bco_newjump2, bank 2 idx 309), sidestep_a, walk_back.

Skipped at the gate (logged): block (same zero-root shared guard clip),
dash_fwd ("does not start upright"), hit (frame-0 toe 0.13 m — an
unwrap anchor artifact: the same shared clip passes under Roger's
reference triples; backward anchoring improves it to 0.13, still past
the second-pass limit), hit_launch, ko (non-upright start), sidestep_b,
walk_fwd.

Join gaps (no record exists in Jun's own motbin — full evidence in the
bake report and the join lane's jun_slots analysis): light, medium,
heavy, backdash, crouch, crouch_hold. Jun's TTT2 motbin is an overlay:
her standing basics are inherited at runtime from the shared Kazama
core that Unknown's motbin carries explicitly (Uj_lp00 → amlu_lp2,
Uj_lk00 → amju_lk, Uj_rk00 → toju_rk, Uj_sDASHB → toju_back90,
st2cr_00B → ygco_ta_sya, cKAM__00_ → ozco_syagami — all Unknown bank-1
records). Those clips were NOT taken: bank-1 indices are per-character
table indices and do not transfer (verified this lane: shared bank-1
anims sit at different indices in Jun's, Roger's and Paul's tables —
0/52 index agreement Jun↔Roger). Locating Unknown's own bank-1 table
base in data005 is the one remaining research step: six candidate
tables share the Kazama-family layout (their idx-134 entry decodes
byte-identical to Jun's stance clip), but they disagree on the basic
slots (5 distinct variants at idx 219/234/235), so picking one would be
guessing. Candidates: 44851360, 150079668, 163712228, 198828228,
254734996, 259686676. The discriminating test for the next lane:
fingerprint Unknown-unique boss anims from her record set against each
candidate table's entries.

Consequence, stated plainly: Megami moves, jumps, sidesteps and throws
on Jun's own clips (2+4 plans 'grab' with Jun's Tco_ntukami), and her
strike inputs present the grab clip through the engine's generic
last-resort tier because no strike clip exists in her bank yet. She is
playable and fightable; her standing basics land when the Unknown
table is pinpointed. Menajerii has no such hole: her basics present
Roger's own medium/heavy clips (light is a gate skip, so '1' also
presents heavy).

## Row 48 (CI finding, resolved as a documented gap)

Binding megami to the jun bank activated the suite's row-coverage rules
(test/attack-anim.test.mjs), which surfaced a legacy stamp: 270 of
Megami's moves carry `anim_t3 {bank: 'jun' | 'tekken-wiki', row: 48}`
from the 2026-10-23 anim-fix ("true-map class-only"). Row 48 is a
Tekken 3 table row number; it has no TTT2-Jun counterpart (in Jun's
join table, record 48 is a tag change-in record, and no standing basic
strike records exist in her motbin at all — see the join-gaps section
above). The row therefore stays unfilled: this PR amends
attack-anim.test.mjs with a KNOWN_ROW_GAPS entry (megami / jun / 48,
both bank spellings) so the suite records the gap instead of failing
on it, following the repo's documented-gap convention (cf.
roger-bank-coverage.test.mjs). When Unknown's bank-1 table is
pinpointed and Jun's basics are baked, row 48 is filled from the
inherited Kazama core and the KNOWN_ROW_GAPS entry is deleted in the
same PR. Nothing was fabricated to satisfy the test.

## Probes (identity-gated, vs Pantera, local served tree)

Gate for every run: p1.char.id matches + roundState 'fight' verified
before any input was trusted. Menajerii: 69 skinned meshes, t3actions
15 (= the baked bank). Megami: 52 skinned meshes, t3actions 7.

Menajerii per-input plans (plan-probe): 1/2/3 → heavy (generic tier,
Roger's t3_heavy), 4/1+2/d/f+1/d/f+2/u/f+4 → medium (Roger's
t3_medium). Video-run plans (fresh timing): 2 → medium armR, 3 →
medium legL, 4 → medium legR, d/f+1 → heavy armL, 1+2 → medium armR.
Throw probe (throw-probe-menajerii.json): 1+3 and 2+4 both plan
'grab' — Roger's own Tco_ntukami throw clip. A full null-plan scan of
her 193-notation moveset in the node harness returns zero unresolved
inputs, and test/attack-anim.test.mjs passes 9/9 with both fighters
in the loop. No bind pose, no donor bank, no kuma clips anywhere.

Megami per-input plans (plan-probe): all strike notations → no own
plan (no strike clips — the gap above). Throw probe: 2+4 → 'grab'
(Jun'﻿s Tco_ntukami), 1+3 → no plan, '2' and 'd/f+2' → 'grab'
presentation via the generic last-resort. Damage sampling in the
throw probe read 0 within its windows (throws whiffed at the probe's
1.1 m spacing); damage numbers are the moveset's own values, unchanged
by the bank swap — the bank changes which clip plays, not move data.
