WrestleMania XIX templates. This PR's answer is `docs/research/pulls/creation-suite/wwe2k-firepro-template.json`:

creation-suite/code lane — this lane does not edit suite code. WMXIX is deliberately NOT wired:
its 169 banked rows have 0 inputs and PROVISIONAL labels, not real move names, and wiring them
