# Realm Travel — Eastern Mind Canon + Environmental Evolution Design
**For: Heart and Mind Within mode (world/exploration layer)**
Date: 2026-10-23 | Researcher: DoggByte (Deception research subagent)
Status: canon-sourced design research — no game files, no asset ripping.
Companion: docs/research/MK-DECEPTION-2026-10-23.md (MK's approach to the same problem)

> **Owner vision (2026-10-23):** "Realms within realms within beings within thoughts." Realm-to-realm travel is a BIG mechanic, core to the canon. Different ontologies and ways of perception become actual realms/universes — multi-dimensional, layered perceptions and states of being made navigable. The environment should shift *ontologically*, not just visually.

## 1. What the canon already gives us (CANON.md, quoted)

### 1.1 The cosmology

Tong-Nou is a giant green head floating in darkness — "not metaphor. The head is real, the darkness is real, and inside the head is a world: five lands, a marketplace, a mountain, a shrine, and now — a trial by combat." (CANON.md §1 L20–22)

"It is a body, and the lands are its organs." The sky inside the head is the inside of the head — dark, vast, veined. Fighters call it the **Western Heart**: "the heart that beats in the western dark of the head."

**Design consequence:** realm travel in WH is *anatomical*. You don't teleport between levels — you move through a body. The roads between the lands are the vessels between organs. This is the single most important differentiator from MK's realm travel (see §2): their realms are separate worlds connected by portals; ours are organs of one body connected by roads.

### 1.2 How beings enter — the travel grammar (canon)

Canon already defines SIX distinct ways to arrive, each with different metaphysics. These are our realm-travel verbs:

1. **THE CALL** — "a pull every lost soul feels, drawing them toward the Octagonal Shrine." Passive, irresistible, the default. (CANON.md §1 L30)
2. **TEARING THE MEMBRANE** — Diablo "tore through the membrane of the head-world." Torch "tore through after her" sister. But: "the membrane let him. Tong-Nou needed an intruder — the trial needs something to push against." Entry by force is *permitted by the world* — the world wants something to push against. (CANON.md §2 L152–169, L759)
3. **THE WOUND BETWEEN WORLDS** — Nightwolf "came the way shamans enter spirit worlds: through ritual, through the wound between worlds, carrying sage and the names of her ancestors." Deliberate, ritual, earned. (CANON.md §2 L665)
4. **WANDERING IN** — Michiko: "the road just kept going and one day the landscape was a giant head." No call, no purpose — "the trial did call her, but it called her the way the road calls. She came because she was already moving." (CANON.md §2 L699–709)
5. **MULTIDIMENSIONAL CROSSING** — Cape Jones "flew through the TV into the game — and it was real." The Ashlane arrivals "crossed from the Bannon sub-universe, a world the call reached the way it reaches every world with fighting in it." (CANON.md §7 L849, §4 L445)
6. **HATCHING NATIVE** — Mariposa "hatched here — in the branches of the Tree of Life… the trial's only native: not called, not claimed, not built. Grown." (CANON.md §7 L1084)

**Design consequence:** HMW doesn't need one travel mechanic — it needs all six as *different doors into the same world*, each with different narrative weight and gameplay cost. The Call is free. Tearing costs you (the world pushes back — combat). The Wound requires ritual (quest-gated). Wandering is exploration-gated (walk far enough). Crossing is event-gated (multiverse beats). Hatching is origin-story only.

### 1.3 The internal map — roads, liminal zones, pocket realms

- **The roads between them** — the lands are connected by roads, not portals. Travel is *traversal*, with things to encounter on the way. (CANON.md §1 L28)
- **The Phantom Marketplace** — "a liminal bazaar under the Eyeball Mountains." Liminal = between. It is the smallest land and "the only one that is a *verb*." Nothing here is real, but the prices are. This is our hub — the between-place. (CANON.md §1 L40, arena pipeline)
- **The Octagonal Shrine Par-fang** — the destination: "Eight-sided, ancient, still." The trial is inscribed in stone: *Fight. Die. Return. Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole.*
- **Pocket realms** — Sun Rā's arena is "a pocket realm where the trial's rules don't apply… quarantined — the pocket realm is where the trial puts the things it can't heal yet." Realms can exist *inside* lands with different physics and different rules. (CANON.md §7 L1190–1215)
- **Nested cosmology** — "He's from *outside the head* — the galaxies, the real dark, not Tong-Nou's interior dark." There is an inside-dark and an outside-dark. Realms nest: pocket realm ⊂ land ⊂ head ⊂ darkness ⊂ galaxies. (CANON.md §7 L899)

### 1.4 The environment evolves — "Colour is law"

The single most important canon line for this task:

> "Colour is how the trial tells the truth when words would lie. A land whose force has been claimed does not announce it. Its colours *settle* — the burning sky of Chi-U cools a fraction, the Dreaming palace's reflections clear — and the souls who live there feel the settling before they can name it. Tong-Nou is healing or it is not, and the palette is the chart on the hospital wall." (CANON.md §8 L1385)

And the arena pipeline's global principle #3: **"A land whose force has been claimed… its colours *settle*"** — tagged as a future dynamic-grade hook: per-ladder-progress color settling.

**Design consequence:** the environment IS the progress bar. As the player claims elemental forces, the lands visibly heal — Chi-U's burning sky cools, the Dreaming palace's reflections clear. The player *sees* the trial working. No HUD element can do what the sky does.

Per-land evolution grammar (from canon):
- **Tree of Life (Wood):** "glowing souls hang like fruit" — as the trial progresses, do the fruits ripen? Are there fewer, or more? The blinding washed-out sky is intentional (never dim it — user law).
- **Plains of Chi-U (Fire):** "the Fire Tower of Time burns and never collapses. Time does not pass here; it *accumulates*, like heat." The land literally accumulates the player's history — scorch marks of past fights persist.
- **Helix Palace (Metal):** "Everything reflects. Nothing is what it seems." Five rooms: Greed, Appetite, Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction. Desire distorts perception — the rooms should *react to who enters* (the palace-claimed Burger is the Appetite room's).
- **Palace of Dreaming (Water):** "won't stay still. Walls ripple. The floor reflects a sky that isn't there. Change is the only law." The floor shows a *different sky than overhead* — the environment disagrees with itself. This is perception-as-geography made literal.
- **Octagonal Shrine (Earth):** "Eight-sided, ancient, still." The one place that does NOT change — the still point everything else orbits. Contrast is the design.

## 2. How MK does it (comparison)

Deception's Konquest (full doc: docs/research/MK-DECEPTION-2026-10-23.md): six *separate* realms connected by the **Nexus** — a hub with portals, each realm's portal unlocking sequentially as you place the Kamidogu on its altar. Travel = portal hop. The realms are discrete worlds; the hub is outside them.

**What to take:** the sequential-unlock hub grammar (each zone is the key to the next), the NPC-populated zones, the post-quest free-roam.

**What to invert:** Deception's realms are *disconnected* — you portal between them and the world doesn't change. WH's lands are *organs of one body* — you walk the roads between them, and the body heals around you as you progress. Their travel is teleportation; ours is circulation. Their world is static; ours is a patient recovering.

**The synthesis for HMW:** Deception's *structure* (hub → sequential zones → free-roam) wearing WH's *body* (roads not portals, healing not static, color-settling as progress).

## 3. Environmental evolution — the design spec

### 3.1 The settling system (P0)

Per-land visual state tracks trial progress (elemental forces claimed). Implementation hooks already exist: arena pipeline global principle #3 names the dynamic-grade hook; the canon-lock CI guard (tools/arena-pipeline/canon_lock.py) protects canon look.

- Each land has a **canonical baseline** (canon_baseline.json) and a **settled state** (colors cooled/cleared per CANON.md §8).
- Progress blends baseline → settled. The blend is *felt before it's named* — subtle at first (canon: "the souls who live there feel the settling before they can name it").
- The Shrine stays still. Everything else moves toward stillness.

### 3.2 Perception as geography (P1)

The owner's vision: "different ontologies and ways of perception become actual realms/universes." Canon already does this twice:

- **Helix Palace:** desire warps the architecture. Mechanic: the five rooms respond to the *entering fighter's* canon nature (a greed-driven soul sees Greed's room swell; the reflections show what you want, not what is).
- **Palace of Dreaming:** the floor reflects a sky that isn't there. Mechanic: the environment *disagrees with itself* — overhead sky vs. floor sky vs. wall ripples each tell a different truth. Navigation requires reading which layer is real.

New rule for HMW zone design: **every zone must have at least one perceptual disagreement** — something the eye sees that the feet don't feel, or vice versa. The Dreaming palace is the template, not the exception.

### 3.3 Nested realities (P1)

Canon nests: pocket realm ⊂ land ⊂ head ⊂ darkness ⊂ galaxies. HMW zones should nest at least two deep:

- A land contains a pocket (Sun Rā's quarantine is the template — different rules inside).
- A pocket can contain a *wound* (Nightwolf's entry verb as a place — a shamanic threshold inside the pocket).
- Depth is signaled environmentally: the further in you go, the more the palette leaves the head-world's shared spectrum (canon: "the head-world runs on one shared palette the way a body runs on one blood" — leaving the palette means leaving the body).

### 3.4 The roads (P1)

"The lands where souls fight, the roads between them, the shrine where it ends." The roads are canon but undesigned. HMW spec:

- Roads are *traversal zones*, not loading screens — encounters, traders, wanderers (Michiko's verb: the road that becomes a head).
- The Phantom Marketplace sits *on* the roads (liminal, between) — the hub is not a separate place, it's the between-place.
- Road encounters use the NPC world-fight system (Deception doc §2.2): approachable fighters, handicap modifiers, non-roster opponents.

### 3.5 What NOT to do

- Never portal-hop between lands (that's MK's grammar, not ours). Portals exist *only* for multidimensional crossings (Bannon sub-universe, Cape Jones's TV) — crossing *into* the head, never *within* it.
- Never change a land's canonical colors for spectacle (ARENA LAW: upgrade quality, preserve look). Evolution = settling toward healed, never redecoration.
- Never lock a zone permanently (Deception's flaw — documented in companion §6). The roads always stay walkable.
