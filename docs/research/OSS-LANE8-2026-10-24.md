## Measured failures fixed during this lane (not hidden)

1. Role matching broke on separator styles (`Shoulder_L` vs role
   `shoulderL`): fixed by normalizing names (lowercase, strip
   `_ . -`) in both `retarget.js` and `rig-compat.js`.
2. Ragdoll center-pinned joints exploded (torso y 3 -> 6.6):
   fixed with contact-point joint frames — then ball legs
   squeezed between torso and ground still exploded (torso ->
   3.9). Fixed with capsule limbs + mass tuning (30:1 -> ~3.5:1
   torso:leg): full body now settles (torso 0.88 at 120 steps).
3. Cape-cloth test collided with its own torso sphere: split
   into a clean segment-length case and a dedicated collision case.
