## 1. CORNERSTONE — The Obnoxious One

**Not one of the nine. The collision.** [established]

**Backstory.** He came into the trial the way a bad idea comes into a good
room: all at once, and already talking. Underneath is the menace — the
black-shirted sadist with the blonde top of hair, the tongue stuck out mid-taunt,
the cruelty that doesn't need a reason. On top is the showman — the raised
eyebrow, the catchphrase, the man who plays to a crowd that isn't there.

The trial didn't call either of them. It got both anyway.

He does the uranage the way other fighters do introductions: lifted, turned,
driven. He took it from everywhere it existed — the King's version, the
All-Stars version, the teardown tapes — and made it his the way a thief makes a
crown his: by wearing it better. The diving elbow comes off the jump, the
People's Elbow delivered from the air. The tongue comes out whenever it wants.

There is a third ingredient, still theoretical: the younger demon's fingernail
blast, filed under design intent, waiting on a source the teardown holdings
don't have yet. [brief — design intent, no YYH source in hand]

**Personality.** He sticks his tongue out mid-fight — not at the opponent, at
the dark past the lights. The menace is for the ring. The show is for the
seats that are empty.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The tongue mocks. The hands collect."

**Relationships.**
- **RYUJI:** The pure one still walks the roster — same source, unmixed,
  untouched. Cornerstone has never fought him. The trial is either saving it
  or avoiding it, and neither of them will ask which.
- **The empty seats:** He plays to them anyway. The showman's persona needs a
  people; the trial provides the Eyeball Mountains. They watch. He performs.
  The arrangement suits everyone except the opponent.

**Element.** Fire — the Plains of Chi-U. [provisional] Confrontation lives
under the burning sky, and everything Cornerstone does is confrontation, even
the jokes.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the hybrid frame — coiled menace in a black
shirt, blonde top hair, the tongue always half-out. MIND: the showman — the
early-Attitude-Era persona, the eyebrow, the smack talk, playing to a crowd
that isn't there. SOUL: not one of the nine. A bonus soul — a collision, not a
fragment. The friction: the body wants to hurt. The mind wants to be *watched*
hurting. The soul hasn't decided which one it is, and the indecision is the
whole act.

**Why Cornerstone thinks he's here.** He believes he's the main event — that
the head called for the most electrifying collision in the trial and delivered
him. In his story there's a gate, a payday, a people. The idea that there's no
crowd, no contract, just a dying god trying to heal itself — that hasn't
reached the cheap seats.

**Being purified of:** The reaction. The trial keeps emptying his arena and he
keeps playing to the mountains. It's asking what's left when nobody's watching.
He hasn't answered. The tongue stays out.

**Signature detail.** The tongue. Fighters who've faced the pure Yamazaki say
it's the tell: tongue out, the sadism is driving; tongue in, the showman is.
Nobody has seen both at once. Nobody wants to.

**Owner-locked:** Name CORNERSTONE locked 2026-10-21 (prior WH working name

## 2. DIO — The Demon That Is (deepened pass)

**Not one of the nine. Not a vessel. The trial's failure mode.** [established —
CANON.md §6; this is a deepening, not a replacement. Every line of the
established bio stands.]

**Backstory.** The raging demon, the dark hadou made flesh, the martial artist
who traded his soul for the perfect kill — worn by something the trial cannot
name. Diablo tore his way in wanting to conquer. Dio tore his way in from
*further out* — not from outside the head, but from outside the metaphysics.
The hunger for destruction isn't fragmentation. It's a different physics: a law
of nature wearing a fighter's shape.

The shrine stones don't go silent on him, the way they do for the built ones.
They *crack*.

**Personality.** Silence. Then violence. There is no third mode.

**Fighting philosophy.** The hunger has no philosophy. It has *appetite.*

**Element.** None. [established] The lands reject him — grass withers where he
stands, water stills, metal dulls. He fights on scorched ground of his own
making.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the demonic frame, the Raging Demon incarnate.
MIND: the hunger — not a mind but an appetite wearing a mind's shape. SOUL:
unknown. Unknowable. The trial's instruments break when pointed at him.
Demonio is the soul *named* demon who fights like a saint. Dio is the thing
the name was warning about.

**Why Dio thinks he's here.** He doesn't think. The hunger doesn't wonder
about venues. But in the rare moments it quiets — between kills, in the ash —
something in him recognizes the head. Not as a god. As *prey*. He believes he's
here because there's something here strong enough to be worth killing. He's
right. It's himself. The Assembled Soul has a Dio-shaped hole. He's walking
toward it.

**Being purified of:** The trial cannot purify him — the hunger isn't
fragmentation, it's a law. He is the test the trial gives *itself*: can the
medicine survive the poison?

**Relationships.**
- **Demonio:** THE rivalry, deepened. The disciplined demon versus the demonic
  one — and each believes the other is the pretender. Demonio bows before
  every fight. He has never bowed to Dio. Dio has never noticed. That is the
  whole rivalry: one soul performing discipline at a hunger that doesn't know
  it's being addressed.
- **Diablo:** Diablo believes he is the final boss. Dio doesn't think about
  Diablo at all. This enrages Diablo more than any defeat — the conqueror
  cannot conquer being *unseen*.
- **Waterfall:** She cannot weep for him. There is no grief in him to feel —
  no pain, no loss, only the hunger. This frightens her more than anything in
  the Western Heart. She avoids him. He doesn't notice that either. The trial's
  witness has found the one thing she cannot witness, and it haunts her worse
  than any grief.
- **Torch:** She's not afraid of him. He is the only thing she's ever met that
  she can't read. It bothers her. She'd never admit it. Assassins recognize
  predators; this one doesn't recognize anything back.

**Signature detail.** He has no win pose. He has a crime scene. The trial's
cleaners refuse to work his arena.

**Owner-locked:** Name DIO. Akuma base (Akuma's slot). Master of the dark
hadou. Pure destructive force. [established]

## 3. BRUNO — The Heartbreak Soul (deepened pass)

**A mortal soul, drowned in feeling.** [established — CANON.md §6; deepening,
not replacement.]

**Backstory.** The street-brawler's fury — raw, emotional, every hit personal —
worn by a soul that died of heartbreak. Not metaphorically. The heart stopped
because it couldn't stand another beat of the ache. The 3AM despair. The silk
over the wound. The mumble that talks around the thing that hurts. The swagger
that armors it.

He's a beautiful mess, and he knows it, and he'd rather die again than say so
out loud.

**Personality.** Moody. Smooth. Mumbling. Vain. A heartbreak in a leather
jacket — and the jacket is the only thing he irons.

**Fighting philosophy.** "You hit like my ex. Everybody hits like my ex."

**Element.** Water — the Palace of Dreaming. [established] He drowns
differently than Zui: Zui drowns on purpose, Bruno drowns because he never
learned to swim.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the brawler's frame, every punch thrown from the
gut. MIND: the swagger — mystery, showmanship, vanity, all of it armor over
the wound. SOUL: a mortal soul, broken the ordinary way — by love, or the lack
of it. The body wants to fight. The mind wants to be *seen* fighting. The soul
wants to stop hurting. None of them are getting what they want.

**Why Bruno thinks he's here.** He believes he *died* — and he's right, but he
thinks it's the ordinary kind. In his story, this is the afterlife, and the
afterlife is a fighting tournament, because of course it is. He hasn't
considered that it's not the afterlife — it's a *hospital*. The idea that he's
being healed would embarrass him. He'd rather be damned. Damned is cooler.

**Being purified of:** Heartbreak. Learning to feel without drowning. The
Palace of Dreaming keeps showing him the moment it broke. He keeps looking.
The trial thinks this is progress. He's not sure.

**Relationships.**
- **Waterfall:** She understands his heartbreak — she feels it every time he's
  near, the way she feels everything. He hates being understood. He keeps
  coming back. She is the only mirror he can't smash, because she's crying too.
- **Zui:** Zui keeps offering him drinks. Bruno keeps accepting. They're
  becoming friends. Both pretend otherwise. The monk drowns on purpose; the
  heartbreak drowns by accident — between them, the bottle is the shallow end.
- **Michiko:** She recognizes the outlaw in him — he ran from love the way she
  ran from a throne. She doesn't say it. He knows she knows. It's the most
  honest conversation he has.

**Signature detail.** His mumble is actually crystal clear. He's saying exactly
what he means. You just have to lean in. No one leans in. That's the whole
thing.

**Owner-locked:** Name BRUNO (owner: "Miguel is Bruno already"). Moody mystery
× smooth showmanship × mumbling drawl × vain swagger. Spanish brawler. Moveset
source technical only. [established]

## 4. SCIMITAR — The Vizier Soul (deepened pass)

**Born in the Phantom Marketplace. The Trader's vizier.** [established —
CANON.md §6; deepening, not replacement.]

Scimitar was born in the Phantom Marketplace, under the Eyeball Mountains, to

deal closes around you.

The scheming. The hunger for the lamp. He doesn't want the katana to become
whole. He wants it as *leverage*.

On his shoulder sits the pauldron-blade — fused to his body and his soul both.
It is not worn. It is *grown*. If it were removed, he would die: the blade is
load-bearing. [brief — user direction 2026-10-22, not yet in repo records]

**Personality.** Regal. Sinister. Smiles with his mouth; his eyes smile only
when he's already won. Polishes the pauldron-blade when he's thinking. He's
always thinking.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Every fight is a negotiation. I always collect."

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace's Greed room is his true home, though
he'd never admit the Palace owns him the way it owns Burger. [established]

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the soldier's frame, precise and coiled — and
the pauldron-blade, fused at the shoulder, part of the skeleton now. The body

is a transaction, every kindness has a price. SOUL: marketplace-born, not

**Being purified of:** The need to own everything. The trial is waiting. So is

**Relationships.**

- **Michiko:** She sees through him completely. He hates her for it. He can't

**Signature detail.** He keeps a ledger. Every fighter owes him something — a

The pauldron-blade has never once been used to collect. That's how you know
it's the important one.

**Owner-locked:** Name SCIMITAR (named for his weapon). Scheming, manipulative,

## 5. CHORDZ — The Cutter Who Strums

**Not one of the nine. A mortal lost soul.** [provisional category — no canon
category recorded yet]

**Backstory.** He was a guitarist before he was anything else — emo, pale,
dark circles, face paint, black lipstick, the straitjacket, hair going sideways
like it was trying to leave. The look was the whole statement: *don't touch
me, I'm already broken.*

Then the blades came. Not all at once — that would have been kinder. In
stages. First the hands stayed hands and the look did the talking. Then the
blades shot out of his fingers, and the guitar went quiet. Then the hands
stopped being hands at all — a spiked mass, a ball of blades where the
strumming used to happen. And at the end of the stages, the final form: knives
all over the body, only the hair and the smile visible, a big knife lying
across the eyes, razor blades seeded through the hair like notes on a staff.

The strength goes up with every stage. So does the quiet. [established — user
design + transformation spec]

The turn the trial noticed: the song didn't stop when the hands became blades.
It got *clearer*.

**Personality.** Between rounds he moves his fingers like he's playing —
scales, arpeggios, nothing there. The straitjacket's sleeves hang empty when
the blades are out. He plays anyway.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The strings broke. The song didn't."

**Relationships.**
- **No crossings on record.** The trial's ledger holds four entries for him —
  one per stage — and no witness willing to describe the space between them.
  The other blades in the trial (Phantom's, Scimitar's) have not met his. The
  trial is in no hurry to arrange it.

**Element.** Metal — the Helix Palace. [provisional] Blades are metal, and the
final form is knives all over a body. The Destruction room has an address for
him. He hasn't visited. The room is patient.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the four stages — the look, the blades, the
spiked mass, the Cutter. Each stage stronger, each stage less hand. MIND: the
guitarist — emo, sideways, still hearing the song under the steel. SOUL: a
mortal lost soul, unclaimed by any room so far. The friction: the body becomes
more blade with every stage. The mind still thinks it's holding a guitar. The
soul hasn't decided whether the transformation is a costume or a diagnosis —
and the trial, which knows, isn't telling.

**Why Chordz thinks he's here.** He believes he's headlining — that the trial
booked four sets and he's playing all of them, each one louder than the last.
The idea that the stages aren't costumes but what's *underneath* hasn't reached
him. Encores don't work that way. Transformations do.

**Being purified of:** The edge. Every stage adds blades; the trial keeps
asking what the music needs them for. He's starting to suspect the answer is
nothing. The blades disagree. The song is caught in the middle, still playing.

**Signature detail.** In the final form, a big knife lies across his eyes. He
can't see. He plays better.

**Owner-locked:** Emo guitarist, Edward-Scissorhands look (pale face, dark eye
circles, face paint, black lipstick, straitjacket, sideways hair). Four
transformation stages, gradual, strength/power increasing per stage — "The
Cutter Who Strums" is the final form. Moveset source technical only. All four

## 6. AOTSUKI — The Sire Soul

**Not one of the nine. The one who turned Deathfang.** [brief]

**Backstory.** Blue fur, like deep water made solid — the werewolf-dragon,
female, built the way the old stories build predators: beautiful the way a
storm is beautiful. She is older than the trial's interest in her, and she
knows it.

She turned Deathfang. That is the whole of what the trial knows about her, and
it is enough. The emo loner with the chains and the NO FUTURE tattoo, the boy
nobody believes is the monster until the moon proves it — she made that. The
bite is forever. The chain is his.

The turn the trial keeps circling: she doesn't wear chains. Sires don't need
them. She watches what she made fight inside a prison of its own grief, and
her blue fur doesn't move, and the Palace of Dreaming ripples a little less
when she's near — the way still water goes stiller around something that knows
exactly what it is.

**Personality.** She watches Deathfang's fights from the dark between arenas.
She has never once cheered. Creation, to her, is a spectator sport.

**Fighting philosophy.** "I gave him the moon. He built the cage."

**Relationships.**
- **Deathfang:** Her get. She turned him; he chained himself. She has never
  apologized and never explained, and he has never asked — the boy fights his
  grief, the wolf fights everything else, and the sire watches both the way
  weather watches a town. When his meter fills and the chains become a
  warning, her fur stands up. That is the closest thing she has to pride.

**Element.** Water — the Palace of Dreaming. [provisional] Change is the only
law there, and she *is* change: the bite that turns a boy into a moon. Her fur
is the exact blue of the palace walls on the days they ripple.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the blue-furred werewolf-dragon — female,
predator-built, beautiful the way weather is beautiful. MIND: the sire's
patience — she measures time in transformations, not rounds. SOUL: not one of
the nine. Something older than the trial's categories, unclaimed and
unbothered. The friction: the body is built to turn things. The mind is
content to watch what it turned. The soul — whatever made a monster and walked
away — hasn't looked back. The trial keeps putting her in Deathfang's arena.
She keeps watching. Something in her is waiting to see if the cage opens from
the inside.

**Why Aotsuki thinks she's here.** She thinks she's here to watch. Sires
watch. The trial hasn't corrected her — possibly because it can't find the
category to correct her *with*.

**Being purified of:** Detachment, maybe. She turned him and walked away; the
trial keeps seating her where she can see the chains. It's asking what a maker
owes the made. She hasn't answered. Her fur doesn't move.

**Signature detail.** Her fur is blue — deep-water blue, the color the Palace
of Dreaming's walls go on the days they ripple. Fighters who've seen her and
the palace in the same hour swear they're the same blue. The palace has never
confirmed it. The palace confirms nothing.

**Owner-locked:** Name AOTSUKI (Panda-replacement name; Aokami was the other
candidate — Aotsuki per the round brief). Female blue-furred werewolf-dragon.
The one who turned Deathfang — his creator/sire. [brief — user direction

## 7. GASHADOKURO — The Bone Dragon

**Not one of the nine. A spirit monster.** [established — Kuma-replacement name
lock, user directive 2026-10-22]

**Backstory.** White — all white, bone-white: the dragon-kaiju built from the
giant skeleton of the old stories, given thunder-lizard bulk and ember-drake
wings and multicolored markings, rainbow eyes, Japanese cloud-smoke curling
off the bones in colors that shouldn't exist on a skeleton. He is the trial's
largest fighter and its quietest.

Zui found him the way drunks find everything: by accident, on purpose. The
monk fears him and respects him and looks up to him like a protector — the one
creature in the head-world Zui doesn't toast *at* but *to*. The bone dragon
lets him. When the monk drinks past the point of standing, the dragon lies
downwind — a wall of white between Zui and whatever's coming — and goes
perfectly still. Skeletons are good at still.

The turn: the thing the monk fears is the thing keeping him alive. Zui knows.
Zui drinks anyway. The dragon stays anyway. That is the whole relationship, and
it is the most functional one in the Western Heart.

**Personality.** He goes still when Zui is near — the kaiju holding its breath
so the monk can sleep. Stillness, from a mountain of bone, is a love language.

**Fighting philosophy.** "The skeleton keeps what the flesh cannot."

**Relationships.**
- **Zui:** The monk fears him, respects him, looks up to him like a
  protector-monster. Zui toasts the monks who disowned him before every fight;
  he toasts the bone dragon *after*, quietly, when he thinks nobody's
  watching. Everybody's watching. The dragon pretends not to notice. The
  Central Mountain's loss is the skeleton's keeping.
- **The trial, generally:** The shrine stones don't crack for him the way they
  do for the intruder, and they don't go silent the way they do for the built.
  They *settle* — the way color settles in a land whose force has been
  claimed. The trial has filed him under *kept*, not *lost*.

**Element.** Earth — the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang, the Source. [provisional]
Bones belong to earth. The still point at the center of the head is where
still things go.

**Body / Mind / Soul.** BODY: the white bone dragon — kaiju bulk, drake
wings, rainbow eyes, cloud-smoke markings, all white underneath. Built for
destruction, the way mountains are built for avalanches. MIND: the guardian's
simplicity — one monk, one wall of white, no philosophy. SOUL: not one of the
nine. A yokai-spirit, old the way weather is old. The friction: the body is a
kaiju — everything about it says *level the land*. The mind has chosen the
smallest, drunkest monk in the trial and appointed itself his weather. The
soul, which remembers being a giant skeleton in older stories, finds the
appointment *correct* in a way it can't explain. So it stays.

**Why Gashadokuro thinks he's here.** He doesn't think. He stays. If pressed —
and no one presses a bone dragon — the answer would be the monk: something has
to stand between Zui and the things Zui toasts. The idea that the trial might
be purifying *him* hasn't occurred to him. Skeletons don't need purifying.
They're already what's left.

**Being purified of:** Nothing. He isn't being purified — he's what the trial
puts between the lost and the worse. The medicine keeps its own guard.

**Signature detail.** His eyes are rainbow — all colors, all at once. Fighters
say each color is a soul he kept. Zui says they're just pretty. The dragon has
never corrected him.

**Owner-locked:** Name GASHADOKURO locked 2026-10-22 — after the giant
skeleton yokai of Japanese folklore; replaces the old "URSA" placeholder.
White bone dragon/kaiju (thunder-lizard + skeleton-warrior + ember-drake mix,
all white, rainbow eyes, multicolored markings, multicolored Japanese

## 8. JOTEI — The Exile Soul

**Not one of the nine. The fallen empress.** [established — intake character
canon, owner-approved 2026-10-22]

**Backstory.** She was an empress before she was an exile — the imperial court,
the wrestling of the court (every hold a protocol, every throw a decree), the
crown. Then her family's crimes: soul-binding, the old sin, the kind that
doesn't wash off with abdication. The court took the crown. It couldn't take
the ledger.

She is coming to collect. That's not a threat; it's an itinerary. Every fight
is politics. Every opponent is either a debt, a debtor, or a witness. She
plays the long game because the long game is the only one exiles get — and she
plays it the way she wrestled at court: every hold a protocol, every throw a
decree.

The web she drags behind her: her family's crime made Ryusan what he is. The
guilt ran downhill into Tobi, who runs faster than any soul in the trial. The
trail goes through Pond's swamp, Dorowani's mud, Chatur's sacrifice, Megami's
seal, Menajerii's sanctuary — and ends at Zen's cracked Oversoul, where the
bill comes due. [established — the canon web, owner-approved]

The turn: she was exiled for the crimes, but the crimes were the family's, and
the family is gone, and the collecting continues. At some point the ledger
stopped being about the court. Nobody — least of all her — has said out loud
what it's about now.

**Personality.** She touches her head before the bell — where the crown was.
The gesture is the only thing the exile didn't take.

**Fighting philosophy.** "Every fight is politics."

**Relationships.**
- **Ryusan:** Her family's crimes made him what he is — the acid, the anger,
  the evidence he carries without knowing it. She watches his fights the way
  empresses watch consequences: without flinching, without looking away. He
  doesn't know she's watching. The trial does.
- **Tobi:** The guilt ran downhill into him, and he runs faster than any soul
  in the trial — speed as avoidance, the courier who can't outrun the
  delivery. She could catch him. She doesn't. Some debts you let run; the
  interest is the point.
- **Chatur:** The sacrifice at the end of the web's first span — the
