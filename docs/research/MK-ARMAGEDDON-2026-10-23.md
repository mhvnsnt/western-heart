# Mortal Kombat: Armageddon (2006) — Technique Research

**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Game:** Mortal Kombat: Armageddon (Midway, 2006 — PS2/Xbox/Wii)
**Why this game:** The user liked it for two systems — Konquest mode (adventure exploration) and Kreate-A-Fighter (character creation). Both ideas feed "Heart and Mind Within" (HMW) mode and our creation suite.
**Scope:** Technique research only — structure, economy, systems. No asset ripping, no extracted content. MK names stay in technical/research files only, never in WH canon docs.
**Primary sources:** Prima Official eGuide (archive.org), GameSpot E3 2006 hands-on, Hardcore Gaming 101 retrospective, GameFAQs walkthroughs/FAQs, Wikipedia, game manual.

> **Correction to user memory (verified):** Armageddon's Konquest stars **Taven** (a demi-god), not the player's created fighter — created fighters were never playable in Konquest. The "walk the world with your fighter, graveyards/temples" memory matches **MK: Deception's Konquest** (Shujinko, open-world realms, graveyard/temple zones). We research Armageddon as assigned, and note Deception's open-world structure in the mapping section since it matches what the user remembers and wants for HMW.

## 1. Konquest Mode — World Design

### 1.1 Premise and framing
You play Taven, a demi-god awakened by his father Argus (who contacts him through obelisks) just before the final battle of Armageddon. The mode is the game's narrative spine — it delivers the plot, not just training.

### 1.2 Structure: linear chapters, not open world
- Konquest is **chapter-based and linear**, ~11 zones across 14 guide stages. You move zone-to-zone; no persistent open world (that was Deception's Shujinko design, which Armageddon replaced after devs took inspiration from **Shaolin Monks**).
- Length: ~2–3 hours straight run (Hardcore Gaming 101); 6–12 hours with full exploration (GameFAQs user reviews).
- **Travel:** progression unlocks the next zone; a map scroll enables the world map overlay — **green circle = next objective, blue arrow = your position**. No fast travel; no return hubs.
- **Checkpoints:** frequent auto-checkpoints. Death (health out, or one-hit-kill traps) sends you back to the last checkpoint.

### 1.3 The 11 zones (walkthrough order)
1. **Botan Jungle** — intro, first relic, first 1v1 fights (Kobra, Kira), first obelisk training, first supermove (Ground Pound). KAF Elder Gods armor piece in a gold chest.
2. **Temple of Argus** — fire-breathing statues, crusher obstacles, arena brawls, a poison-trap chest (run away after opening).
3. **Tekunin Warship** — escape-room design: Sektor's ship with a self-destruct timer (~10 real minutes), key cards, green panels to open doors, rooms gated by beating Tekunin mobs; grants the Fireball supermove.
4. **Arctika** — snow zone, sword/puzzle mechanics, Tengu ninja fights, 7-wave **Obelisk Training** trial that unlocks the Arctika arena, 15 chests (biggest treasure zone).
5. **Lin Kuei Palace** — 1v1 fights vs. Frost (unlocks Escape supermove) and Sub-Zero (Taven's chestplate armor reward).
6. **Charred Mountain** — volcano, meteor-fall triggers, timed sequences.
7. **Netherrealm Cliffs** — fight vs. Drahmin (unlocks Netherrealm Cliffs arena); Taven's gauntlets found after a meteor drop.
8. **Shinnok's Spire** — boss/gauntlet climb.
9. **Shao Kahn's Fortress (2 stages)** — orb-fire puzzles vs. a giant Shao Kahn statue (relics: Sheeva's Earrings / Kintaro's Armor / Goro's Gauntlets), throne room (Reiko fight; Raiden's Hat relic).
10. **Edenian Ruins** — Giant Skull encounters (killing them spawns chests holding the Scorpion's Lair and Edenian Ruins arenas), final relics (#59–60).
11. **Red Dragon Caverns** — Time Stop supermove (fork path, right fork into green light); the finale pushes toward the Pyramid of Argus boss (Blaze).

### 1.4 Exploration loop
- **Chests** (visible) + **hidden chests** (appear only when you walk close — proximity reveal) + **gold chests** (high-value: KAF armor pieces, key items).
- **Relic items (60 total):** character artifacts (Kobra's Glove, Kira's Knife, Kai's Wristbands, Cage's Picture, Jarek's Vest, Kabal's Mask, Dairou's Keys, Hotaru's Flag, Stryker's Radio, Moloch's Ball, Cyrax's Bomb, Kung Lao's Hat, Nightwolf's Axe, Sektor's Rocket, Jade's Bracelet, Sonya's I.D., Raiden's Hat, Motaro's Horn, Scorpion's Spear, Goro's Gauntlets, Kintaro's Armor, Sheeva's Earrings, Blaze's Essence…). Often hidden behind breakable walls ("a section of cliff that does not match the rest") or hazard courses.
- **Koin bags** and breakable objects (stumps, pots, spider-kills, huts).
- **Soundtrack pickups** (musical-note icons) for a music menu.
- **Map scrolls** to view the map.
- **Combat, two kinds:** (a) **brawler mode** — third-person 3D combat with a small move list (uppercut, roundhouse kick, throw, fast punch) + 4 unlockable **supermoves** (Ground Pound, Fireball, Time Stop, Escape), environmental kills (uppercut enemies into giant fans/off ledges), throwable weapons (hammers, swords), fatality prompts on dazed enemies. Fallen foes emit **green orbs** (health) and **blue orbs** (special meter). (b) **1v1 boss fights** — full MK fighting with that character's real moveset (~18 scripted duels: Kobra, Kira, Kabal, Jarek, Sektor, Sonya, Rain, Sub-Zero, Frost, Drahmin, Reiko, …).
- **Obstacle courses:** fire statues with readable patterns, crusher machines with pause windows, spinning blades, flaming-arrow towers, spike pillars, timed escapes.
- **Trap chests:** poison, ceiling ambushes (enemies drop down), self-destruct timers.
- **NPCs:** monks/townsfolk are non-hostile — hitting them drops koin bags (petty crime with no consequences). Hostile mobs roam (Red Commandos, Tekunin, Black Dragon thugs, yetis, tengu ninjas).
- **Obelisks:** story beat + tutorial nodes — Argus "calls" you, teaches a mechanic (hand-to-hand, evasion, throws, supermoves), then a trial fight.

### 1.5 Unlock economy (Konquest ↔ game)
- **Koins:** single currency (Deception had 3 coin types). Earned everywhere — Konquest chests/bags/drops, Arcade, Versus. The whole shop economy runs on it.
- **Relic reward ladder:** 10 relics = Meat (character) · 20 = +20,000 koins · 30 = Daegon · 40 = Pyramid of Argus arena · 50 = Blaze · **60 = everything in the Krypt**.
- **Chests also hold:** alternate costumes for main-roster fighters, arenas (Arctika: beat all 7 Obelisk trials; Scorpion's Lair: 3rd chest right after Giant Skulls; Edenian Ruins: 2nd chest left; The Krypt arena: all 60 relics), soundtracks, KAF armor pieces, and **Taven's armor** (chestplate: beat Sub-Zero; gauntlets: Netherrealm meteor area) — once found, Taven wears it for the rest of Konquest, and it becomes his alternate costume in versus mode.
- **The Krypt → renamed "The Vault"** (Game Informer #154): smaller than the Deception/Deadly Alliance Krypts, and crucially — **content is unlockable from ANY mode** (Arcade, Konquest, etc.), so players aren't forced through Konquest. Cheat codes at the "?" box unlock art/movies/arenas for the lazy.
- **Gating style:** physical/ability-based (Fireball lights braziers; Ground Pound breaks gates; self-destruct timer forces route efficiency) + collectathon gating (relic tiers). No NPC quest-log or branching dialogue — fetch-and-carry runs through obelisk objectives.

## 2. Kreate-A-Fighter — Depth Breakdown

### 2.1 Creation flow (7 appearance pages)
1. **Body Type:** gender (M/F), size (Small/Medium/Large), skin color.
2. **Head:** face preset (Cyborg, Evil, Evil Undead, Old, Possessed, Reptile, Tarkatan, Vampire, Zombie, Flaming Skull, Geisha, …), hair (~30+ styles per gender: Mohawk, Dreads, Anime, Ponytail, Geisha Long…), helmet/hats (Bandana, Ninja Hood, Raiden hat, Kung Lao hat, Horned, Space Ninja, Elder Gods…).
3. **Upper Body:** torso, cloth/sash overlay, gloves.
4. **Lower Body:** pants, belt, boots.
5. **Accessories:** head items (devil horns, pointy ears, masks), chest items (cape, gun, armor), arms (bionic arms, blades, wristbands), legs.
6. **Attributes:** facial sliders only — nose width/length, mouth width, lip size, cheekbones, cheek width, jaw width, eyelids, eye angle. No body-shape sliders (body comes from gender × size).
7. **Extras:** voice (Small/Large Male/Female, ~6 voices), victory kata (win pose: Long Fist, Mantis, Shaolin, Snake, Tai Chi, Karate, Kick…), victory slogan, name, biography.
- Nearly every clothing piece is **dyeable in hundreds of colors** (primary + accent color per piece). "Paper doll" design — parts pulled straight from main-roster costume pieces (e.g., Reptile torso, Cyber Armor, Shokan arms).
- **Class presets (14 designed; 2 shipped):** production planned classes like Humans, Tarkatans, Mercenaries, Black Dragon, Ninjas, Retro Ninjas. On release only Sorcerer (male) and Tarkatan (female) presets existed — the rest must be assembled piece by piece. **Buying every piece of a class unlocks that class preset automatically.**
- One created fighter per player profile (community workaround: new profile per fighter).

### 2.2 Move assignment (the deepest part)
- **Fighting style:** name your own style; pick a **stance from 12** (purely aesthetic per the Prima guide — e.g., Yuan Yang, Val Tudo, Shotokan, Wrestling, Dragon) OR use a preset (Aggressive/Defensive) that auto-builds a balanced style.
- **Per-input move assignment:** for every input notation (4 buttons × plain/directional modifiers, e.g., Square+Down, Triangle+Back, X+Forward, Circle+Down) plus 5 canned-combo strings and 3 air attacks, you pick from **hundreds of named attacks** organized by hit property: High Punch / Low Punch / High Kick / Low Kick / Knockdown / Popup / Sweep / Uppercut / Stun / Stumble / Medium (per Prima's categorized attack lists; individual attacks cost 100–500 koins, some free).
- **Guide's balance advice:** make the first attack of every canned combo a mid (can't be ducked); end at least one combo in a popup (air-combo starter) and one in a knockdown; mix low/mid for close-range mix-ups.
- **Weapon style (secondary style):** weapon type = **swords or axes only** (15 weapons: Autumn Dao, Broadsword, Demon Fang, Katana, Kori Blade, Kriss, Kunlun Dao, Machete, Ninja Sword, Storm Sword, Axe, Cleaver, Crude Hammer, Kick Axe, Spiked Club, War Hammer). Weapon stance is aesthetic; weapon style gets **2× the canned combos** of the hand-to-hand style, so combos are the weapon's main advantage. Same per-input assignment system.
- **Special moves: 5 fixed input slots** (6 on the projectile slot family). Each slot has a fixed, mutually exclusive move pool — you CANNOT stack six projectiles; the design forces a balanced kit:
  - **Projectile slot** (fixed input): Twisting Fire, Blue Moon, Teal Smoke, Catapult Bail, Powerstar, Bouncing Doom, Black Magic, Summoned Magic, Voodoo Trap, High/Low Dragon Fireball, Chest Missile, Upward Missile, Surging Blast, Air Fire Blast, Emperor's Shield, Flame On, Crackling Legs, Iron Leg, Star Screamer, Judgment Fist, Nightshade, Tele-Flurry, Chi-Blast, Crouching Chi-Blast…
  - **Charge slot** (D,D+attack): Flying Kick, Bike Kicks, Rolling Thunder, Running Serpent, Shadow Kick, Flying Lunge, Belly Bash, Monkey Flips, Gusting Cyclone, Grasshopper, Quick Trip, Split Flip Kick, Cold Shoulder, Torso Spin, Diving Corpse, Sliding Foot Grab, Charging Spikes, Backflip Kick, Gut Buster, Flying Flurry, Flipping Heel Kick, Air Rotate Kick, Rising Uppercut, Nature's Torpedo, Avoidance Punch, Twisty Kick, Target Practice, Poston Punches, Cannon Ball, Pretty Kick, Burning Fist, Wind Kick…
  - **Disabling slot** (A,D+attack): Lava Burst, Cannon Drill, Banshee Scream, Death From Above, Uplifting Knee, Rising Heel, Carnival Spin, Lightning From Above, Spin Cycle, Sliding Uppercut, Rolling Uppercut, Windmill Kick, Shocking Bolt…
  - **Teleport slot** (X,W+attack): Sky Kick, Vanishing Winds, Stealthy Shadows, Mysterious Magic, Tricky Portal, Dashing Evade, Rocket Punch, Hellfire Kick, Teleport Grab, Mystic Vapors, Wind Teleport, Boot Kamp, Mind Warp, Squarewave Punch, Tele-Punch, Hocus Pocus, Hoodlum, Pasta Maker, Come Get Some, Whoppa, Room Sweeper, Sumo Stomp, Neck Breaker, Spear, Summon Flames, Iceball, Puke Puddle, Earthquake, Tombstone Drop, Tornado Wind, Air Funnel…
  - **Throw slot** (R1): Hop Kick Throw, Face To Floor, Rollback, Arm Snapper, Windgod Throw, Aussie Bulldog, One-Two-Three, Back Stomp, Trip Up, Flipping Strike, Face Eraser, Heel To The Gut, Chaos Axe Kick, Front Flip, Bouncer.
  - Costs 500–3,000 koins each (most expensive: Voodoo Trap / Tele-Flurry / Teleport Grab / Lightning From Above / Shocking Bolt / Trip Up / Chaos Axe Kick / Bouncer at 3,000). Some free from the start.
- **Specials are borrowed from the main roster** (Scorpion's spear, Sub-Zero's Iceball, Sektor's Chest Missile) — the whole point is assembling your kit from the full game library.
- **Balance guardrails the devs built:** forced one-special-per-slot (no six-projectile builds); guide recommends at least one low-hitting special and one unblockable special in the kit; "some moves and items are free from the beginning" so the mode is playable before grinding.

### 2.3 Stats/attributes tied to appearance
- **No RPG stat points.** The Prima guide, the game manual, GameSpot's preview, and the wiki document NO Strength/Speed/Health point allocation — that claim exists on exactly one fan site (playmortalkombat.com, uncited) and contradicts every authoritative source. Treat as unverified/untrue.
- What DOES tie gameplay to appearance: **body size** (S/M/L changes model scale/hitbox reach) and **gender** (different part pools); stance affects the visual idle/animation only; the Aggressive/Defensive presets auto-build different starter kits.
- In other words: your build's power comes from **which moves you unlock and assign** (the koin economy), not from a stat screen.

### 2.4 Kreate-A-Fighter economy
- **Most parts AND most moves are locked at start.** Unlock via koins earned in any mode (Konquest is the fastest — chests, bags, drops). Per Hardcore Gaming 101: "your coins are much better spent on the character creation parts."
- **No refunds** after purchase; **preview before buying** (the guide stresses: you can see each piece/move before committing).
- Unlock is **per-profile**: once bought, a part/move is available to every fighter created on that profile.
- Special pieces (Elder Gods armor set) are **found in gold chests inside Konquest**, not bought.
- Community note: fighters are password-protectable per profile; created fighters were playable in Arcade, Versus, and online (Xbox Live / PS2 online).

## 3. What WH Steals

Map Konquest → **Heart and Mind Within (HMW)** exploration, and Kreate-A-Fighter → **creation suite**. MK names below are technical references only; WH-side names are our own.

### 3.1 Konquest → HMW exploration
- **Chapter-per-realm walkthrough.** HMW runs as linked zones (Tong-Nou realms: swamp, temple, palace, fortress…), each ending in a boss 1v1 — exactly Armageddon's zone → boss → next zone rhythm. Our zones can pull names/ideas from canon, not from MK.
- **YOUR fighter walks the world (the fix).** Armageddon locked Konquest to Taven; the user wants their created fighter as the explorer. That's our upgrade: HMW adventure stars the player's custom character, earning gear and fighters as they go.
- **Deception's open-world layer for the remembered feel.** What the user actually remembers (open world, graveyards/temples, wandering) is Deception's Shujinko design — hub realms you can revisit with warp travel. HMW should layer this on top of Armageddon's chapter spine: linear main path + revisitable hub realms for side content.
- **Objective marker system.** Green circle = objective, blue arrow = player on the map scroll. Direct steal for HMW's quest UI — readable, zero tutorial needed.
- **Relic economy.** 60 lore-tied artifacts with a stepped reward ladder (characters, arenas, koins, "everything") is the single best retention mechanic in the mode. WH version: per-realm relics tied to our canon (Trial artifacts, Nine-Soul relics), with milestone rewards at 10/20/30/40/50/60.
- **Chest taxonomy.** Visible chests / proximity-reveal hidden chests / gold high-value chests / trap chests (poison, ambush). Hidden-by-proximity is a cheap, effective exploration reward. Gold chests = creation-suite gear (direct parallel to Elder Gods armor pieces).
- **Found gear you wear.** Taven's armor (beaten from bosses, worn for the rest of the run, becomes his versus alt) is the model for HMW gear: boss-dropped attire pieces that equip immediately and persist into versus as attire unlocks.
- **Two combat modes.** Brawler mob combat (small moveset + unlockable superpowers, environmental kills, orb pickups) vs. scripted 1v1 boss duels. HMW mobs can be Tekken-fight-system brawls; bosses are real versus fights.
- **Obelisk tutorial nodes.** A floating advisor (Argus) that "calls" at set points, teaches a mechanic, then tests it. Copy the pattern with a WH narrator figure — story and tutorial in one node.
- **Ability-gated progression.** Fireball lights braziers / Ground Pound breaks gates / timers force route planning. HMW zones should gate with earned abilities, not just flags.
- **The Vault rule.** Everything unlockable from ANY mode (Konquest, Arcade, Versus), never forcing one mode — plus cheat-code backdoors for accessibility content. Adopt for our unlock shop.

### 3.2 Kreate-A-Fighter → creation suite
- **The 7-page creation flow maps to our WWE-flow directive.** Body → Head → Upper → Lower → Accessories → Attributes → Extras is the same rhythm as the mandated WWE sequence (body → head/face → skin → hair → facial hair → attire). Midway and WWE converged on the same flow independently — follow it.
- **Per-input moveset assembly is the key mechanic.** Name your style, then assign individual named moves to every input + canned-combo strings, drawing from the full game library. This is EXACTLY the user's moveset-library vision (section: user directive "MOVESET LIBRARY SYSTEM") — library organized by type, swap/customize/save per fighter. Midway validated the design; we build it on the Tekken move library.
- **Fixed special-move slots as balance design.** One projectile / one charge / one disabler / one teleport / one throw — no six-projectile builds. Steal the constraint pattern for any "super/extra move" slots in our suite: slots are typed, pools are curated.
- **Koin economy for parts AND moves.** Most parts locked at start; earned currency buys them; no refunds; preview before purchase; unlock is per-account and shared across all created fighters. Direct model for our creation currency loop (earn in HMW/fights → spend in suite).
- **Class presets that auto-unlock.** Buy every piece of a set → the preset appears. Steal for attire-set completion rewards.
- **Biography = arcade ending.** The bio text you write becomes your fighter's ending cinematic. Direct parallel to our canon-grounded bios — the player's words become their fighter's story payoff.
- **What NOT to copy:** one-fighter-per-profile (we allow many); stances as purely cosmetic (ours come from real Tekken teardown data); no test-dummy preview (add the preview the GameFAQs reviewer asked for); the unverified "stat point" claim — our builds get power from move selection, same as MK.

### 3.3 Do NOT
- **Do not** copy MK's linear-only Konquest as the whole HMW design — the user remembers and wants Deception-style open exploration too. Use Armageddon's chapter spine + Deception's open hubs.
- **Do not** put MK content in WH canon writing or bios (user law — MK names live in technical files only).
- **Do not** rip/extract MK assets into the public repo (technique research only — always).
