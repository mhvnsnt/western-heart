TREE OF LIFE · LAND OF WOOD

🜃 Soul-Fruit Harvest · tamaire, Japanese undōkai

Myth root: tamaire — the festival ball-toss, hurling balls into the high basket.
WH identity: the Tree of Life "holds glowing souls like fruit" (canon). Souls gather at festivals to toss spirit-orbs back into the branches — every orb returned is a soul guided home.
How it plays: tap to launch a spirit-orb; arcs with real physics. Land orbs in the glowing fruit clusters. 10 orbs, 45 seconds.

PHANTOM MARKETPLACE · UNDER THE EYEBALL MOUNTAINS

🎲 The Trader's Chō-han · chō-han bakuchi, Japanese dice gambling

Myth root: chō-han — the classic dice gamble: even (chō) or odd (han).
WH identity: the Trader runs the table himself. "Nothing here is real, but the prices are." Bet shards; the house takes its cut — the Trader always eats.
How it plays: stake shards, call CHŌ (even) or HAN (odd). Two dice. Win pays 1:1 minus the Trader's 5%.

OCTAGONAL SHRINE · CENTRAL MOUNTAIN · EARTH

⛩️ Shrine体系 · the shrine terminal, playable now

WH identity: the Octagonal Shrine keeps a terminal in its room for arriving, where arriving souls are read before they are weighed. It sees in sixteen colors and speaks with one voice at a time.
How it plays: the terminal boots below. Type HELP, or tap a command: ORACLE draws three canon words; SPRITE is a sixteen-color drawing board; MUSIC plays single-voice shrine hymns; GAME descends the Central Mountain — dodge what the mountain puts in the way, and the shrine pays Soul Shards for distance survived. SHARDS shows your balance.
