HOW TO PLAY

MoveLeft joystick / A · D keys
Jump / CrouchHOLD joystick up/down / W/S key
SidestepTAP joystick up/down (background/foreground)
LP1 / RP2LP1 Left Punch / RP2 Right Punch buttons · 1 / 2 keys
LK3 / RK4LK3 Left Kick / RK4 Right Kick buttons · 3 / 4 keys
ThrowLP1+LK3 or RP2+RK4 together (1+3 / 2+4)
Break ThrowWhen grabbed: TAP the matching limb — LP1 breaks 1+3, RP2 breaks 2+4, LP1+RP2 breaks command throws. Read the attacker's lead arm!
StringsChain limbs: LP1,RP2 — directionals: f+LP1, d/f+RP2 — motions: qcf+RP2
Soul SurgeS button / L key (full Nine-Soul Meter)
BlockB button / Space (hold)
DodgeD button / Shift
Move ListTraining mode: M key or MOVE LIST button

Light hits grant +8 soul, heavy +11, taking damage +6, blocking +4. Fill all 9 souls, then unleash your Soul Surge.
