HOW TO PLAY

MoveLeft joystick / A · D keys
Jump / CrouchHOLD joystick up/down / W/S key
SidestepTAP joystick up/down (background/foreground)
LP / RPLeft punch / right punch buttons · 1 / 2 keys
LK / RKLeft kick / right kick buttons · 3 / 4 keys
ThrowLP+LK or RP+RK together (1+3 / 2+4)
Break ThrowWhen grabbed: TAP the matching limb — LP breaks 1+3, RP breaks 2+4, LP+RP breaks command throws. Read the attacker's lead arm!
StringsChain limbs: 1,2 — directionals: f+1, d/f+2 — motions: qcf+2
Soul SurgeS button / L key (full Nine-Soul Meter)
BlockB button / Space (hold)
DodgeD button / Shift
Move ListTraining mode: M key or MOVE LIST button

Light hits grant +8 soul, heavy +11, taking damage +6, blocking +4. Fill all 9 souls, then unleash your Soul Surge.
