TRANSMIGRATION

🜏 NINE LIVES OF TONG-NOU · EASTERN MIND, WIRED INTO WESTERN HEART

Die. Pick a face. Live again.

Rin lost his soul to the island Tong-Nou. A friend lent him a temporary soul — 49 days. The only way home is through death: die and be reincarnated 9 times, once as each of the eight creatures of Tong-Nou, and then as yourself. Death here is not a game-over. Death is the progression.

On each death, assemble the next life on the face-pick screen — one pair of eyes, one nose, one mouth, from the original D_FACE art. The face you build calls the vessel. Finish that creature's mission to earn its Nameplate. With all eight, the seal opens, the five Magatamas are gathered, they forge the Katana of Five Forces, and the soul is recovered at the Octagonal Shrine Par-fang.

WH rule, stated plainly: the original game offers two options per face part (eight faces, eight creatures). This screen offers three — the third part of each kind is Rin's own face, and it only answers once all eight creature Nameplates are held.

LIFE 1 · RIN

The first death

Rin stands in Tong-Nou's fire room. The left flame is the way out of this life — and the amulet Yashiro's friend gave him will catch him on the other side. Die, and the Rin Nameplate is yours.

D_FACE · THE FACE-PICK

Assemble the next life

Pick one eye, one nose, one mouth. The screen tells you what the face calls before you commit — a spent vessel (its Nameplate already yours) cannot be picked again, and your own face will not answer until the eight are done.

MISSION

    Dying mid-mission spends the life and undoes the mission — the vessel can be picked again, but its work starts over.

    THE LIFE IS SPENT

    Die again

    NINTH LIFE · RIN RETURNS

    The seal on Central Mountain

    Eight Nameplates, eight lives lived. Rin is himself again — the ninth life. Set the Nameplates in the seal and it opens.

    THE FIVE MAGATAMAS · ONE PER LAND

    Gather what is scattered

    Water and Wood already came home inside Byou's life. Metal waits on the Helix Palace roof, Fire by a candle in the Land of Time, Earth on Central Mountain itself. Take each one.

    FIVE MAGATAMAS · COMPLETE

    Forge the Katana of Five Forces

    The Tou-gyou emblems are consulted in the five lands in sequence — Desire, Dreaming, Life, Time — and back to Central Mountain. Then the blade is given.

    OCTAGONAL SHRINE PAR-FANG · CENTRAL MOUNTAIN

    Enter through the window

    Use the katana. Then use the five Magatamas. What Tong-Nou took, it returns — it was never a devourer. It is a purifier of souls.

    🜏 THE SOUL IS RECOVERED

    Rin is whole.

    Nine lives. Nine deaths. Eight Nameplates, five Magatamas, one katana — and a soul, purified, come home. The head-world keeps turning, and the Trial remembers every life you lived in it.

    THE EIGHT CREATURES · KNOW THE VESSELS

    Who you may become

    Lands and missions are the original's (GameFAQs walkthrough, lordgod666 v1.02; Osamu Sato Wiki). The face each vessel answers to is discovered on the face-pick screen — the screen tells you before you commit.
