# WESTERN HEART — STAGES
## The Seven Lands as Fighting Arenas

Seven stages. Five lands of Tong-Nou + the Phantom Marketplace + the Central Mountain shrine. Each stage's **geometry** is adapted from Tekken 3's stage format (flat fighting plane, bounded arena, background layers). Each stage's **visuals** are entirely Eastern Mind bitmap extractions — the land's identity rendered as a fighting arena.

**Asset rule:** No new environments. Stage geometry follows Tekken 3's arena format (extracted). All visible surfaces are Eastern Mind extractions.

---

### STAGE 1: PLAINS OF CHI-U — Land of Time (Fire / Confrontation)
- **Land:** Shi-chieng. The element is Fire. The meaning is Confrontation.
- **Visual:** Scorched plains under a burning sky. The Fire Tower of Time looms in the background — a burning pagoda that never collapses. Clock faces embedded in the ground, half-melted.
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Land of Time background bitmaps, Fire Tower sprites, clock/face motifs.
- **Tekken geometry donor:** Open plain stage format (e.g., Tekken 3's grass/plain stages) — flat, no walls, maximum movement.
- **Force claimed on win:** **Fire** — your attacks gain +chip damage for the rest of the set. Confrontation rewards aggression.
- **Music:** Eastern Mind Land of Time music extraction (Sato's violently energetic composition).

### STAGE 2: HELIX PALACE — Land of Desire (Metal / Eternity)
- **Land:** Yui-wang. The element is Metal. The meaning is Eternity.
- **Visual:** A spiraling palace interior. Five rooms visible in the background: Greed, Appetite, Immortality, Sexual Desire, Destruction — each a doorway glowing with its own sickly color. Everything is reflective, metallic, too shiny. The bedazzled-Jello aesthetic at its most intense.
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Helix Palace room bitmaps, metallic texture extractions, the five-room door sprites.
- **Tekken geometry donor:** Enclosed palace stage format (walled arena — walls enable wall-splat combos).
- **Force claimed on win:** **Metal** — your health regenerates slightly between rounds. Eternity sustains.
- **Music:** Eastern Mind Land of Desire music extraction.

### STAGE 3: PALACE OF DREAMING — Land of Dreaming (Water / Change)
- **Land:** Mon-chien. The element is Water. The meaning is Change.
- **Visual:** A palace that won't stay still. Walls ripple like water. The floor reflects a sky that isn't there. Objects morph when you're not looking at them (background animation only — the fighting plane stays stable).
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Palace of Dreaming bitmaps, water/morphing background sprites.
- **Tekken geometry donor:** Standard arena with visual distortion layers (background-only effects, no gameplay impact).
- **Force claimed on win:** **Water** — your sidestep becomes faster. Change rewards adaptation.
- **Music:** Eastern Mind Land of Dreaming music extraction.

### STAGE 4: TREE OF LIFE — Land of Life (Wood / Life)
- **Land:** Ming-ken. The element is Wood. The meaning is Life.
- **Visual:** A colossal tree dominates the background — the Tree of Life, its branches holding tiny glowing souls like fruit. The Sha-mo Desert stretches to the horizon on one side. Roots break through the fighting plane as decorative (non-interactive) geometry.
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Tree of Life bitmaps, Sha-mo Desert backgrounds, soul-fruit sprites.
- **Tekken geometry donor:** Outdoor natural stage format (flat with scenic background).
- **Force claimed on win:** **Wood** — your reach extends slightly (hitbox extension). Life reaches outward.
- **Music:** Eastern Mind Land of Life music extraction.

### STAGE 5: PHANTOM MARKETPLACE — The Trading Hub (Eyeball Mountains)
- **Land:** None — the liminal space between. Eyeball mountains watch the fight.
- **Visual:** A marketplace of impossible stalls under mountains that are giant eyeballs. The eyes follow the fighters (background animation). Stalls sell things that shouldn't exist. This is the mid-boss stage.
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Phantom Marketplace bitmaps, eyeball-mountain sprites, stall/object sprites.
- **Tekken geometry donor:** Bustling background stage format (crowd-stage energy, but the crowd is... eyes).
- **Special:** Mid-boss fight — **The Trader** (Mokujin mimicry moveset). Winning here doesn't claim a force; it lets you *trade* one claimed force for another.
- **Music:** Eastern Mind marketplace music extraction.

### STAGE 6: OCTAGONAL SHRINE PAR-FANG — Central Mountain (Earth / Source)
- **Land:** Tong-nou itself. The element is Earth. The meaning is Source.
- **Visual:** An eight-sided shrine at the peak. Stone, ancient, still. The sky above is the inside of the head — dark, vast, veined. This is where it ends.
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Shrine Par-fang bitmaps, Central Mountain backgrounds, head-interior sky textures.
- **Tekken geometry donor:** Final boss stage format (smaller arena, dramatic lighting, enclosed).
- **Force claimed on win:** **Earth** — your defense solidifies (damage reduction). Source grounds you.
- **Special:** Final boss — **The Assembled Soul** (True Ogre boss moveset). All nine styles. Yourself.
- **Music:** Eastern Mind Central Mountain music extraction — the most sacred-sounding track.

### STAGE 7 (BONUS): THE DRILL WOUND — Inside the Skull
- **Land:** None — a secret stage. In Eastern Mind, clicking the skull makes a drill burst out, leaking green blood.
- **Visual:** Inside the wound. Green blood rains. Eyeballs float past. The walls are bone and screaming faces.
- **Unlock:** Win without losing a round in arcade mode ("flawless trial").
- **Eastern Mind sources:** Drill-wound event bitmaps, eyeball sprites, green-blood effects.
- **No force claimed.** This stage is the wound. It gives nothing. It only witnesses.

## STAGE DESIGN NOTES

**Elemental order (arcade ladder):** Wood (Life) → Fire (Time) → Earth (Mountain) → Metal (Desire) → Water (Dreaming) — the generating cycle (Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal enriches Water). Then Marketplace, then Shrine.

**Why this order:** You begin in Life (where souls start), pass through Time (confrontation), ground at the Source (understanding), descend into Desire (the test), dissolve into Dreaming (change), trade at the Marketplace (choice), and ascend to the Shrine (becoming whole). The ladder is a spiritual journey, not a difficulty curve.

**Wall vs. open:** Helix Palace and the Shrine are walled (wall combos enabled). All others are open (maximum movement, ring-outs impossible — souls don't fall out of the head).
