# Bass Armstrong — Complete Movelist Reference

**Character:** Bass Armstrong (Mr. Strong in DOA5)
**Style:** Pro Wrestler | **Height:** 6'6" | **Weight:** 345 lbs
**Archetype:** Power grappler — slowest strikes in the game, highest throw damage

Compiled from DOA2 (GameFAQs/video-games-museum FAQs), DOA Dimensions (3DS), DOA5/5U/LR (Dead or Alive Wiki), and DOA6 system data. DOA5 is the primary reference; DOA4 data is substantially similar.

## 7. Tag Throws (DOA5)

Bass has special tag throws with: **Tina Armstrong, Ayane, Bayman, Jann Lee, Gen Fu, Kasumi, Zack, La Mariposa, Mila, Rig**

Notable:
- With Tina: **Docking Driver** (66F+P+K), **Lock-On Sky Twister Press** (236F+P+K)
- With Ayane: **Two Platoon DDT** (66F+P+K)
- With Zack: **Alley-Oop** (66F+P+K)
- With anyone else: **Oklahoma Stampede** (66F+P+K, F+P)
