## Teardown Notes

- **EX series (PS1):** PS1 disc images extract with standard tools. Models are low-poly but the move data and character designs feed the chimera build.
- **SFIV PC:** Clean Steam files = direct access to game data. No console decryption needed. Best teardown target.
- **PS3 ISOs:** Need RPCS3 or PS3 decryption tools. Larger effort.
- **Rashid** is SFV-only (not in any of these games). His models are already pulled from DeviantArt/SFMLab.
