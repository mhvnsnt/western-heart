# EM-07 — take on Guile (The Airbase)

## Archetype study
- Sonic Boom (charge b~f+2) — the fastest projectile in the archetype; zoning core
- Flash Kick (charge d~u+4) — invincible anti-air
- Upside-down Kick (f+4 / df+3+4), Sobat (f+3) — long-range pokes
- Playstyle: the wall — holds ground with pokes + Sonic Boom, Flash Kick is the threat
- Translation: Guile fits Tekken as a keepout turtle: `df+3` long pokes, Sonic Boom chip
  with Tekken-style sidestep counterplay.
