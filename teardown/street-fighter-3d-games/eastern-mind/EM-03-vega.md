# EM-03 — take on Vega (The Spanish Claw)

## Archetype study
- Rolling Crystal Flash (charge b~f+1) — rolling ground approach into claw slash
- Flying Barcelona Attack (charge d~u+2 then claw/barcelona — wall dive) — the iconic cage dive
- Sky High Claw (charge d~u+1+2) — long-range claw dive
- Izuna Drop (flying throw follow-up) — airborne command grab
- Translation: Barcelona dive maps to a Tekken-style `f,F+2` jump-in with follow-up branch
  (`+1` = claw strike, `+1+3` = Izuna throw); claw reach = long poke range on `1`.
