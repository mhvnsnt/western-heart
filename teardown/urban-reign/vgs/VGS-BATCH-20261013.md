# Urban Reign VGS batch decode — 2026-10-13

All 48 VGS files from DVD000.AFS range-fetched from the `urban-reign-iso-v1`
release (no full ISO download; 14.5MB total), parsed with
`tools/vgs_decode.py`, batch-driven by `tools/vgs_batch_decode.py`.

## Results

- **48/48 files parsed** (magic 03 08 04 00 verified, header/column/display-list).
- **38 files** yielded texture blobs → 8-bit grayscale PNGs in `decoded/`
  (rendered at 320px wide, even-field deinterlaced — the baseline that made
  title.vgs recognizable in the original crack).
- **10 files** are display-list-only (752-byte files: 2x4 grids, no texture
  blob) — recorded as `no_blob` in VGS-INVENTORY.csv. These are frame/UI
  chrome definitions, not texture carriers.
- VGS-INVENTORY.csv: per-file size, grid, column count, sprite count,
  texture-blob offset/length, status.

## Honest state of the renders (read before judging the PNGs)

The PNGs are **baseline 8-bit grayscale dumps, not final art**. The exact
CLUT/color pipeline is still open — most files render as interlaced-looking
grayscale structure, not recognizable color art. What the display lists DO
give us for the next refinement: exact per-sprite (w,h) and texture
EE-RAM addresses (e.g. title.vgs col0 has a 320x224 backdrop sprite plus
16x16 / 48x24 / 64x32 furniture pieces). Per-sprite carving keyed off the
display-list dims is the obvious next step, plus the CLUT hunt.
